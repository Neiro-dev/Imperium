## Statements

### text

- Narration borrows the attitude RimWorld and its DLCs take toward comparable things: who is speaking, why the thing exists, what facts the player needs. It uses no "so many % vanilla, so many % Empire" ratio and does not require a bureaucratic word in every sentence. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L6-8
- Content decides tone. The Empire is shown by objects, actions, reliable settlement and the consequences of authority, not by praise. The player can operate while ignoring politics. Unknown history, behind-the-scenes motives and guesses about people are not written as omniscient fact. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L10
- Three narrators are distinguished. System/narrator/UI (系统/旁白/UI) states currently known facts, actions and results, plainly and with restraint, not as official documents. World characters (世界人物) speak of occupation, situation, interests and personality; position, personality and political stance do not map one to one; in a letter a character may have a short personal tone that changes no result (01 §4.3). Formal institutions (正式机构) speak of authority, procedure, approval/refusal, delivery and settlement: result first, then necessary reason and next step, with moderate gravity. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L12-16
- Item/machine/resource text keeps construction, use, inputs and real limits, with a short helpful origin. Machine descriptions follow the way vanilla describes machines such as the Militor, in Imperium's own design language. It avoids "the Empire is necessarily strongest", invented maintenance or combat ability, and any tactics tutorial, formation advice or role statement (the player judges the use). `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L22 `BASEGAME` (for the vanilla Militor description style)
- Building text keeps function, real working conditions, what it operates on and necessary limits. It avoids presenting a dead ruin as at once repairable/usable and avoids invented interactions. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L23
- Research/Techprint text keeps what is actually opened and the difference between authorisation, research and manufacture. It avoids "authorised means built", hidden added research waiting, and "ancient means advanced". `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L24
- An Orders offer text keeps the target, the frozen reward, the deadline and a separate acceptance state. It avoids risk rating, recommended gear, enemy strength, internal probability or hidden tendencies. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L25
- An accepted Quest text keeps the current physical objective, legal actions, deadline and delivery conditions. It avoids repeating the full offer, mechanical tutorials and court-intrigue explanation. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L26
- Claim/ruin text keeps limited records, the currently known target, the real recovery and its consequences. It avoids omniscient archaeology records and treating every excavation as a political test. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L27
- Event/letter text states what is happening or has happened and the necessary response. It avoids announcing arrival of people, reward payment or enemy clearance ahead of time. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L28
- Dialogue text gives clear option consequences and character difference; the player must not need to read political hints to choose correctly. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L29
- Formal notices give the outcome, necessary reason and next step; they avoid meaningless regulation, flattery or deliberate contempt. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L30
- Edicts (敕令), great campaigns (大战役) and rebuilding (重建) are rare and solemn, and state real duties and results; wording does not stand in for armies, logistics or engineering. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L31
- Description pattern for machines, and likewise similar items and buildings, taken from the common way of all vanilla machine descriptions: (1) the first sentence classes weight category plus type plus design intent, in the vanilla Chinese pattern "一种轻型……机械，设计用于……", names key equipment, gives no feature list; (2) one restrained sentence on strength and weakness, without numbers or rules; (3) a second paragraph only for a few machines (about a quarter in vanilla), written as narrative of the name's origin, the historical situation or what people experienced, from the viewpoint of people in the Imperial world, in a cold tone, no witty maxims, no praise; utility machines usually get one or two sentences; Imperial flavour comes from these people and institutions, not from repeating the word "Empire"; (4) no tactics, formation advice or operation tutorial. `Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L33-37 `BASEGAME` (for the vanilla description facts) [用户裁定 2026-10-26]
