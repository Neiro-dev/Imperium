## Statements

### imperium

- After the colony takes the core from the starting ruins, the Empire's envoy comes, and the player decides whether the colony joins the Empire or stays outside it, and how far it takes part. `docs/imperium.md` L8-L14, L22-L23
- A colony that stays outside the Empire can still play to an end of the game; after refusing it can excavate the old empire's ruins and reach an end. `docs/imperium.md` L13-L14, L24-L25, L93-L94
- Every part of Imperium plays out through the colony's relation to the Empire, and the join-or-refuse choice starts it. `docs/imperium.md` L16-L17
- The Empire's people and business come to the colony whether or not the colony is ready, and the Empire answers what the colony has done to it later, sometimes long after. The player meets an empire far older and stronger than the colony, one that does not wait on it. `docs/imperium.md` L29-L33, L40-L42, L99-L100
- An attack on an imperial caravan is answered by the Empire later, while imperial visits keep coming even if the colony asks the Empire for nothing. `docs/imperium.md` L35-L36, L40-L42
- The end of the game decides whether the Empire keeps or loses this planet, not the fate of the Empire as a whole; the colony is one of many places the Empire governs and its end stays that size. Neither end describes anything beyond this planet. `docs/imperium.md` L46-L55
- The Empire content falls into three parts along the Empire's time: the past, the present and the future. New Empire content belongs to one of the three; content that fits none is not added. `docs/imperium.md` L75-L76
- The past is the ruins, which the colony enters to excavate technology lost since the silence. `docs/imperium.md` L77-L78
- The present is the Empire of today and its effect on the colony: a colony that joins serves it and receives its commendations; a colony that stays outside bears its ill will and pressure. `docs/imperium.md` L79-L82
- The future is the end of the game, which decides the fate of the Empire on this planet. `docs/imperium.md` L83
- The end is one of two: the Empire, helped by the colony, takes the planet back and the colony rises to a high standing with it; or the colony ends the Empire's rule here, after which no one takes its place and the planet sinks deeper into disorder. `docs/imperium.md` L84-L86
- The parts are not lines of play: a colony that joins the Empire and one that stays outside both go through every part. `docs/imperium.md` L88-L89
- Mantle generates the world the parts take place in and runs whether Empire content is on or off. `docs/imperium.md` L91-L92
- Techprints run across the past and the present (detail in the ruins document). `docs/imperium.md` L95

### empire

- The Empire shows itself to the player through its people, events and notices; the player never sees a number for the colony's standing with the Empire, and the relation works underneath (as Ill will describes). `docs/imperium.md` L59-L64, L101, L103
- No number for the colony's standing with the Empire appears on any screen. `docs/imperium.md` L68-L69, L103
- A colony in the Empire is never asked to manage or understand the Empire's inner politics. `docs/imperium.md` L59-L60, L70-L71, L104
- Text shows the Empire through what it does rather than telling players what it is. `docs/imperium.md` L102
- The Empire's elements come from Rome as reliable sources describe it, not from popular images of Rome such as its engineering and its military. `docs/setting.md` L10-L11, L60-L61
- The Empire is not a reproduction of Rome. `docs/setting.md` L13, L95
- The Empire does not call itself Rome. `docs/setting.md` L10-L11, L59
- The form of a language does not set the Empire apart from Rome. `docs/setting.md` L58
- Each of the Empire's people is either a combatant or a non-combatant. `docs/setting.md` L75-L76
- The Empire is a bureaucratic empire and a state of people; it keeps running whoever is missing from it, the emperor included. `docs/setting.md` L96-L97
- The Empire governs its territory as administrative regions. It does not station a standing force in every region; how each region is patrolled, defended and garrisoned is decided for that region. `docs/setting.md` L98-L99
- The Empire's present court is not on this planet. `docs/setting.md` L42, L100
- The Empire's people are the people of the Empire, who fill all of its roles. `docs/setting.md` L101
- The Empire's mechanoids are the Empire's machines, which generally have plain strengths and weaknesses. `docs/setting.md` L102-L103
- The security of important persons takes an elite approach: the honor guard. `docs/setting.md` L77-L78

### world

- Rome is treated as one: nothing the player reads splits Rome into separate states or peoples, and text uses no name that sets part of Rome apart as another state or people. `docs/setting.md` L10-L11, L20, L62, L93
- Rome is the Roman state and civilization from which the Empire takes its elements. `docs/setting.md` L93
- The ruins lean toward Old Rome and the present Empire toward New Rome. `docs/setting.md` L21-L22, L57; `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L62 [HISTORY]
- The player learns that the Empire's court is not on this planet and that this planet is one of its outermost regions, governed by a bureaucracy; the colony is at the edge of a large state. `docs/setting.md` L42-L48
- This planet is a frontier world to the Empire, one of its outermost regions. `docs/setting.md` L52, L104
- The regions are background that helps the player understand the Empire; the player never manages them and they are not a game system. `docs/setting.md` L43-L44, L47-L48, L53, L79-L80
- The base game's Empire does not exist in this world. `docs/setting.md` L83
- How the Empire came through the silence stays unexplained. `docs/setting.md` L84
- The Empire's look meets the constraints in the section Appearance of #63; the rest of it is the artist's. `docs/setting.md` L88-L89 [LOOK]
- The ruins are what the old empire left before the silence. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L93

### military

- No person of the Empire uses a gun; the player fighting imperial forces of every kind meets no gun, no hand-held crossbow and no sling. `docs/setting.md` L26-L27, L34-L36, L66
- None of the Empire's weapons, artillery included, is fired by gunpowder or drives a projectile electromagnetically. `docs/setting.md` L26-L28, L37-L39, L67
- The Empire can still field artillery, bolt throwers among it. `docs/setting.md` L28
- Artillery does not count as a gun, and a bolt thrower counts as artillery, not a crossbow. `docs/setting.md` L69
- The hand-held crossbow is not a weapon of the Empire, and slings are not used. `docs/setting.md` L71
- Other ranged technology is open for the Empire's weapons. `docs/setting.md` L68
- The mechanoids' built-in weapons are set in the section Weapons of #63. `docs/setting.md` L69-L70

### relations

- The colony's relation to the Empire is join (serve, receive commendations) or stay outside (bear ill will and pressure). `docs/imperium.md` L79-L82
- A colony in the Empire reaches the old empire's sites through claims; a colony outside reaches them by its own excavations. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L73
- A colony in the Empire cannot excavate on its own. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L37, L81
- Membership trades what the colony finds for what the Empire grants: a colony in the Empire gets its core technology from the Empire's grants, hands over the old empire's technology it finds, and receives present technology in exchange. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L26-L29, L38-L40, L80, L82-L83
- A colony in the Empire cannot keep found technology by registering it. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L32-L34, L84

### ruins-tech

- A colony outside the Empire takes on excavations of the old empire's ruins, finds techprints that carry the old empire's technology, and analyzes it with the analysis device; this is a way to technology that does not pass through the Empire. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L10-L13, L88-L89
- The first excavation the player takes on, not counting the starting ruins, unlocks making the analysis device. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L20-L21, L75-L76
- A colony with the analysis device can analyze excavated technology of the old empire with it. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L22-L23
- Excavations, claims among them, follow the base game's quest form. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L71 [BASEGAME]
- A colony in the Empire and a colony outside it reach the same sites. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L74
- The ruins take the base game's forms for places on the world map: ruins that quests lead to and, where Odyssey is active, ruins that stay where they are. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L60-L61 [BASEGAME]
- Where Odyssey is active the player also finds ruins of the old empire that stay where they are on the world map, yielding techprints that come only with Odyssey; without Odyssey only the ruins that quests lead to appear. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L43-L45, L54-L56
- Ruins that stay give the colony something the ruins of quests cannot, suited to what players take up Odyssey for: techprints that come only with Odyssey. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L46-L47, L65-L66
- The Empire yields techprints, and so do the ruins; both forms of ruins can yield techprints. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L63-L64
- Techprints are a layer of technology across the past and the present; those of the ruins carry technology of the old empire. `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L94-L95

### playstyles

- Imperium has playstyles for its Empire content, as Anomaly has for its own. `docs/playstyles.md` L51 [BASEGAME]
- In the standard playstyle the game has the starting ruins from the start, so the Empire content is there from the start; the quest to the starting ruins appears. `docs/playstyles.md` L9-L13, L17-L18, L55
- In distant empire the starting ruins do not appear and Empire content comes less often than in standard, as Anomaly's threats do in ambient horror. `docs/playstyles.md` L21-L26, L30-L33, L59-L60 [BASEGAME]
- In custom difficulty the player sets the share of Empire content and Empire content follows that share, as Anomaly's threat sliders do for its threats. `docs/playstyles.md` L36-L47, L64-L65 [BASEGAME]

### expansions
