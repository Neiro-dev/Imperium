### cities

Roman settlements, general (SPEC; items the file itself marks replaced carry flags)

- The Empire's Roman settlements have one single Roman kit; the camp tiers (City, Castellum, Fortlet, Burgus) are tiers of that one kit. Form and skin are separate: form comes from terrain and the city plan, skin from the biome region; any landform can be combined with any climate. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md` L119 [R-1029-1323, R-1029-0710b]
- The skin is by region, dry-hot (干热区) or temperate-cold (温寒区); at this stage the two versions share the same colours and materials and differ only in layout. The skin only decides stone colour, plants, field kind, garden and cemetery placement. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md` L126; L434 [R-1029-1358, R-1027-1904a]
- Form has two rows and one city site takes one of them by terrain: a "no-highland" row (axis plus grid; the upper city is a landmark zone on an artificial terrace) and a "highland" row (the upper city is the acropolis (卫城, with its own inner wall) at the highest point, and the old main street runs from the main gate along the terrain to the acropolis gate). Both rows can add a waterside (water gate and waterside wall). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md` L121-124 [R-1029-0710b]
- Roman settlements draw on both peaks of Rome ("two peaks"): the default and first example take the new (second) peak, naming Constantinople, Thessaloniki and Smyrna; the old (first) peak contributes regular grid, colonnades, squares, aqueducts and baths; copying the old peak's small-block insula wholesale for a whole city is left to ruins. HISTORY (the concept of two peaks of Rome). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md` L128 [R-1028-1130, R-1028-1145, R-1029-1504]
- Artistic expression has priority over realism: things that exist in reality but look cluttered (for example dead-end streets) are not made. Rome is the "planned" type (draw first, then build, straight lines, held up by water); the medieval is the "grown" type. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md` L128 [R-1029-1818]
