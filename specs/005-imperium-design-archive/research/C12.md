## Statements

### cities

Walls, towers and gates

- Neighbouring wall towers stand between 24 and 60 cells apart (the kit's values). Within that range, a stretch whose tower distance differs from its face's trial value by more than 30% is only noted. The trial values by face are: facing an approach road 26, open flat ground 32, rock-backed 45, water-backed 55. All these numbers are trial values. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L55, L59 [R-1103-1338⑤②]
- A land gate has a gate group (门组) of two towers, one on each side of the gate opening. The two count as one node: the stretch between them is not judged for tower spacing, and the opening between them is not counted in the tower-spacing statistics. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L59, L62; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/measure.md` L12 [R-1103-1338⑤②; R-1030-2133]
- Each gate-group tower is at least 24 cells (the lower spacing limit) from the neighbouring tower on its outer side. A gate stands on a curtain wall (幕墙); only water gates (水门) are exempt, and a water gate is not a gate group. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L62 [R-1103-1338⑤②]
- Wherever the curtain wall turns (by 1 degree or more) there is a tower. A corner tower is a large tower of 9 cells; a smaller corner tower is only noted. Two neighbouring towers that both turn the same way within the lower spacing limit are noted as "not forming a string". `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L63 [R-1103-1338⑤②]
- Every turn of the wall has a stated reason, taken from: rock (岩), water (水), marsh (池沼), gate (门), approach road (来路), block edge (街区边), large piece (大件). The thing named must lie within 20 cells of the tower (trial value). A large piece is a composite piece or any piece covering 300 cells or more (trial value). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L64-68 [R-1103-1338⑤②]
- A concave corner (a turn toward the inside of the city, opposite to the turn of the whole ring) may be explained only by rock, water, marsh or a large piece, never by a gate, an approach road or a single block edge. The towers on both sides of a concave corner exist and are no more than 60 cells apart. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L69 [R-1103-1338⑤②]
- A convex corner of the wall on the land side has an obtuse interior angle; a right or acute interior angle (90 degrees or less) is an error. A corner is exempt when one of its sides lies against water (within the 5-cell bank belt) or against rock (within 12 cells), because such a corner is attacked from one side only. Whether a side counts as against water or rock is judged by distance, not by whether a gate opens on it; a gate on a water-facing side is a bridge gate or water gate and does not change that. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L82 [R-1103-2238]
- Wall segments are written in 16 directions in the generator and the wall check; the check also reads the whole wall ring through an entry that stops on any segment not running in one of eight directions (horizontal, vertical, 45 degrees). See Conflicts. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/gen.md` L27; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/代码地图.md` L187-190 [R-1110-1412a]
- Wall stretches come in three kinds: land wall (陆墙, the outside is land), water-facing wall (临水墙, the outside touches sea, river or lake) and rock-as-wall (岩当墙, a stretch where a rock body serves as the wall and the wall line is written at the rock edge). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/复刻城格式.md` L55
- Towers are of four kinds: corner (转角), beside a gate (门旁), mid-segment (段中), other. Gates are of five kinds: main gate (主门), side gate (侧门), wicket gate (便门), water gate (水门), palace gate (宫门). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/复刻城格式.md` L34-37
- A wall stretch that borrows a river bank, water, bank or sea coast keeps the wall cells at the bank belt of 5 cells from the water; a narrower or wider gap is only noted, and a wider gap is noted as "may be relaxed at a bend of the bank". A stretch that borrows "river" is a wall crossing the river with water gates and is not counted as along the water. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L71 [R-1103-0143③, R-1103-1338⑤]

Rock as wall

- A rock body is not built over: a curtain wall whose 3-cell body presses 3 cells onto rock is an error; where rock stands in the line, the rock serves as wall and the wall stops at the rock edge. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L77 [R-1030-1550, R-1029-2258]
- A stretch marked rock-as-wall must really lean on rock: at least half of its 3-cell wall body is on rock or within 2 cells of rock (trial value), otherwise it is an error. The stretch still joins the wall ring and keeps the towers at both ends; the stretch is not judged as pressing rock. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L57, L78 [R-1030-1550, R-1029-2258]
- The wall's rock-as-wall word is the same word and meaning as the `seg_kind` "岩当墙" of the replica city format. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L57; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/复刻城格式.md` L55

River, banks and water gates

- If the wall touches the river, it touches it at exactly two places, one where the river enters the city and one where it leaves; each has one water gate, and each water gate has two towers (gate-side towers). A city on one bank only is not checked. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L79 [R-1030-2201①, R-1103-0126a, R-1103-1516, R-1029-0935a per the code map]
- When a river divides the ground, the bank holding the first plaza (第一广场) is the core bank. If the opposite bank holds city pieces (a bridgehead group does not count), it is a district: the wall body does not cross the river there, no wall body lies on the opposite bank, and entry and exit each get one water gate. If the opposite bank is not a district, no wall body and no tower stands on it, and no bridgehead fortress (桥头堡) is set (not set since R-1105-1758); a gate group or bridgehead group placed on the opposite bank, over half its bridge body on the river excepted, is an error. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L104 [R-1030-2201②, R-1105-1659, R-1105-1758]
- Each approach road (来路) of the world gets one land gate (main or side gate), matched by the bearing seen from the city centre; a road with no gate is an error and an extra land gate is only noted (a road to fields has a wicket gate). Where two or more land gates match roads of the same rank in the A-type city, the main gate is the one farther from the palace district; roads of different rank are only noted because there is no rank vocabulary. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L80 [R-1030-1550⑥, R-1103-0718④ per the code map]
- A gate's passage runs horizontally or vertically (the axis nearest to the outward wall normal). The street at the gate does not meet it diagonally, along the wall, or off the gate centre by more than half the passage width; passage width is 5 for the main gate and 3 for the others (trial values). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L81; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/draft.md` L37 [R-1030-2046①, R-1102-2315 per the code map]

Streets, the core and what the city must hold

- The first plaza is placed away from the river: it does not stand on river water or across both banks, and it is at least the quay belt (码头带, 5 cells) plus the shortest bridgehead street (桥头街, 5 cells, trial value) from the river. When the opposite bank is a district there is a recognisable main bridge, and its centre line falls within the first plaza's cross-wise extent. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L105 [R-1103-0039a, R-1103-0739④, R-1103-1944⑦, R-1030-2201③ per the code map]
- The first plaza is centred in the city: the distances from its centre to the city edge in north, east, south and west are measured; an edge with water within bank belt plus 3 cells (a water-facing hard edge) or rock within 12 cells (a rock-facing hard edge) is left out; among the open (land-facing) edges, smallest divided by largest must be at least 0.6 (trial value), and fewer than two open edges are not judged. The aim, from R-1110-1207, is that the city grows from the middle as one mass with rock and water only as the edge behind it. The palace district (卫城) is not measured, and the check applies to a whole city only. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L107 [R-1110-1207]

Templates, sample and site shaping

- A template (版型, `imperium-template-1`, R-1111-1855) is classed by city-site type (城址型), tier (档, which sets the settlement map size) and plot condition; the coverage table counts for each combination how many are frozen and how many kept.
