Walls (城墙, 墙)

- The wall's slope set was 16 directions: horizontal, vertical, 45°, plus 2:1 and 1:2; every defence front may use all of them, and a front takes the direction nearest to the line it follows (river bank, rock edge, approach road, planning axis). - The wall is a tower chain in the manner of Nova Roma: the city sits inside, not on, the grid. A defence front fixes which sides of the city are defended, what each faces (approach road, river, rock) and where the gates are. The guide line follows visible things: river bank, rock edge, edge of swamp or soft ground, ponds, and the city's own extent computed by block (blocks plus garden land and orchards inside the wall).
