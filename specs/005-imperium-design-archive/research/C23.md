### core

- A quest that does not expire leads the player, early in the game, to ruins near the colony; the ruins are a decayed town, different in each game, and the colony takes the core from them. `docs/core.md` L8-L16
- When the colony is in the starting ruins, the player sees a decayed town, not the old empire. `docs/core.md` L21-L23
- Once the colony has taken the core, the starting ruins disappear. `docs/core.md` L24-L25
- The core is an early goal close to the colony and sets off the colony's first meeting with the Empire. `docs/core.md` L13-L13
- The starting ruins are generated by Mantle on the model of a small or middle-sized town of the old empire. `docs/core.md` L59-L63
- The starting ruins hold the core and disappear when it is taken. `docs/core.md` L63-L63
- The core is put to the use that the colony's relation to the Empire calls for: the imperial insignia (inside the Empire), the analysis device (outside it), or an ultraheavy mechanoid where Biotech is active; the colony can also sell the core. `docs/core.md` L25-L30
- The imperial insignia, which the colony makes when it joins the Empire, uses the core and reads techprints. `docs/core.md` L36-L37, L67-L68
- The analysis device, which a colony outside the Empire makes (when excavations allow, `docs/ruins-and-technology.md` "Excavations"), uses the core and analyzes the old empire's technology more slowly than the imperial insignia does. `docs/core.md` L38-L40, L69-L71
- Where Biotech is active the core is used in an ultraheavy mechanoid. `docs/core.md` L72-L72
- The colony can sell the core to other factions. `docs/core.md` L40-L40, L73-L73
- The core cannot be destroyed. `docs/core.md` L77-L77
- When the building that holds the core is destroyed the core drops; when the colony deconstructs that building the core is returned, so the core can move between the imperial insignia, the analysis device and an ultraheavy mechanoid. `docs/core.md` L42-L55, L78-L80
