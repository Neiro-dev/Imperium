### world

- Rome references are taken only from Roman history outside Italy (eastern provinces, Gaul, North Africa, Iberia); Italian cities such as Pompeii and Ostia are not references for cities and buildings; Britannia and Germania are too remote; Italian local special cases and textbook army camps are not imperial rules. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/审查清单-条文.md` L53-54 HISTORY
- A feature enters a rule only if it is shared by large cities outside Italy (Constantinople, Antioch, Alexandria, Ephesus, Thessalonica); forms seen only in small towns, army camps and colonies are not made rules. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/审查清单-条文.md` L55 HISTORY
