### world

- "Rome" means one continuous state and civilisation from before the common era to 1453; the card's wording is Rome, ancient Rome, New Rome (新罗马) or a specific city and date. The word "Byzantine" is not written (a cataloguing word used only when searching), and "Eastern Rome" is not made a separate class. Ancient Rome and New Rome differ only in skin and content (see 《罗马城-做法与顺序》一). Defaults and first examples take New Rome; where New Rome's evidence is thin, ancient Rome is not used to fill it and no numbers are invented. Fire is one of New Rome's working-fluid expressions and liquid fire is kept. The two types of Roman city are called the dry-hot zone and the temperate-cold zone; "巴西尔" and "查士丁尼" appear only as people's names in historical examples. `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L99-105 HISTORY [R-1028-2325, R-1030-2152, R-1029-1504, R-1029-1358, 设计会话定]
- Roman references take New Rome only; what is unique to ancient Rome is not a reference. Imperial mechanoids all take New Roman forms, not divided by Rank into eras, ancient Roman forms being only for ruins. The base reference set is the eastern provinces outside Italy, Gaul, North Africa and Iberia; Italian cities such as Pompeii and Ostia are ruin content and not city or building references; Britannia and Germania are too remote to use.
