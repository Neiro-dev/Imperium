## Statements

### mantle

- Imperium uses its own generator for its own settlements and in the end does not depend on the CBF/MO research architecture to run. The quality target is at least the complete building groups, blocks, courtyards and terrain harmony of CBF-class settlements; this is a target, not a claim that it is met. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L8
- The base strategies are only CompactTown and OrganicVillage; they are tendencies toward concentration or spread, not a full city/village ecology simulation. Use-driven generation for city, village, fortress, market and mine is a named direction of discussion but does not automatically approve five new generators, buildings or quests. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L10
- Real-world relations of production, housing, transport, defence and land use are used to explain why space is organised as it is; CBF is for learning methods; community images are visual reference only; the design does not mean simulating centuries of economic history or unlimited resident AI. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L12
- Terrain first, buildings after (from 2026-10-27 23:50): on non-player maps the mod generates, the terrain template fixes the terrain during the base-game map generation and counts as natural landform; the building stage reads the template's annotations (urban area, anchoring segments, holding points, corridors, bridge sites, field zones) and no longer infers positions from random terrain. Player colonies, contact ruins on the start map, underground layers and paths with the template off still infer from the actual terrain. Whatever the path, the building stage checks against the final actual terrain and does not assume the template intent was fully realised. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L16
- Natural landform is completed first and then read (real rivers, rock bodies, caves, vegetation, natural passages, continuous buildable space); an ungenerated empty map is not treated as plain, later base-game steps' changes are included in the final check, and no height/slope data is invented. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L18
- The default tendency comes from the terrain template (real terrain when the template is off), the style rules and functional relations; explicit configuration still applies. Concentration is not symmetry, dispersal is not deliberately spreading rooms apart, and a settlement is not artificially broken up when the input has no significant obstacle. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L20
- Building candidates, real entrances, shared space, roads and necessary local terrain changes constrain one another and may be coordinated iteratively at planning. Approach road, main street and gate direction are fixed first by template and style rules (Justinian grid and main street, Basil's old main street, Modern lays the road net first); inside a block, side roads, lanes and buildings constrain each other, and buildings are not scattered first and then refused needed road connections. Local rules and straight roads may arise naturally; that does not forbid all local regularity. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L22 [names Justinian/Basil replaced, see terms]
- River, rock, coast and urban flat ground are fixed at template stage only; the building stage makes only structure-type terrain (moat, channel, pool, bridge, clearing belt, paving) and a few fallbacks (replace the bearing ground, seal rock cracks), which are counted. Legal local terrain changes for real buildings, roads, courtyards, entrances and defence are allowed, with no separate engineering cost, extra resource budget or player construction action. Large-scale unconditional mountain-cutting or river-filling is not allowed. A map with a river may concentrate on one bank without forced crossing or bridging. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L24
- Buildings are based on complete usable pieces, real doors, interior facilities and real demand; functional cores bind to concrete buildings, not abstract centre points; shared space matches real entrances and used cells of the final map. A small public open space is not automatically called a large courtyard, plaza or mature block. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L28
- Road, entrance forecourt, courtyard and building footprint are different space duties; a passage that needs protection is not covered by later buildings; a courtyard does not become free building land just because it has no entity; names such as Ward/District/Block/Cluster do not prove a same-named complete urban-building capability. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L30
- The generation flow is: base-game generation (with the template switch on and the map in range, the terrain step is set by the terrain template) → necessary natural terrain → generationId and terrain snapshot → Imperium construction transaction → preliminary planning and structure check → legal local adjustment, layout coordination and final legality check → entity commit → automatic check of the real buildings, roads, entrances and reserved space. `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L36-L45
- When the template contract does not hold, another template is tried inside map generation; if that fails the map falls back to base-game terrain and the old path, with a record.
