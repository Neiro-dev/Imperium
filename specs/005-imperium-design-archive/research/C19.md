### imperium

- The Empire (the thing Imperium's player joins) is described as a machine that links orders, law, roads, documents and army into a network; its legitimacy comes from order running, not from any person; it absorbs outsiders but ranks them by status; it works through intermediaries while guarding against them; it outlasts any individual and the emperor is only one post in it. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L42-48 [R-1027 session, 用户 10-27 20:59]
- For a frontier colony the Empire is not a person one can befriend: it is a huge, rule-keeping system that does not care who you are, only whether your node runs; it gives order and strength beyond what the colony could build, and the price is becoming part of it. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L50
- The user's point: the Empire is a machine that keeps running without anyone, the emperor included. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L6 [用户 10-27 20:59]
- Imperium's own setting: the old core "Sophia" has lost contact, a remote branch sets up a new court on the existing military, political and industrial system and continues its legal line, technology and institutions; the machine lost its centre and still runs. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L40-41 (cites 01§4.1)

### cities
