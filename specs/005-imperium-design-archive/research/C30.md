### imperium

- Imperium is positioned as an expansion-pack-like mod (the report's table cell reads "01 定位资料片"); the positioning is cited from the main-axis document 01 §2. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md` L62 [cited, 01§2]
- A project-level risk the report names as already in 01 §2: the official game may release a faction DLC (the report writes "官方出派系 DLC 见 01§2"). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md` L75 [cited, 01§2]
- Design principles cited from 01 §6: the foundation is complex while the outside is simple ("底层复杂、外表简单"); new objects must pass the "three questions for a new object" (新物件三问, R-1030-0025b), which judge whether an object is worth the effort; a content-quantity benchmark exists. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md` L68 L71 [cited, 01§6, R-1030-0025b]
- Vanilla mechanisms are used as far as possible (reuse), with the three questions as the check. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md` L123 [cited, 01§6]
- Optional DLCs and third-party compatibility are kept as optional (01 §6, 10-29 20:28). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md` L72 [cited, 01§6]
- Player enjoyment comes first (玩家乐趣优先); when something goes wrong, it is fixed in the architecture (R-1029-1316, R-1104-0958c). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md` L133 [cited, 01§6, R-1029-1316, R-1104-0958c]

- The "unattributable" and "diffuse" principles (不可归因, 弥散) mean the player is not given a contribution number. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md` L129 [cited, 01§2]
- Imperium allows other mods to extend it: the report says "对外开放扩展已定允许", with ICF layer L1 maintained as a public interface (ICF 七). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-模组工程.md` L139 [cited, ICF 七]

### cities

- Reference studies take the idea of why a wall runs as it does (参照取理念：墙为什么这样走). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md` L144 [cited, R-1103-2236①]
- The Anno 117 wall research was done against the card 罗马城-墙 (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md`, exists) and the community-reference card on city-building expressiveness; the report lists five points where Anno 117 disagrees with the card and states the card was not changed: 〔墙·四·塔距〕 (the player's city places no towers; Anno's not taken), 〔墙·二·塔间直墙〕 (curved wall; not taken), 〔墙·一·一道墙〕 (two walls were seen; "有过"), 〔墙·二·墙的构造〕 clear belt against fields placed outside the wall (card not changed), 〔墙·三·岩与屏障〕 (building a wall on rock not taken; only the filling of a gap at a rock edge). `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-纪元117城墙.md` L26 [report; contents of the card are only as the report quotes them]
- The report's own recommendation (not design): read the wall as the outer edge of the city, hugging the city, both ends closing on water or rock; a clear belt outside the wall with different ground; gatehouse the largest piece on the wall with the approach road aimed at the gate opening; at a harbour the shore, dock belt and wall run parallel with the dock belt outside the wall. `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-纪元117城墙.md` L24 [report proposal, not a ruling]
