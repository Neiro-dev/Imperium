- The mod's terrain acts at the base game's landform step, behind a new switch; with the switch off the map is identical to vanilla cell for cell. `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/地形与选址.md` L24 [R-1028-0020]
- The landform stage fixes the river course, rock bodies, coast, the load-bearing ground of the city area, the gate-road corridors, bridge sites and exclusion zones.
