# Inside the city

This section covers what lies inside a Roman city's walls: the core and its squares, the main street, the secondary axis
and the old road, the street grid, colonnades, kerbs and road surfaces, the palace quarter, the rings, the attraction
field and the households, the composite pieces and buildings, and the materials of streets, squares and buildings. It
meets `cities.md` (what a city is, its eras, the order in which it is made and how it grows, including the old wall line
of an expanded city), `cities-siting.md` (terrain and where a city stands), `cities-walls.md` (walls, towers, gates and
the gate group), `cities-outside.md` (rivers, bridges, quays, the harbour and the water that runs through the city) and
`cities-forms.md` (templates, kits, spec cards and their families, and the city grammar, including the traced reference
city of template No. 1).

## The core and the squares

### First square and civic core

The city grows from its core, and the core is the first square (第一广场), not the acropolis or the palace quarter (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L84; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·做法与顺序.md` L39, L160).

The main core (主核心), the first square with the civic core (公共核心) around it, sits where the arrival road crosses the
river, squarely facing the main bridge, and stands back from the river by the quay belt (码头带) and a bridgehead street
(桥头街) rather than hugging the bank, so that a straight street runs from the main bridge into the first square and the
centre reads as grown out of the ford, not as a quay (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L14-14, R-1103-0039a,
R-1103-0739④, R-1103-1944⑦; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L765,
L766). The first square must stand from the river at least the quay belt (码头带, 5) plus the shortest bridgehead street
(桥头街最短, 5, a trial value); where the far bank is a district of the city, the main bridge must be recognisable and its
centre line must fall within the first square's width, and city check I10 reports an error otherwise
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L105). The first square also stands in the middle of the city: of
the edges that face open land, the shortest distance from its centre to the city edge is at least 0.6 of the longest (a
trial value; a smaller ratio is an error), counted only over the directions facing land, with fewer than two such
directions not judged, while edges facing water or rock are left out, so that the city grows from the middle as one mass
with rock and water only as the edge behind it (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L107, R-1110-1207).

The first-square group has square paving, a fountain, a hall on one side and colonnaded shops on the others, with staggered street mouths, and stands where the main street (主街) meets the secondary axis (次轴) (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L750, R-1103-0016).
A square is ringed by public buildings and shop arcades; houses stand back in the second ring with their doors on the back street and do not surround the square (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L15-15; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L30-30, R-1030-1550).
A colonnaded square is about 3:2 in length to width, with its monuments toward the edge and its centre left empty (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L15-15; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L47-47).
The main street and the secondary axis enter a square facing its main building or monument, and the other small street mouths are staggered like a windmill, not set straight across, so that the square is not cut into a crossroads and cannot be seen through from inside (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L15-15, R-1103-1338⑥, R-1103-1944⑪; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L47-47).
Along the main street a square stands every so often, each with a monument (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L13-13, R-1030-2226).

### Secondary cores and neighbourhood squares

The harbour district along the quay and the inside of each gate each form a small secondary core, and a city of small
area has none (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L19-19, R-1030-1550, R-1030-1802). Public facilities gather in one
large public area for the whole city, around the first square, and in one small square for each neighbourhood (片)
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L23-23, R-1111-1239b). A neighbourhood floats between 2800 and 9200 cells, weighted
by the attraction field (吸引力场): neighbourhoods near the core and the main street are small and their squares dense,
those at the edge and on back streets larger, with a little randomness; one under 2800 cells merges into a neighbour and
one over 9200 is cut again; the strength of the weighting is a trial value (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L861,
L863, R-1111-1630b, R-1111-1643). A small square is 200–800 cells, a trial value, and opens at a junction of three or
four streets, at a bridgehead, at a corner cut by a slanted street or beside a market, formed by the neighbouring blocks
each giving up a corner, so that small squares are dense near the core and sparse outward
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L861, L863; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L23-23). Each neighbourhood's
small square carries a fountain basin and a market, and no tavern (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L19-19,
R-1111-2221; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L854, R-1111-1300) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L849). A private yard is part of its household (household = house + yard), and the public area and the private yard do
not conflict (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L23-23, R-1111-1239b).

### Landmarks and small items

The city has no zone of colossal buildings: its landmark is the palace quarter, and no arena is built by default (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L27-27, R-1030-2226, R-1030-1620).
A round square stands where the old road meets the main street (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L85-86, R-1029-1727②, R-1029-2050a).
The imperial landmarks (地标) of a city are a four-way gate (四面门, A28: four 3×3 octagonal piers under a covered cross opening through which the street passes), a monumental fountain (A29: a niche of about 9×7 with a pool, a row of statues and marble steps, facing the main street or a square), a monumental column (A30: a 3×3 marble base with a column) in the colonnaded square, and the public baths (A26) (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md` L441-445, R-1028-1205).
The four-way gates come as A28, A28b and A28c at 5×3 (11×9), 5×5 (11×11) and 3×3 (9×9), A29 is 13×7, and A30 is 7×7 (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/roman-cards/罗马规格卡总表.md` L21-L23; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/roman-cards/罗马规格卡总表.md` L24; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/roman-cards/罗马规格卡总表.md` L25).

## Main street, secondary axis and old road

### Two types of main street

The main street has two types, set by terrain (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L64-64, R-1102-2344).
In type A (甲型, gate to gate) the main street runs from one land gate to another, the squares hang on its middle stretch, and the palace quarter stands on one side, joined to the main street by the secondary axis (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L64-64; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L741).
In type B (乙型, palace to gate), when land remains on one side only, the main street starts at the palace quarter and forks on the land side to two gates (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L64-64; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L741).
Seen from the core, two arrival roads entering from roughly opposite sides, at an angle of about 120° or more, make type A, and arrival roads all on one side make type B (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L64-64; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L751, R-1103-0039a; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-原意走样二.md` L72-L72, L16-L16).
Streets from the other gates lead to the nearest stop, not to a cross of two axes (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L65-65, R-1030-0940③), and streets inside the gates lead to the nearest core (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L90-91).

### Secondary axis

There are never two axes of the same rank: beside the main street there may be one shorter secondary axis, which ends at the palace or a sanctuary (圣所) (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L66-66, R-1030-1550⑤; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L83).
In type A the secondary axis belongs to the palace quarter: it runs from the palace gate to the main street, and the junction is the first square (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L66-66; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L743, R-1102-2346).
In type B the secondary axis and the main street meet in a four-way gate, with no square (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L66-66).
The cathedral opens no axis: it sits beside a small square next to the main street and is a landmark by its mass (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L66-66, R-1102-2346, R-1110-2314a; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L743).

### Old road

In type A the old road (老路) that crosses the city is the main street: a polyline of straight segments turning only at nodes such as squares and gates, with colonnades on both sides, to which the planned grid attaches block by block with step edges (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L83-83, R-1102-2345, R-1103-1338⑥; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L742) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L742).
Each long straight street ends in something that closes it, a landmark, fountain, hall or gate tower, so that long avenues have an end view and the old road's flavour remains (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L83-83; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L772).
In type B, when a world road enters the map obliquely, the old road, the pre-existing road (先在路), keeps its own direction through the city beside the main street, snapped to the nearest of the eight directions, and an arrival road meeting the grid squarely, at less than 15°, makes none (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L84-84; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L742).
The old road starts from the gate where the arrival road enters the map, not necessarily the main gate, and that gate counts among the city's gates; it is 5 cells wide and has no colonnade (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L84-84).
The old road fixes only the gate it enters by and the direction of its crossing stretch, not the body of the city (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L85-86, R-1030-1448②).

### Eight directions and slanted districts

Main streets, slanted streets and old roads run in only eight directions, horizontal, vertical and 45°, and where they
turn with the terrain one or two short transition segments are added at the bend (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md`
L73-73; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L846, R-1111-1050). A 45° segment is as wide, measured square to its line,
as a horizontal or vertical segment of the same grade, so its cross-section in cells is about 1.4 times the straight
width, and slanted streets do not look a grade narrower (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L73-73;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L857, L860, R-1111-1630a) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L857).
Houses on both sides of a 45° segment turn 45° with it, and the colonnade runs on along the slant from gate to gate,
broken only at junctions (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L73-73, R-1111-1036, R-1103-1220④). A neighbourhood turns
45° only where it hugs the terrain, along a river bank, coast, slope or rock edge, and then the whole neighbourhood
beside that edge slants, its streets and houses following the edge, not one row only; away from the terrain it returns
to horizontal and vertical, and on a plain with nothing to follow, an old road entering the map at a slant can produce
one slanted neighbourhood (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L74-74; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L848, L862,
R-1111-1239a, R-1111-1631). Where a slanted street meets the grid at a junction, the triangle cut off becomes a
street-mouth small square with one small-item position in its middle and none opened elsewhere (infill step 2); the
comparison draft also describes the inner angle of a slanted street's or old road's bend cut into a small triangle,
which it rates as matching, evidence weak, no addition needed (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/理念与机制对照-草案.md`
L34-34; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/理念与机制对照-草案.md` L4; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L165-171).

## Street network

### Grid and blocks

Inside the city a regular street grid is laid along the main street, in one direction and in rows and columns, with
small lanes kept and no dead ends, lanes joining streets at both ends (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L78-78,
R-1030-2221, R-1028-1145). Blocks (街区) are bounded by streets and differ in size and proportion, as long strips, narrow
street-front blocks or one house filling a block, and blocks beside slanted streets, river banks or rock are cut along
that edge into trapezoids and parallelograms, so that the city reads as a surveyed Roman city whose blocks are not equal
squares (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L78-78; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L795, R-1110-0048a;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/街坊外廓-两盛期查证-2026-10-30.md` L4-4) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L732). The city is not one equidistant grid with blocks all of one size (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/红线表.md` L27;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/城建错题转拦截-2026-11-05.md` L40-41, L64-65). That the planet's map has only orthogonal cells
means that the street network as a whole cannot turn freely, not that blocks are rectangles
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/原意走样排查-2026-11-06.md` L18, L28). Blocks follow the main road they face, and a block's
edge may slant (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/复盘-城建-近百版的基础错-2026-11-05.md` L12-13). The street net is pieced together
block by block, the blocks differing in size, alignment and paving, and the seams run along irregular old streets
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L79-79; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/罗马理想城-参照图读法-2026-10-30.md` L17-24).
The picture-review checklist asks that blocks around the core be small and dense, growing larger toward the wall with
garden land, and that the distance of small blocks from the core be measurable

### Road grades and widths

Roads are the main axis of the plan: the network is formed first, as nodes and links, and widths are then set by grade; roads do not give way to houses, and where a width presses on a house the house shrinks or moves (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/城建-生成流程.md` L12; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L944, R-1112-1834).
Both ends of every road land on a road, a gate or the map edge, so that none dangles, and squares and small squares are not nodes of the network but are added after the road axes are fixed and only need to touch a road (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/城建-生成流程.md` L12; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L949, R-1112-2025) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L944).
A road's grade comes from its use, from what it connects, whether people pass through it, or whether it is only a gap between households, and its width then follows the grade, never the width of a band measured on a reference image (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L98-98, L134-134; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/sample.md` L259-260, R-1112-1410).
Widths are odd wherever possible, an odd width having a centre line: main street 5 or 7, old road 5, secondary street (次街) 5, lane 3, ring road (环城路) 3 and narrow way (窄道) 1 (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L98-98; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L927, L929, R-1112-1441) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L924).
An even width is allowed only where the original image gives it in pixels and it lies within one cell of the grade's width; the main street states its width, and a width not stated follows the grade (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L98-98, R-1112-1537).
The 1-cell narrow way serves only as the gap between households and as filling (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L134-134, R-1112-1441).

### Roads reaching buildings

In traced lines and trace-converted grids, roads run in eight directions only; a road cell never covers a house cell;
every building has at least one side against a road, a yard or a porch (counted per building, a wall between does not
count); and the network joins the streets at the map edge (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L97-97;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L900, R-1112-0147). Each road names the building it leads to, and that building,
its small yard included, touches the road or its extension; a yard counts only if it is written as belonging to that
building, and a yard written to none belongs to none (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L97-97;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L923, L925, L928, R-1112-1351, R-1112-1455). A road is drawn to reach its
destination: the building is fixed first, and the road is pulled from the nearest street in eight directions through the
gaps between houses, so that each road's reason can be read and nothing is unreachable
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L900). Streets, lanes, gate passages, roads outside the gates and field paths
form one network, and with the gates open the whole map is a single connected piece (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md`
L100-100, R-1028-1120). A 3-cell ring road runs along the inside of the wall, and buildings do not touch the inner wall
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L90-91, R-1028-0012).

### Colonnades and kerbs

The main street has a colonnade (柱廊) 2 cells deep on each side from gate to gate, with true columns in rows evenly 3 cells apart (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L72-72, R-1029-1250c).
The colonnades break only at the mouths of streets and squares; shop doors open on the colonnade's back wall, and at most one or two stall tables stand between the columns (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/理念与机制对照-草案.md` L32-32; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/理念与机制对照-草案.md` L4; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L73-73).
Shops encroach on the street only on secondary streets, and the main-street colonnade stays continuous (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L749, R-1103-0015).
In section the main street runs: row units of A1 shops, a 2-cell colonnade, columns, the roadway, columns, a colonnade, row units; the colonnade belongs to the city and not to the A1 units, columns stand about every 3 cells on the street line, and the row units come in runs of 7, 4 and 3 rooms, shops (A1, A27b) first behind the colonnade and dwellings after (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md` L295-306; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L750).

The kerb is limestone brick, told from the road by the grain of brick against the grain of slab (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L95-95, R-1029-1916④) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L614).
Kerbs are drawn with base-game floor boundaries and add no object (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L76-76).

### Street and square surfaces

Road surfaces are only stone slab and marble, with no dirt or gravel roads, and their look is borrowed from Anno 117's slab and marble roads, with kerbs on both sides and a centre of another colour (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L96-96, R-1111-1319; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L855).

## Palace quarter

### What it holds

The palace quarter (宫区, also 宫城) holds the palace, the guard and the imperial facilities, and has no arena or theatre, so that it reads as the Empire's seat in the city and not as a palace with an arena (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L38-38; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778, R-1103-1944⑤).
The palace is the group of the governor's palace (长官府): the palace gatehouse, a colonnaded court and the main body of the governor's palace; the guard's barracks stand on both sides inside the palace gate, and the guard has no separate camp (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L38-38; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L39-39, R-1030-0147①).
The governor's palace is built from spec card A12, remade (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L39-39, R-1029-1736).
The quarter is made at the scale of a big New Rome city such as Thessalonica, Smyrna or Athens, with Constantinople's Great Palace borrowed only as an idea, and takes about 5% (4–6%) of the walled land, so that it is one end of the city and not a capital's colossal zone (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L40-40; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L865, R-1111-1834).

The quarter has no church: the palace church and the monastery group are removed, and the only church generated in the city is the cathedral (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L887, R-1111-2232; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L38-38).

### Governor's palace and central garden

The quarter steps down in three tiers from the city side: on the upper terrace the palace gatehouse faces a marble square before the palace, with barracks on both sides inside the gate; on the middle terrace a colonnaded court has the main body behind it; and the lower terrace joins the shuttle pad (停泊点) (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L41-41; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L865, L887, L907).
Gate, court and main body stand on one axis (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L41-41).
The centre of the quarter is a garden (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L41-41; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L910, L911).
There is no separate octagonal hall: the governor's seat, the Tabularium branch office (档案署分署) and the governor's living quarters are rooms inside the main body; the governor's palace is about 23×31; middle and small cities drop the colonnaded court; and a small palace quarter likewise has no hall (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L42-42; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L907, R-1112-0916) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L865).
The main body is the core of the quarter: its ancillaries grow against it, sharing its walls or standing around its yard, not alone on open ground, and what does not fit or suit the main body is built as separate facility buildings outside the quarter, so that the quarter reads as one main building with its ancillaries (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L43-43; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L908, R-1112-0926).
Room use is set by the furniture in each room (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L43-43; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L908).
The main body follows the skeleton of the Anno 117 governor's palace: one building around a yard, wings sharing walls, corner pavilions, an arched gatehouse across a wing and a main door under a half-round roof with a colonnaded front; at the north end of the audience hall a semicircular apse holds the governor's seat, and A12's office, living quarters and archive move into the wings (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L44-44; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L909, R-1112-0930).
Its basis is meaning, not historical correctness: it reads as New Rome carrying the Roman civilisation it inherited (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L909; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L44-44).
How the quarter meets the streets, squares and housing outside it is learned from the area around the Anno governor's palace (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L45-45; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L942, R-1112-1819f).

### Order of housing

Housing is laid in order: plots are cut, each block cutting street-front plots around its edge with the inside left as yard; the attraction field is computed; grades are set; households are merged; cards are placed, with stretching; and the gaps are filled in six steps, gardens and stack yards coming only in the last (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L117-117, R-1103-1220①; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L768).
Streets and public facilities come first and houses after: the skeleton and the composite pieces are laid first, the field is computed from what has been laid, and only then are plots cut, tiers set and cards placed; at the end of each period the field is computed again and merging judged again, so that old plots grow slowly larger and show their age (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/理念与机制对照-草案.md` L35-35; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/理念与机制对照-草案.md` L47-62; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/理念与机制对照-草案.md` L4).

### Attraction field and workshops

The attraction field has positive sources, the squares, the church, the colonnaded main street and landmarks, and negative sources that count noise only: the stone-cutting workshop, warehouses, store-shops, depots, the workshop group and quay stack yards (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L121-121, R-1103-1220②).

### Placing by household

Housing is placed by household, not from a block library: the land that streets enclose is divided along the street into
households, each with its door and street-front wall facing the street and a limited depth, and each takes one spec card
by its plot size (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L130-130, R-1030-1930). Things with an inner structure, such as a
church with its small square or a workshop with its work yard, use building-group cards, and the 23 block modules
already made serve only as household samples and for comparison (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L130-130). One card
makes at most 15% of the buildings in a city, the buildings of one card differ by variants, and a stretched card still
counts as the same card (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/罗马城排法-算法.md` L56-56, R-1030-0005;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L768). A layout is checked so that every piece touches a street, uses lie where
they belong, houses are not all alike and there are no large empty stretches
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/罗马城排法-算法.md` L59-59). A household (户) is one plot together with whatever the
plot holds, such as a yard, garden, vine trellis, colonnade, shrine, workshop yard, stock pen, shed, yard path or front
steps, and "regular" refers to the whole plot, while the house on it may be L-shaped, stepped or of several blocks
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L156; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L957, L962, R-1113-0004). Buildings
tightly adjoining one another form one household and one building, the house's shape being their combined outline, and a
yard joined to them belongs to that household (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L153;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L953, R-1112-2214). A well is a private pool in the courtyard
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L674). Vine trellises and shrines are not made for now

### Slanted houses

Houses take one of three orientations, horizontal, vertical or 45°, their street-front wall following the street before
the door (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L131-131, R-1111-1036). A 45° house is enclosed by stepped walls joined
corner to corner, its door on a horizontal or vertical cell of a step, and diagonal wall textures are later art
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L131-131; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L845). So a neighbourhood along a
slanted street or a river bank reads as rows of slanted houses, not as steps and empty triangles
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L845). Slant cards are made only for the house types that fall on 45° plots, the
small house, the street-front shop and the small workshop; big pieces stay horizontal or vertical and the plot gives way
to them (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L878, L879, R-1111-2114). A slant card is generated from the straight
card as a variant beside stretching: its outer frame turns 45°, its walls step in eight directions, its furniture stays
horizontal and vertical, the triangles along its walls become storage or yard corners, and its sizes are measured on
slanted ground, with one narrower size for small houses; the generated cards are drafts, checked by viewing the images
and corrected by hand at the source (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L878, L879). On a slanted street, a
continuous row of small terraced houses of the A27 slant card on one street face merges into one long slanted house
divided by household, with one ring of stepped outer wall, shared party walls and a street door for each household,
while rows on different sides of a street are not joined (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L164-164;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L889, R-1111-2245). This is the only place where the one-cell gap between
households gives way (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L889; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/进度-斜卡三轮.md` L8, L74).

## Buildings

### Form

Roman character is tiered, so the whole city is not built of composed volumes (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L543-L552). Rich and public buildings are composed of 2–4 volumes with one symmetry axis, door, hall, court and back
hall on it, an open court with a colonnade and a pool or flowerbed, and a vestibule projecting onto the street with
columns; the side-street apse-hall house of the 4th–6th centuries may have door, hall, court and apse off one axis
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L543-L552). Public buildings and churches keep their apses in both eras, while
among rich houses the apse hall is the main type and side-street houses and 10th–12th-century noble houses take square
halls (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L678, L681). Ordinary buildings, tenements, shop houses and colonnaded
shops, are rectangular blocks sharing walls in rows, not small palaces, with an atrium and a row of columns before the
door, in two or three outlines of one kind (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L543-L552). Columns inside a court
belong only to rich and public courts; ordinary houses have the atrium and a front row of columns only
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L543-L552). Warehouses and workshops may be long but are broken into sections
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L543-L552). Rectangular row houses serve only as the sides of closed blocks
enclosing a court, their street side carrying a colonnade and a wide door (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L151-151,
R-1028-2252). Rooms are joined by open courts and colonnades, not by corridors, and the instruction infers (推) from this

## Materials

### Colour and floors

From above, RimWorld does not show roofs, so a city's colour comes only from its walls and floors; the floor has the largest area, so floors are set first and walls after (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L212-212, R-1028-2258).
The colour goal is a classical science-fiction Roman Empire: one style across the city, blending into the base-game art and using base-game materials and finishes as far as possible, with no glossy metal walls, the feel of technology coming only from imperial objects (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L213-213, R-1027-1801, R-1110-1248).
Streets and squares are light, public and imperial buildings ivory and the brightest, ordinary residents warm blocks, and green comes in patches; accents use only purple and gold, never red (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L214-215, R-1029-0755, R-1029-1957, R-1029-1514).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: according to the cited survey pages, roofs are not drawn in the top-down view (they show only in the
> roof-visibility layer), eave shadows are drawn around buildings (fixed in 1.5), and walls and floors can be dyed, to
> be checked against `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/社区参照-罗马城与聚落生成-2026-10-30.md` L92-92;
> `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/社区参照-罗马城与聚落生成-2026-10-30.md` L113-116;

### Walls and doors by tier

Materials follow the four tiers of version 2 (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L219-221; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/roman-cards/罗马规格卡总表.md` L82-L90; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L466, L575, L569).
Public and imperial buildings, the landmarks and the baths, have limestone walls painted ivory (`Structure_Cream`, 195,192,176), and only the facades of imperial landmarks take the ornate wall (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L219-221, R-1029-0755, R-1028-1214; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L856, R-1111-1325).
Rich houses have limestone walls in their natural colour, with marble columns for their colonnades and porches (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L219-221, R-1029-0800).
Ordinary residents have sandstone in its natural colour, a warm pink-brown, so that from afar white public buildings float among the warm ordinary blocks (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L219-221; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L466, L575, L569; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/回报/城卡.md` L72).
Warehouses, workshops and military buildings have natural limestone with no colonnade (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L219-221, R-1029-0800).
On buildings marble is used only for columns and porches (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L219-221, R-1029-0755).
All doors are plain stone in its natural colour, and the imperial purple is not put on doors (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L219-221; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/roman-cards/罗马规格卡总表.md` L94).
Spec cards take their material by tier, gold belongs only to the Empire, and cards other than landmarks use no ornate wall, quoin or large ornate door (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L222-223, L226, R-1028-1214).
High-window walls stand only on the long outer walls of halls: in a columned hall the cells in line with the columns are solid with one or two open cells between them, in a hall without columns two solid cells stand at each end and at least two between windows, and windows take at most 40% of a wall, while houses, barracks, warehouses and workshops have plain walls throughout (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L269-L276).
Imperial building materials are made only for colours or defence strength that paint cannot give, few in number like a DLC: stone-like, in soft whites, no brighter than the base-game cap of about 200, with strength near plasteel (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L375-L378).
The materials of cities and buildings, limestone, sandstone and marble masonry, do not follow Italy (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L208, R-1030-1246③).
