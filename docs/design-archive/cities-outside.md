# Outside the walls, rivers and water

This section covers the land outside a Roman city's walls: what a middle or small city has there and what a big city
leaves out, the cemeteries and roads outside the gates, and how those roads are drawn. It also covers the water in and
around a city: how a river is brought to the city and reshaped, how the city sits across it, its bridges, quays and
harbour, and the seaside city. It gives only the newest line on each subject; each older line that a newer setting
replaced is one Deleted note, and the city's own water system, which belonged to the hydraulics axis the maintainer deleted on 2026-11-24,
is one such note. It meets `cities.md` on the phases in which a city is laid out, `cities-siting.md` on terrain, hills,
mountains and coasts, `cities-walls.md` on the moat, the cleared strip, the gates, the water gates, the wall along water
and the small forts on rock beside the wall, `cities-inside.md` on the palace quarter, the streets, the cemeteries
inside the city and the port among the city's groups, `cities-forms.md` on templates, and `water.md` on hydraulics as a
system beyond one city.

## The land outside the walls

### Middle and small cities

Outside the city walls the land follows the von Thünen rings (杜能圈): garden plots (园圃) lie nearest the gate, larger
fields lie farther out, and beyond the fields the original terrain is kept (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L12;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L790). The Empire keeps no animals, so the outermost ring holds no pasture and no
gate is opened toward one, and no woodland is grown for firewood, since the industrial Empire has no firewood; the
countryside is meant to read as an industrial Empire's, without herds or woods (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L790; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L12; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·墙外.md` L49).

Estates (庄园) stand along the main road, each with several square fields (方田), and the fields are not drawn as one
block outside the city (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L20; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/罗马城排法-算法.md` L81; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L705). The square fields follow the direction of the city's street net:
the main street (主街) leaves the gate into the countryside, and the field blocks form a grid along the directions of the
main street and the secondary axis (次轴) (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L21; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778). The city keeps one main street and one shorter secondary
axis, and no two axes of equal rank are drawn to divide the fields, so that the fields outside the gate share the
direction of the streets inside and read as a surveyed Roman countryside (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L21; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778). Estates are put together
from existing Roman cards, with no new object (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L23; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L668).

No olive groves or vineyards are planted outside the city (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L803; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L15). Where fruit trees would stand, cypress
is used (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L683; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-城卡同类冲突-2026-11-10.md` L31).

### Big cities

A big city keeps none of this: outside its wall only the road from each gate is kept and joined to the map edge, with
no garden plots, fields, estates (villas), ruins or roadside cemeteries (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L961; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L972; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L13-14). The stated
effect is that the big city fills most of the map and the plants saved outside cost no performance (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L14). The open
land kept outside the wall for defence, the cleared strip and the ground left for raids, is counted apart from this rule
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L961; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L14). Middle and small cities keep the rings, estates and fields above (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L972; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L13).

### What does not stand outside

Where a city has farm land outside, it holds no farmhouses or sheds, only fields and the small paths that join them to
the gates or roads, since the Empire's setting has no farmhouses (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L253; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L27; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L1167). No suburban workshops such as kilns or tanneries stand outside
the gates, since industrial Rome has no pre-industrial workshops (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L500; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L28). Outside the city there are no
suburb streets, workshops, inns, taverns, shops, farm huts or sheds (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/红线表.md` L19; `Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L217; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/批量整理-2026-11-06.md` L29).

### Order and cover

The land outside the walls is laid in the fourth phase, and the cemeteries outside the gates from the first (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L16;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L801). Garden plots, cemeteries and rows of cypress (成行的柏树) are laid from the outer edge of the cleared strip
(清空带) outward, so that nothing in front of the wall gives an enemy cover (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L803; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L15).

## Cemeteries outside the gates

A living city has its cemeteries outside its gates, on both sides of the road out, a stretch from the wall and beyond
the cleared strip, in the New Rome (新罗马) skin (皮) (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L32; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L803; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L43). Their graves are the
base game's graves and sarcophagi with New Rome textures, and the source's stated effect is rows of graves on both sides
of the road out, read as a living big city (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L32; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L803). The cemeteries outside the gates are placeholders at
first, to be given art later (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L33; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L744). Old Rome (古罗马) graves are only roadside tombs of the old Roman
style, in ruins (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `docs/glossary.md` L30).

The cemeteries outside the gates are laid in the first phase with the first wall, so that when a big city's second wall
takes them in they can stay as church graveyards (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L801; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L803; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L32). A big city keeps no roadside cemetery

`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L32; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·墙外.md` L56). The cemeteries inside the city are in `cities-inside.md`.

## Roads outside the gates

### Route

Each gate has a road (门外大道) that runs over land to the map edge with at most two bends; it crosses rivers only at a
bridge site and does not cross lakes or sea (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L141; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md` L286). At least 15 cells outside each gate are load-bearing
land, and the moat and the earth bridge over it do not count as facing water (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L142). A gate of a city on an island
opens toward the landing point, where a quay place is marked, and no road is drawn to the map edge (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L143). The last
stretch of the arriving road outside the gate runs along the wall, and the road widens in front of the gate (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L39;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L705; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L755); the heading of the stretch before the gate is in `cities-walls.md`.

### How roads are drawn

Roads are redrawn only on the city and settlement maps this mod generates; the player's home and other maps keep the
base game's roads (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L731; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L43). A redrawn road
has a smooth line falling on the grid in clean steps, and a layered section of surface, kerb and shoulder
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L43; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L731). Forks, bridgeheads and gate
fronts widen into small paved patches (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L43; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L731). Road surfaces are only stone slab and marble, with no dirt or gravel, one system inside and outside the walls
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L855; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L856). Outside the walls the main
road is wide, of hard stone slab with a kerb, and marble is laid only on its last stretch before the city and on the
widened patches before gates, at bridgeheads and at forks; small roads and field paths are slab
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L856; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L43). Materials are put together from
base-game floors as far as possible, with at most one added kind, a Roman stone paving that players can also lay
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L731; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-城卡同类冲突-2026-11-10.md` L47).

The form of a road, its line, section, grade and widened nodes, is shared by all factions, while its surface and roadside
furniture follow the faction's technology: tribal trodden dirt paths, medieval dirt and gravel roads with stone bridges,
modern asphalt (the base game's old asphalt) with street lamps (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L731). New Rome's road is stone-paved with a kerb
and electric lamps, and an Old Rome ruin road is broken old paving, grown with grass and missing blocks (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L47;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L731). New Rome roads draw no drainage ditch, because a ditch cannot be drawn readably from above (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L803).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: the older line of Roman road surfaces, in which a gate road was paved near the gate and dirt beyond, the

> (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md` L286; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成第2轮重做-第一段批准与第二段补充.md` L370; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L731). It was replaced by slab and marble only, with no dirt or gravel, and no drainage

## Rivers

### The river comes to the city

On a plain map with a river, the river is by default reshaped to Rome's need so that the city straddles it and the river
runs through the city; a plain with no river at all is to be discussed separately (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md`
L12; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L728). The river comes to the city, not the city to the river: on the city's
map, terrain generation by default lets the river cross the map naturally through the middle, with the city in the
middle (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L13; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L728). Hills, mountains and
coasts follow their own defaults, given in `cities-siting.md` (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L12;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L728).

The river on the world map is the river that enters the map, and its width is the base game's width for that
world-map river grade (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L17; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L730). The base-game course keeps the map edges where it enters and leaves, and
the stretch between is relaid whole (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L18; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L730). From where it enters, the river meanders at random, with no
set angle around the city and no hard right-angle bend (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L19; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L734).

Where two rivers meet, both layouts are made as variants: the main river runs through the city and the tributary joins
it downstream outside the wall, or the confluence lies at the heart of the city, the two rivers meet inside, the city is
in three parts and there are more bridges (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L27; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L730).

### Where the city sits across a river

A city with a river is built across it by default, because a city on one shore was found too hard to show and a big
river can be crossed as well, with two or three bridges (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L727; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-原意走样二.md` L19; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/罗马城排法-算法.md` L20). The core, the palace quarter
(宫区) and the public core (公共核心) lie on one bank, inside the bend if there is one (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L23; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L808). How large
each bank is and whether the far bank (对岸) becomes a district follow the terrain and the approach roads of that map;
they are not set in advance as a larger and a smaller bank, and the river is not kept away from the middle (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L23;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L808; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L842). A river city is not graded big, middle or small: whether the far bank becomes a district and how
large each bank is are variants chosen at generation, read from the reasons the map's terrain and approach roads give,
not from an area formula (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L37; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L791; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L808).

When the far bank is a district, the wall crosses the river and encloses both banks, with one water gate where the river
enters and one where it leaves; the core bank holds the core, the palace quarter and the public core, and the other bank
the harbour district, workshops, warehouses and inns, the sizes of the banks growing from the terrain with no fixed
ratio (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L38; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L794; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L808). When it is not a district, the wall encloses only the main bank and no bridgehead
fort (桥头堡) stands on the far bank, because people can wade a river and a fort on the far bank would guard nothing
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L39; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L791; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/原意走样排查-2026-11-06.md` L16). The wall encloses a city, not an empty far bank (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L39; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L786; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/批量整理-2026-11-06.md` L28). The far bank is a
variant taken at generation, because a RimWorld city is fixed once it is generated and does not grow (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L786).

How the wall meets the river and where its towers stand when it spans both banks is in `cities-walls.md`.

### The main street and the bridges

In a waterside city the long axis and the main street follow the water, with no fixed ratio of length to width (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L44;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L801). The core is placed near where the approach road crosses the river, with the main bridge facing it, but a
stretch away from the bank, across the quay band and a stretch of bridgehead street (桥头街), so that a straight street
leads from the main bridge into the first plaza and the centre never sits on the bank (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L765).

A city on a river has two or three bridges: one monumental main bridge (主桥) joining the main street and facing the
first plaza, and one or two secondary bridges (次桥) to the far bank's blocks (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L45; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L727; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L842). A bridge
is built where the main road network has to cross the river, and an island in the river is used to divide the crossing
into two spans (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L850; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L68). Seen from above, a bridge is a Roman stone arch bridge: a stone-paved deck, low
parapets on both sides and piers showing above the water (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L45; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L804).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: the older setting that lengthened a waterside city to about 1.5:1 (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L723), replaced by no fixed ratio
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L801).

### River works

Reshaping the terrain is an important part of the Roman city: inside the city the river is made a watercourse with bank
walls (岸墙), quays and bridges, while outside the city it stays as it was (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L57; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L725). The course and width of
the river inside the city are not changed: along the quays the bank walls follow its bends, and only the stretch that
serves as a wall face is made a long straight bank, so that the river reads as a river through the city, not a straight
canal (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L57; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L772). Where the river serves as the wall face, the bank does not give way to the natural shore: it
is rebuilt to the wall's logic, the bank line following the wall by embankment and cutting (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L62; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L712;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/handcity/2026-11-03/整城v3/r2-进度.md` L9); the wall's directions are in `cities-walls.md`.

River works serve only the city: no large cut and fill is made to straighten the wall, and the amount cut and filled is
still reported (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L58; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L725). The reshaping is not wild and spends no great cost on deliberate straightening: the
main river is not bent into a few straight reaches, and outside the city the river keeps its natural bends (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L66;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L693; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L725). Edges made by filling and cutting are laid with soil and grass, the bank line has no right angle,
and no slope is made (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L67; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L804).

## Quays, harbours and seaside cities

Both banks inside the city get bank walls and quays (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L49; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L727). A harbour basin (港池) may be dug into the
city, with the wall turning around it and towers on both sides of the harbour (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L49; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L760). Along the river
inside the city, between the river and the first row of houses, lies a band of bank wall and quay 5 cells wide, the same
number as the bank band outside the wall; the quay group (码头组) and the quay street (码头街) fall in it (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L50;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L765). The band is not left blank: it holds the quay group, the quay street and stockyards (堆场) (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L51;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L766).

The port (港口) is a composite in a symbolic sense, port, inn (客栈) and post station (驿站) in one, so inns and post
stations form no group of their own (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L745; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L757; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L142). In a city with water the port is the quay group by
the river, sharing its warehouses; in a city without water it stands inside the main gate beside the gate group, its exit
facing the main street (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L758; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L142). The port handles only the water route: quays, inn and post station, with
no shuttle point, while freight and Imperial trade visits go to the Imperial air port (帝国空港), which is in
`cities-inside.md` (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L881; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L142).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: the older setting in which the players' shuttle point landed at the port, inside the quay group, so that
> the water and air routes met in one place (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L757; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L758; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·城内.md` L49-50). It was replaced by the Imperial air

A Roman seaside city grows toward its harbour: streets lead to the harbour, landmarks face the sea or stand high,
warehouses line the quay, and the harbour is set into the sea wall; by default it has a harbour and warehouses and no
fields (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-河与水.md` L112; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L668). A warehouse there is only a warehouse, with no grain-ship or ration story, since New Rome
is industrial (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L668). The buildings by a seaside city's quay are warehouses (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L804).
