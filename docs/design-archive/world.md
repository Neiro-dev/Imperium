# The world

This section covers the world Imperium is set in: Sophia and the silence, this planet at the Empire's frontier and its
regions, the Empire as a machine that outlived its centre, Rome as the one civilization the Empire takes its elements
from, how the design draws on Rome, and the path of civilization and technology that made the Empire what it is. Where a
newer setting replaced an older line, or the maintainer deleted it, as with the hydraulics, the older line is given once
as a Deleted note. What Imperium is as a whole is in [imperium.md](imperium.md); the Empire's institutions, people and
the way it governs its regions are in [empire.md](empire.md); the ruins and techprints are in
[ruins-and-technology.md](ruins-and-technology.md); the Empire's weapons are in [military.md](military.md); its
mechanoids are in [mechanoids.md](mechanoids.md); its looks are in [art.md](art.md); the Roman city is in
[cities.md](cities.md); the terms are in [terms.md](terms.md).

## The settled world

Imperium-Knowledge's charter settles the world in three parts: Sophia and the silence, the evolution of civilization and
technology, and the political backstage (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L89, L91, L99, L148;
`Imperium-Knowledge/目录/主文档-01至08.md` L12-15).

## Sophia and the silence

Sophia (索菲亚) is both the planet where the Empire began and its old capital, and the Sophia sector is the core sector
around it (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L93; `docs/glossary.md` L37). IF Terra is a research name for this
alternate-history line of the Earth, not another mother planet from which people emigrated
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L93). A huge disaster of unknown cause struck the old core and paralysed central
administrative coordination and long-distance communication with the outlying sectors, and repeated attempts at contact
gave no reliable result; this breakdown is the silence (失联) (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L95;
`docs/glossary.md` L36). It is not confirmed that the planet Sophia, the core sector or the old centre was destroyed, and
whether the old centre exists, has declined, ignores the frontier or has another successor centre all stay unknown
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L95; `docs/glossary.md` L37). The truth about Sophia, the fate of the lost
personnel, the emperor's hidden matter and the concrete persons of the groups stay unknown as well
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L162).

The old empire, the Empire as it was before the silence, left the ruins (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L93;
`docs/glossary.md` L29). Before the disaster it ran small experiments with spirit-awakening (启灵) technology inside its
political system and gradually gave them up because investment and return did not match; the few prototypes in the ruins
do not mean that the present Empire takes psychic power as its mainstream, and the end of that road is not put down to
the later loss of contact (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L146).

An outlying branch set up a new court and a Roman political centre on the existing military, political and industrial
system, continuing the legitimacy, technology and institutions of the old (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L97).
Real successor states are only design references, not history in this world (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md`
L97). The present Empire has strong military, industrial and transport ability, but being strong does not mean being
willing to conquer everything or able to copy and allot all its technology without limit
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L97). How the Empire came through the silence stays unexplained
(`docs/setting.md` L84). The base game's Empire does not exist in this world (`docs/setting.md` L83).

The Empire does not call itself Rome (`docs/setting.md` L10-11, L15-16, L59; replaces
`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L39, which has the Empire call itself Rome). How the Roman line reaches the
Rimworld, when Earth's Rome fell in 1453 and humanity scattered from Earth, is left unexplained on purpose: the
connection is left to the truth in the ruins, where the player pieces it together, and texts and archives give no clear
answer while never contradicting themselves (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L39, L118). The Empire is not the
villain but has things it does not want to say, which serves its texts and the truth of the ruins: after the last
communication record is handed up, for example, only a version the Empire has edited comes back
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L118).

## This planet and its regions

This planet, the one the colony is on, is to the Empire a frontier world, one of its outermost regions
(`docs/setting.md` L52, L104). The player learns that the Empire's court is not on this planet and that the planet is
governed by a bureaucracy, so that the colony stands at the edge of a large state (`docs/setting.md` L42-48). The regions
are background that helps the player understand the Empire; the player never manages them, and they are not a game
system (`docs/setting.md` L43-44, L47-48, L53, L79-80). How the Empire governs, patrols and garrisons its regions is in
[empire.md](empire.md).

## The Empire as a machine

The Empire is a huge machine that keeps running without any person, the emperor included
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L154; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L6; `docs/setting.md` L97). It
links commands, law, roads, documents and the army into one network: its legitimacy comes from order itself running, it
takes in outsiders but ranks them by status, it works through intermediaries while guarding against them, and it
outlasts any person, the emperor being only one post in it (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L44-48). To a
frontier colony it is not a person to befriend but a huge, rule-keeping system that does not care who the colony is,
only whether its node runs; it gives order and strength beyond what the colony could build, and the price is becoming
part of it (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L50). Imperium's own setting embodies this: the old core lost
contact, an outlying branch carried on its legal line, technology and institutions, and the machine, having lost its
centre, still runs (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L40; `Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L154).

Stories come from the friction between the machine, which runs by its charter, and the colony, which runs by human life,
not from gaps left on purpose; the design keeps the machine consistent, by its rules and predictable, and leaves the
collisions to RimWorld's storyteller (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L154;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L58-65). The research note gives such collisions: a shuttle landing on
time into a burning colony, a reward fixed by the charter that does not match the colony's losses, the line "this
service is entered into the imperial archive", an inspector checking by the book while a raid is at the door, and a
colony grown so strong that it becomes a dangerous intermediary whom the old guard begins to suppress
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L59-64). Making the machine a person is the mistake: delegates of the
three departments visiting, colonists given offices in the city, and the absence of anyone treated as a gap
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L66-69; `Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L154). In the same
way, the three departments do not appear in person and leanings are felt only through content, the charter fixes
thresholds so that a rich colony is recognised sooner by its strength, inspectors act by the charter, the colony pays no
tax, imperial staff act only inside the area the player marks out, and the emperor at the triumph in the capital is a
post within the ceremony, which is itself a means of building loyalty
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L71-76).

## Rome

Rome is the one Roman state and civilization, from its beginnings to 1453, from which the Empire takes its elements
(`docs/glossary.md` L34; `docs/setting.md` L93). It is one continuous state and civilization, the evidence being the
continuity of its throne, its law and its military and political system, and not a period
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L37; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L99;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/罗马建筑法与行规-理念.md` L6). Rome is treated as one: nothing the player reads splits
Rome into separate states or peoples, and no text uses a name that sets part of Rome apart as another state or people
(`docs/setting.md` L10-11, L20, L62, L93). The design writes "Rome", "ancient Rome", "New Rome" or a specific city and
date; it does not write "Byzantine", which serves only as a cataloguing word for searching, and it does not treat
"Eastern Rome" as a class set against Rome (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L37, R-1030-2152;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L99-100).

The charter's positioning gives Imperium "a coherent Eastern-Roman-style (东罗马式) civilization identity"
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L36), and its addition sets out what that identity is: the high technology is
Rome at its peak, and the line runs from ancient Rome through medieval Rome to 1453 as true Roman continuity, not the
costume of one period (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L37). The high technology is what Roman civilization,
unbroken, would be at its peak, not a science-fiction skin pasted onto Rome (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L571). The civilization it draws on is this continuous development of Rome, from ancient Rome through its later eastern
empire to the successor states (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L101). The old empire mainly used Latin and had
a Greek tradition, while the present Empire is mainly of Greek administrative culture and keeps the old Latin military
and legal names; language does not show the level of technology (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L101), and
the form of a language does not set the Empire apart from Rome (`docs/setting.md` L58).

### Old Rome and New Rome

Within the one Rome, the design tells two looks apart: Old Rome (古罗马), the look and content of the old empire and its
ruins, such as temples and roadside tombs of the old style, and New Rome (新罗马), the look and content of the present
Empire and its living cities, such as churches, city cemeteries and machines (`docs/glossary.md` L27, L30). The ruins
lean toward Old Rome and the present Empire toward New Rome (`docs/setting.md` L21-22, L57;
`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L62). The rules for building cities, walls and districts are not divided by period: the
Roman kit has one set of rules, and Old Rome and New Rome tell apart only the skin and content of cities and objects
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L724, R-1030-2152; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L101; replaces
`Imperium-Knowledge/裁定归档/2026-10.md` L35, which laid all Roman material out as two heydays of equal standing). The
present Empire's machines all take the forms of New Rome, not divided by rank or by period, and Old Rome's forms are kept
for the ruins (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L140, R-1105-1908; `Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L218;
replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L216, which had the Empire take a later stage of New Rome from rank III).

Defaults and first examples take New Rome, and where New Rome's evidence is thin, ancient Rome does not fill the gap and
no numbers are made up (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L103). Liquid fire is kept
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L104, R-1029-1504). The two types of Roman city are called arid (干热区) and
temperate (温寒区) (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L105; `docs/glossary.md` L162, L208).

## Drawing on Rome

The Empire's elements come from Rome as reliable sources describe it, not from popular images of Rome such as its
engineering and its military (`docs/setting.md` L10, L60-61; replaces the first point of
`Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L303, which judged Rome by whether it reads like New Rome and did not look history up
for the design). The Empire takes elements from Rome and is not a reproduction of Rome (`docs/setting.md` L13, L95).
Roman forms are designed on the logic of how things were really used and how they followed the terrain in their time,
not as an idealized look, so that each form can say why it looked so in Rome; city walls following the terrain are the
example (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L193, R-1103-2243). Visual choices, such as emblems, flags, colours and
components, look first at how the Romans themselves did it before anything is invented
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L238, R-1029-1507).

Every item of imperial content, whether component, building, mechanism or facility, answers the lineage test (谱系检验):
what its ancestor is in the Roman line, from ancient Rome to medieval Rome to 1453; what cannot answer does not enter
imperial content (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L38). The basis is that the high technology grew out of Rome
itself and is not a skin, as the legion became the mechanical garrison post; that the time depth of the line gives the
Empire its huge machine and the colony its smallness; and that a line of inheritance cannot be copied, which is the real
divide from VFE Classical and the base game's Royalty (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L38). The ancestor matches
in at least two of function, name and place in the city, while the form may differ, and content made for medieval
compatibility is not subject to the test (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L38).

Having existed is not being able to represent: only features shared by the large cities outside Italy enter the rules,
and forms seen only in small towns, army camps and veteran colonies do not become rules
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L38; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/审查清单-条文.md` L55;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L111). The base set of references is Roman history outside Italy: the
eastern provinces, Gaul, North Africa and Iberia; Italian cities such as Pompeii and Ostia are content of the ruins, not
references for cities and buildings, and Britannia and Germania are too remote to use
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L109, R-1030-1246, R-1030-1252; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L692; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/审查清单-条文.md` L53-54; `Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L284;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/改法-入口瘦身-2026-11-10.md` L157; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/规则表-2026-11-11.md`
L38; replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L37, which drew the range from the Empire's core belt with Italy
in it). Italian local special cases and textbook army camps are not imperial rules
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/审查清单-条文.md` L54). References take what New Rome inherited from earlier and both
heydays share; what is recorded only for the New Rome period and in scholarly opinion is doubtful and is no basis alone,
while what was unique to early Rome, the camp city, the Italian city and the early empire's rules, is no reference, and
Italy itself serves only as ruins and comparison (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L891, R-1111-2307;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/设计会话工作规矩.md` L111).

## Civilization and technology

Imperium's industrial start is invented: large-scale water-powered production becomes lastingly profitable, which brings
long-term investment, maintenance, the machining of parts, talent and the organization of manufacture, and these then
drive heat engines, electricity, automation and spaceflight; it is not that a watermill exists and an industrial
revolution follows (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L103, R-1103-1133). Why the Empire grew as it did follows
from its situation, not from being different for difference's sake, through four causes
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L103):

1. It is a hegemonic empire, not one state among competing states: it faces mostly nomads and raiders on the frontier
   and no equal opponent, wants reliable, mass-producible and simply supplied equipment rather than generation after
   generation of new weapons, and so formed no firearms revolution (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L104).
2. It has long been short of soldiers: machines replace soldiers and are the main combat force, inheriting the Roman
   army's strengths of discipline, formation and logistics, each weak alone and very strong in combination
   (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L105).
3. Industry grew out of state engineering: once expansion stopped, labour became dear, and with the state's demand for
   army grain and city supply, large water-powered production became profitable; state workshops produce to uniform
   specifications, and the Empire is standardized everywhere (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L106).
4. Legitimacy rests on continuity: the ancestors' form is orthodox and state workshops keep the proven patterns, and
   after the disaster of the silence, not depending on the network and being repairable when broken count for more
   (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L107).

The spine of the Empire's technology is the tradition of building machines: its start is the mechanics that really
existed in the Hellenistic age, and the "if" is that the economic need of water-powered production pulled it on to
precision machining, automation and machines, machines first and space later (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L109,
R-1103-1133). The name of this axis is internal only, with the working name 自动机传统 (the automaton tradition): players
never see it, and the research names they see follow the base game's structure without a new axis word, as the base game
never named its mechanoid line for players (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L190;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L110). One other name was weighed and withdrawn: "Mechcraft"
(机械工艺), recommended in a first draft and withdrawn on review at 11:05, because in the base game's Chinese it reads as
the fabricor's line, it collides with the Crafting skill, it looks too like Mechtech in the research list and it is an
engineering course name; the same note says 自动机 reads in Chinese as a term of computer theory, jargon like 工质, yet it
stays the working name (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L104-109).

This world line had no gunpowder and firearms revolution, so weapons, armour, engineering, power, materials, the arms
industry and logistics developed along other needs, not merely keeping the look of an ancient bow; ordinary combustion,
chemistry, fuels, metallurgy, jets in the manner of Greek fire and spaceflight are not ruled out
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L111). Different fields may be unevenly strong, and none is declared to lead
across the board before it is designed (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L111). The Empire's weapons are in
[military.md](military.md).

What the base game fixes, psychic abilities, mechanoid cores carrying psychic source power and the mechanitor framework,
is true in this world, and the Empire developed these on its own and reached the same end; this covers only what the
base game fixes (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L190). The Empire had machines first and space later, its cores
carry psychic source power with poor efficiency, the line that mechanoids came from Roman automata is not written, the
player is given no psychic skills, and the present Empire still does not take psychic power as its mainstream
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L190).
