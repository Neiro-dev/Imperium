# Forces and weapons

This section gives who fights for the Empire and how its forces are made up, what its weapons are and are not, its
combat machines rank by rank as the military design orders them, the balance its forces aim at, and the guardians that
defend the old empire's ruins. It meets [mechanoids.md](mechanoids.md), which holds each machine's own card and the
design of the Empire's machines as a whole (bandwidth, cost, cores, rebuilding, the work machines and the families of
weapon effects), wherever a combat machine is named here; [empire.md](empire.md) for the Empire's people, the commander,
rank, and the military aid that influence buys; [ruins-and-technology.md](ruins-and-technology.md) for the ruins the
guardians stand in and the techprints of weapons and equipment; and [cities-inside.md](cities-inside.md) for the
barracks and garrison stations of imperial cities.

## Who fights for the Empire

The Empire is a state of people, whose people fill all of its roles, and each of them is either a combatant or a
non-combatant (`docs/setting.md` L96, L101, L75-76). When the player fights the Empire's people, none of them carries a
gun (`docs/setting.md` L26-27, L66). The security of important persons takes an elite approach, the honor guard
(`docs/setting.md` L77-78), and how each of the Empire's regions is patrolled, defended and garrisoned is decided for
that region (`docs/setting.md` L98-99). The commander is the imperial officer, a mechanitor, who leads the Empire's
mechanoids in musters, garrisons, transits and raids, and whose mechanoids go out of control when he falls
(`docs/glossary.md` L48; `Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L169). Biotech, when it is active, adds the
Empire's mechanoids whose counterparts come with it, and Imperium is complete with Royalty alone (`docs/glossary.md`
L14; `docs/imperium.md` L108-111).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: an older line made the Empire's forces machines: the Empire sends humans only as officials and
> commanders, its escorts and soldiers are all machines (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L157), its military
> strongpoints hold only commanders and machines (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L187), machines are its main
> fighting force, and the weapons and armour that people use are mostly ceremonial (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md`
> L125), and the weapons it issues go basically to machines (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L108). The root
> documents replaced it with an Empire of people who fill all of its roles, each a combatant or a non-combatant
> (`docs/setting.md` L96-97, L101, L75-76).

"The Empire uses no guns" binds the Empire's own persons: the gun-carrying persons among the base game's Empire person
kinds now in use are deleted directly, imperial military persons are designed separately later, and CBF's garrison
persons are not used for the imperial side, though they still apply to other sides
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L171; `Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L47). The removal of
the gun-carrying Empire_Fighter kinds from the Empire's use is not yet implemented
(`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/code-status.md` L73). The colonists' weapons are designed separately, not copied from the
machines' weapons (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L108).

The garrisons of imperial outposts are NPC variants of the Empire's base machines with a few people, commanders and
officers, and no base-game guards stand at imperial outposts; the pawns of the machine garrisons are scheduled
separately (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L209). The military aid sends NPC variants of the base machines, and which
machine types each of the three departments sends is decided once the machine roster is complete
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L210). Caravan machines carry for imperial transport convoys in missions; they are for
NPCs only and are not opened to the player, since the Empire does not open everything to the player, and their design is
pending (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L208).

## The Empire's weapons

### What the Empire does not use

No person of the Empire uses a gun, and when imperial people attack the colony, none of the weapons the player sees is a
gun (`docs/setting.md` L26-27, L34-36, L66). None of the Empire's weapons, artillery included, is fired by gunpowder or
drives a projectile electromagnetically (`docs/setting.md` L26-28, L37-39, L67). The Empire can still field artillery,
bolt throwers among it (`docs/setting.md` L27); artillery does not count as a gun, and a bolt thrower counts as
artillery, not as a crossbow (`docs/setting.md` L69). The hand-held crossbow is not a weapon of the Empire, and slings
are not used (`docs/setting.md` L71); weapon forms take Rome's representative ones, Rome did not use the crossbow as a
weapon, and individual cases that have sources do not count (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L200, R-1104-2217).
Other ranged technology is open to the Empire (`docs/setting.md` L68): a weapon is first thought of from future
technology and then from historical examples (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L200, R-1104-2217).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: an older line drew the limit of the Empire's weapons more narrowly: its ranged weapons left out firearms
> only (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L200), electromagnetic projection was first counted as a gun, and the
> machines' integral weapons were then allowed the gun form with only gunpowder left out
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L201; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L424, R-1106-2019), and a hand-held
> crossbow, if adopted, would lean toward foreign, Latin or mercenary forces and specific uses, with no unit approved
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L142). The root documents' weapons replaced it (`docs/setting.md` L64-71).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: whether a beam counts as a gun is undecided, and so are the armour penetration of a ranged machine's

> (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/RTS远程兵种武器类别调研-2026-11-05.md` L462, L473, L501); a survey recommends keeping the

### Classical in form

The Empire's weapons look classical but are modern weapons of another line of technology
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L122). Without gunpowder there is no road of chemical explosive propulsion
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L123). Firearms depend on propellant and the chemical industry and supply chain
behind it, which the frontier after the disaster cannot sustain, while spears and bolts can be forged on the spot
wherever there is steel, which fits the lesson of not depending on the network and being able to repair what breaks
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L124). The legion's form carries legitimacy, and the conservative Empire takes the
look of its ancestors as orthodox (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L126). Contact with outside firearms does not by
itself bring a full re-equipment: the comparison is with mature systems of weapon, manufacture, control, supply and
tactics, selective learning needs a concrete benefit, and a long history does not stand in for real performance
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L144).

The Empire's technical principle, for all machines and equipment, adds no parts to classical weapons and equipment but
pushes their own principle to the extreme: the pilum pierces armour with its long thin shaft, so it is thrown by a
mechanical arm to the effect of a heavy warhead, and as the aqueduct bears weight with arches, the loader bears weight
with arches and carries goods far (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L128-132). Two earlier examples, the
petasos brim shading an observation slit and the comb teeth of the vallus, are deleted
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L135-136).

The bow is the Empire's regular main ranged tradition; large ballistae are a separate lineage of mechanical artillery,
and advanced melee, armour and engineering keep evolving as well (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L142).
Mechanization lightens the burden of drawing, repeated control and arrow supply, but it neither prescribes high-arc fire
for the whole army nor claims that the bow is better than other principles in every battle
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L142).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: the weapons and equipment these reasons and principles drove by water or by fluid pressure belonged to
> the hydraulics the maintainer deleted: the ballista developed into projectors driven by hydraulic and pneumatic
> pressure, in the aqueduct's logic of Roman form and industrial function (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L123);
> and the throwing spear carrying its own water propulsion, the falcata with a liquid channel in its spine, the water
> wheel made a turbine ring that powers cranes, and Ctesibius's double-cylinder pump made as a pair of Roman columns
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L130-134).

### The machines' weapons

The mechanoids' built-in weapons are set in the section Weapons of pull request #63 of the Imperium repository
(`docs/setting.md` L69-70). The three families of weapon effects, mono and plasma among them, are used only at ranks IV
and V (`docs/glossary.md` L123, L126; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L442, R-1110-0207). The weapons of ranks II
and III do the base game's ordinary damage, bullet, stab, cut and blunt, with none of the families' added effects, and
look like the base game's charge weapons (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L442;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/入账-机械1110批.md` L19, L29). The weapons of the Empire's machines are integral, like the
base game's mechanoid weapons: a dropped one is destroyed and colonists cannot take it; the form may be a gun, with a
barrel and no grip, trigger or stock, like the base game's mini-shotgun, and its name and description may be written
apart from its form, as the base game's charge lance looks like a gun and is called a lance
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L424, R-1106-2019).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the root documents set the machines' built-in weapons in a pull request the archive cannot read
> (`docs/setting.md` L69-70), so whether the integral gun form and the ordinary damage of ranks II and III still hold or
> were only not restated is unclear (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L424, L442).

### Equipment

The oil flask is a civil administration item of rank I that is thrown to spread a patch of oil, which slows whoever
walks into it and turns into a sea of fire when lit (`docs/glossary.md` L82; `Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L90).
The standard forward smoke projector is a military rank I piece of equipment that projects a cloud of smoke at a spot,
which blocks sight, and is refilled with chemfuel (`docs/glossary.md` L89); its smoke time is decided to be that of one
base-game smoke grenade, not yet implemented (`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L26).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: how long the smoke of one smoke grenade lasts, which the smoke projector's smoke follows, to be checked
> against `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L26).

## The combat machines by rank

In a discussion record, the basic soldier draws on the ordinary infantry of the Eastern Roman battle line, which carries
out regular fighting and keeps the ranks; the shield-and-spear unit strengthens defence, blocking and guarding of
positions; the archer gives sustained ranged support; and heavy cavalry draws on the charge of the cataphract and the
possibility of cavalry using ranged weapons, made into two combat modes of one machine
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L399-405; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
L436-439). All of the present Empire's machines take the form of New Rome (新罗马), with no division by rank or by peak,
and the forms of ancient Rome are kept for the ruins alone; machines of ranks II and III differ by the amount of armour,
their bulk and their banners, and a machine that rises a rank does not have its look redone by era
(`Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L218, R-1105-1908; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/散兵.md` L10) (replaces rank II as
the old peak and rank III as the new, `Imperium-Knowledge/裁定归档/2026-10.md` L6).

Each combat machine is matched to a base-game mechanoid of its tier, and its own card is in
[mechanoids.md](mechanoids.md).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: the base game's mechanoids by bandwidth and combat points, which the pairings below rest on: bandwidth
> 1, militor 45, scorcher 75, termite 110; bandwidth 2, cyclops 110, pikeman 110, legionary 150, scyther 150; bandwidth
> 3, tesseron 150, lancer 190, tunneler 250; bandwidth 4, the four centipedes 400; bandwidth 5, centurion 250, diabolus
> 500, war queen 600; to be checked against `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/原版数据/规律.md` L91-105).

### Rank II

Rank II has one combat machine, the stratior (机械列兵), matched to the base game's militor, and five machines in all,
one for combat and four for work (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L405, R-1106-0647). The stratior is the Empire's
most basic and most numerous soldier, humanoid, a ranged soldier that is made to be easy to produce in bulk
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L405, L425; `docs/glossary.md` L139). Its name means an ordinary soldier, the
ordinary infantry that fights in and keeps the ranks of the New Roman battle line; "line infantry" and "volley" are only
popular names and stay off its card (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L444, R-1110-0208). It stands to shoot, with a
long cooldown and a slightly heavier single shot; the long cooldown is not turned into setting up, an overlong aim or a
synchronized volley, and it has no stopping power or slowing of its own, no deployment, formation bonus, aura or active
skill; it is not fragile, and it is a choice beside the militor in the same slot, not the militor's upgrade
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L436, L444). The militor holds close by, while the stratior fights at middle range
and lasts a little longer (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L444).

The stratior has the base game's militor light body, with the militor's health and part health, and armour of 30%
against sharp and 15% against blunt (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L444). Its gun does ordinary damage, 15 a shot,
with armour penetration by the base game's default; the damage, the aiming of 1.5 seconds and the cooldown of 3.5
seconds stand until testing; its accuracy at touch, short, medium and long range is 65%, 80%, 75% and 55%; and its
bayonet stabs for 10 with 15% penetration every 2 seconds, above the militor's headbutt and below a knife
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L444, R-1110-0208). The gun is one integral long gun, a little longer than the
mini-shotgun, and until its formal name comes with its look the game uses the code's temporary name 列兵长枪
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L449, R-1111-0646).

### Rank III

Rank III holds the scutator (机械戍卫), matched to the scyther, the sagittor (机械弦卫), matched to the pikeman, and the
deepborer (深钻机), matched to the tunneler; the scorcher's flame slot and the cyclops slot that Odyssey adds are not yet
made (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L441, R-1109-2337). The bandwidth and body size of rank III machines follow
their base-game counterparts, and the regular army moves a little slower than the base game, showing a formation rather
than an assault (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L66); the values of each rank III machine are checked again when
rank III reopens (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L393, R-1105-1528).

The scutator is rank III's basic line infantry (基础阵线步兵), not heavy infantry: the regular front line, relying on its
ranks and its shield, with no single quality standing out (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L445, R-1110-0208a;

reopens from the usual values of a base-game medium machine of bandwidth 2, apart from the ranged shield, and are not
piled toward toughness (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L445). Its ranged shield stops shots, it holds the line and
pushes forward, slows with its thrown spear and stops charges, with a little more ranged and a little less melee than
the machine it grew from, the proportion and values to be set when rank III reopens (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
L423, L445). The spear it throws is the same as the spear in its hand, in texture, stab damage and values, balanced
again when rank III reopens; spare spears sit behind the shield, and when an enemy comes within 8 cells it pulls one and
throws it, with no stock, no count and no supply; the slowing is the effect of being hit by the spear, the thrown spear
has no formal name of its own, and the machine's look carries no water elements (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L446,
R-1110-0208b). The heavy javelin, the pilum, with its breaking of shields and armour, stays with the old empire's legion
soldier (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L402).

The deepborer belongs to the tunneler's family: its body keeps its role of self-defence with deep drilling as side work

R-1106-1648a).

Rank III also has core machines, which come as core technologies when the colony leans far enough toward a department
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L119; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L159). The signifer (军旗机), the military
department's core machine, carries the imperial standard and has no weapon; it is fragile and only gives a moving
operating range at the base game's mechanitor's measure, with no extra bandwidth, fire, medicine or aura, and the
operating range is extended only by the banner and by fixed-range facilities, the one exception being the Alarius's
relaxed pursuit (`docs/glossary.md` L135; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L159). Its values, set on 2026-10-26 and
awaiting testing, are bandwidth 1, speed 3.8, armour 10% and 5%, health 0.8, and a recipe of steel 80, plasteel 52 and
components 7, value about 844 (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L113-120). The lictor (机械扈从), the civil administration's
core machine, is named after the attendant of Roman magistrates and carries the fasces: it joins the line with bandwidth
3 and does control, escort and prison work there (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L194-196; `docs/glossary.md` L115).
Its fasces has two forms: without the axe, by default, a blunt blow that stuns people and adds EMP against machines,
breaking shields without harming friendly machines; with the axe, a heavy sharp blow of high damage and no control; the
player switches between taking a prisoner alive and a quick win (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L197). It takes those
downed in the axe-off form to prison on the spot, which the player can switch off, and works as a warden (feeding,
recruiting and lowering resistance, conversion and enslavement with Ideology, suppression of slaves with Ideology,
interrogation with Anomaly) and escorts in the base game's escort mode (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L198-200). Its
draft values, to be measured, are speed 3.4, body size 1.0, armour 60% and 30%, health 1.5, and a recipe of steel 100,
plasteel 110 and components 10 (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L202). The merchant department's slot for a badly
lopsided, non-standard machine is left empty until rank III reopens (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L393,
R-1105-1528). Salvus, a repair and rescue machine, is paused, with no final look or full play, and its old sketch values
are not revived (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L125-127).

### Rank IV and above

The scorpio (机械伏蝎), a bolt thrower, moves to rank IV's shooter slot, its base-game counterpart still to be set
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L441, R-1109-2337). It is the first of the large ballista line of mechanical
artillery, without gunpowder, and it fires piercing bolts in a straight line only when set up
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L166, L170; `docs/glossary.md` L132). It cannot attack while moving, its deploying and
folding take about 360 ticks each for now, and its range is longer than that of every mobile machine, the absolute value
to be set after checking the base game; each bolt hits up to three targets with falling damage, 100%, 70% and 40% for
now, and stops at walls and buildings, cover intercepting it as in the base game; friendly fire follows the base game's
rules, its ammunition is unlimited, and it survives like the base game's militor (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
L170-175). It does not take the cyclops's special attack on shields, which is left to the old empire's legion soldier
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L393).

The cataphract (具装骑兵) of rank IV is matched to the base game's centipede tier: a heavy main battle machine whose core
work is breaking formations, one machine with two modes in place of placeholder types for the centipede's three
weapons; its prototype is the Sasanian or Eastern Roman cataphract (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L439,
R-1109-2331e). Its charge ignores pawns in its way, is stopped by terrain and tramples enemies on its path before it
fights in melee; its ranged mode fires heavy shots one by one from a halt at middle range, may move between shots and
needs no setting up; switching modes takes time, its shooting is automatic, and it has no forced charge after a shot, no
projectile-specific mechanic, combo or charge bar (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L439).

The bombardier (机械焰蝽) is matched to the base game's diabolus: a fire cannon that fires only once set up, the final
weapon of heavy fire; it does not take over all of the diabolus's values and attack rules, and does not inherit its
death explosion; its range, damage, tier and unlocking are set separately (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L437,
R-1109-2331c; `docs/glossary.md` L103).

The mechanicus (检修机) is a rank IV base machine matched to the base game's paramedic: it neither makes nor repairs
machines and only takes the mechanitor's place in commissioning them at a build depot; the mechanitor repairs as in the
base game, the field repair swarm repairs injuries within its range and the auto-rebuild depot repairs and rebuilds
wrecks, while the berth only charges (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L151-155; `docs/glossary.md` L119). The
Empire's mother machine is matched to the war queen and belongs only to a high-rank mother-machine branch: it stores
resources, builds sub-machines inside itself and keeps, maintains, releases and recovers them, its sub-machines being
reusable, not timed summons, with a cap for each mother (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L440, R-1109-2331f).
Rank IV's Legio special techprints keep only a direction, with no count of types or combat values
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L161).

## Balance

Against the base game's AI, the Empire's force aims to win about half of its fights, and in the fights it wins no more
than half of it survives (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/Code-RankII-戍兵复测.md` L38;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/Code-RankII-规模对战.md` L17, R-1104-1350). Strength is judged at the scale of a normal
raid (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/整理记录-2026-11-02.md` L192, R-1104-1518a). By the rule for judging, the
Empire's side is too strong mainly against the base game's AI and against melee
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/Code-RankII-对照测试.md` L22, R-1104-1015). When every row of the Empire's side comes out
strong in the large-scale tests, that is read as a diagnostic signal of being too strong, not as a verdict
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/Code-RankII-规模近战.md` L26-34, R-1104-1709, R-1104-1724).

## The old empire's guardians

The guardian machines of the old empire (古帝国守卫) appear only as enemies and the player cannot obtain them; ruins no
longer hold lost machine technology, and the pack camel, Legionarius and siege machines are deleted
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L205). The old-empire guardian is its own object and is not used as a placeholder
for the player's machines (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/对象表.md` L21; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/红线表.md`
L16).

The guardians only guard sites and never raid colonies; they wake when the player acts, not on a random countdown; their
weakness comes from their behaviour, so ordinary weapons can beat their trick; and each site holds one to three of them,
with no reinforcement (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L94). Guardians beaten by their intended trick shut down
and yield more old-empire parts and documents, while brute-force dismantling yields only wreckage
(`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L94). The vigil (守夜者), in the first layer and deeper, patrols a fixed route
and on seeing colonists runs to a horn pillar in the site, whose sound wakes every guardian there, so the player stops it
on the way or takes the horn down first (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L91; `docs/glossary.md` L155). The custos
(守像), in the second and third layers, hides among the statues of a hall and steps down from its plinth when the player
restores power to the area or removes the archives; while its plinth is powered it heals and reduces damage within a
range through clearly visible conduits, so cutting the conduits or luring it out of range defeats it
(`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L92; `docs/glossary.md` L151).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: the vigil's water cannon, which knocks people down and puts out fire with low damage, belonged to the
> hydraulics the maintainer deleted (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L91).

The old empire's legion soldier (军团兵) belongs to the guardians: it attacks, throwing the heavy javelin, the pilum, as it
charges, which breaks shields and armour, and then fights with short sword and large shield; the classic legion of pilum
and short sword is ancient Rome and stands among the ruins' guardians, and in the Alpha and Beta versions the player
only sees and fights it (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L257, R-1031-0109; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/戍卫.md` L67,
L121). In the maintainer's picture of its entrance, when a ruin's door opens, a sleeping legion soldier wakes, stands,
throws and charges, the exact waking and triggering left to when the ruins are made (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
L258, R-1031-0110; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/古帝国守卫.md` L12).
