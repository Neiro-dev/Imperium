# Terms

This section lists the names in force in Imperium's design and the names they replaced, one row for each thing, by the
groups of Imperium-old's glossary: imperial interaction; techprints, research and equipment; mechanoids; ruins; world
and setting, with Rome and its periods; city building and generation; and the framework. Base-game terms that the design
uses come last. How names are chosen and written, the order of terms and the keeping of protected terms are in
[text.md](text.md); what each named thing does is in the section on its subject, such as
[mechanoids.md](mechanoids.md), [empire.md](empire.md), [administration.md](administration.md),
[orders.md](orders.md), [ruins-and-technology.md](ruins-and-technology.md), [cities.md](cities.md) and the cities-*.md
files, and [mantle.md](mantle.md).

## Reading the tables

Each row gives the term in force as the glossary writes it, the Chinese name where the sources give one, what the thing
is, the names it replaced and the pointers. The glossary lists the terms in force with their meaning and source, and
the deprecated-terms data lists the names replaced or withdrawn, each with its new term, `a new name is pending` or
`removed` (`docs/glossary.md` L3-5; `CONTRIBUTING.md` L103-109). The two are the newest texts of the design, so where an older passage gives
another name for the same thing, the row gives the glossary's name and lists the older one among the names replaced. A term the glossary marks as not shown to players is a design name only. English
renderings of names the sources give only in Chinese are this archive's.

## Base-game terms

The glossary also lists the base-game and expansion terms that the design uses, with, where there is one, the key that
holds each text (`docs/glossary.md` L3-5; `.claude/rules/game-text.md` L36-39). Imperium adds to some of them: Biotech,
when it is active, lets the colony make and control imperial mechanoids and adds the Empire's mechanoids whose
counterparts come with it; Odyssey, when it is active, adds ruins of the old empire that stay where they are on the
world map and yield techprints that come only with it; the Empire replaces goodwill with relations of its own; Royalty
is the expansion Imperium remakes and the only one it requires; registering a techprint the Empire yields authorizes its
technology at once; the colony may buy a shield core from rank I and make one from rank III; the medieval and tribal
city kits are built for the medieval and neolithic tech levels; and the monosword, the plasmasword and the zeushammer
are the models of the mono, plasma and EMP families of imperial weapon effects (`docs/glossary.md` L14;
`docs/glossary.md` L25; `docs/glossary.md` L28; `docs/glossary.md` L35; `docs/glossary.md` L86; `docs/glossary.md` L92;
`docs/glossary.md` L124; `docs/glossary.md` L127; `docs/glossary.md` L144; `docs/glossary.md` L190-192).
