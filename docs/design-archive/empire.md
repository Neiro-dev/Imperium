# The Empire

This section gives Imperium's Empire as a faction: what it is and what it draws on, how it is governed, the people it
sends and how they look, what of the base game's Royalty it has given up, and the colony's place in it, rank, with
promotion, the leaning of the three departments, influence, the campaign, rank V and the cost of joining. The Empire's
forces and weapons are in [military.md](military.md) and its machines in [mechanoids.md](mechanoids.md); this section
meets mechanoids.md where rank opens the Empire's machines and where rank II brings a mechlink. How a colony first meets
and joins the Empire and how the Empire answers a colony outside it are in [relations.md](relations.md); the imperial insignia, landing pads,
imperial area and visits in [administration.md](administration.md); orders, requests and claims in
[orders.md](orders.md); techprints and core technologies in [ruins-and-technology.md](ruins-and-technology.md); the
colours the Empire shares with its cities and machines in [art.md](art.md); and the Empire's own cities in
[cities.md](cities.md) and the sections that follow it.

## What the Empire is

### Rome behind the Empire

The Empire's elements come from Rome as reliable sources describe it, not from popular images of Rome such as its
engineering and its military (`docs/setting.md` L10-11, L60-61). The Empire takes elements from Rome and is not a
reproduction of Rome (`docs/setting.md` L13, L95). It does not call itself Rome (`docs/setting.md` L10-11, L59), and the
form of a language does not set it apart from Rome (`docs/setting.md` L58).

Rome is one: every Roman example, rule and check in the design has entries and historical examples from both peaks,
peak one and peak two (盛期一、盛期二); the default value and the first example come from peak two, and ancient Rome is
its early layer (`Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L56, R-1028-2325). Trebizond (特拉布宗) is not a reference,
and real successor regimes are design references only (`Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L216, R-1105-1642).

The Roman elements the Empire takes are, in politics, the emperor and the purple robe, and in culture, marble, the toga
and the Greek robe, and Greek and Latin; it has no senate, and its politics behind the scenes holds no senate vote
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L113).

### A machine of command

The word imperium (命令权) first meant command power, so an empire is first the range in which commands reach and are
carried out, and a territory only second (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L10-12). The Empire is a
bureaucratic empire and a state of people, and it keeps running whoever is missing from it, the emperor included
(`docs/setting.md` L96-97). History gives these cases: emperors made outside the capital, the run of emperors of the
third-century crisis, the Eastern Roman state outliving its overthrown emperors, and the Chinese dynasties that kept one
bureaucratic system (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L34-39). The Empire is not personified
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/删除核对-2026-11-03.md` L102).

The Empire governs its territory as administrative regions; it does not station a standing force in every region, and
how each region is patrolled, defended and garrisoned is decided for that region (`docs/setting.md` L98-99). Its present
court is not on this planet (`docs/setting.md` L42, L100).

The Empire's people are the people of the Empire, who fill all of its roles (`docs/setting.md` L101), and each of them
is either a combatant or a non-combatant (`docs/setting.md` L75-76). The security of important persons takes an elite
approach, the honor guard (`docs/setting.md` L77-78). The Empire's mechanoids are the Empire's machines, which generally
have plain strengths and weaknesses (`docs/setting.md` L102-103).

### Lineage and technology

The Empire's lineage is Rome's own alternative timeline and is meant to be realistic: large-scale hydropower production
that stays profitable over time brings investment, maintenance, the machining of parts, skilled people and the
organization of manufacture, which push heat engines, electricity and automation, and the Empire built machines first
and went to space afterwards (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L86-90). Its
real starting point is the machine-building of the Hellenistic age, Ctesibius and Hero, with the one "if" that it did
not stop at wonders and toys; each step needs a real demand and benefit, not symbolism, and no myth, old doctrine or
literal coincidence is used to attach the lineage, so a draft that used the Talos myth and Galen's pneuma was withdrawn
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L86-90). The Empire's machines and their
cores are its own research, not learned from elsewhere
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L95). The old empire once ran small-scale
trials of awakening psychic ability in people and abandoned them because input and return did not match, and the present
Empire does not treat psychic ability as mainstream
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L98).

The Empire's technological axis is a tradition of making machines, derived from the Empire's circumstances
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L1448, R-1103-1133). The ruling names those circumstances as hegemony rather than
competition among states, a long shortage of soldiers, an industry grown out of state engineering and legitimacy through
continuity; inside the design the tradition is provisionally called the automaton tradition (自动机传统), a name players do
not see (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L190, R-1103-1133).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: the same ruling keeps waterworks (水利) as the core of the Empire, with the aqueducts, channels and water
> features and a water network that may charge machines; this belonged to the hydraulics the maintainer deleted
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L190).

The Empire keeps no animals (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L693, R-1105-1737c): outside its cities there are no
pastures and no firewood woods (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L790, R-1105-1737c).

## Government and politics

The Emperor holds real central authority. The military group (军事集团), the civil service (文官体系) and the commerce
network (商贸网络) are interlocking, mutually dependent institutional forces, not three mutually exclusive camps; the old
groups may include loyalists, and newly risen forces are not by nature progressive or reliable
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L150). Preservation policy, legal procedure and the arrangement of
interests produce stability and rigidity together; the Empire as a whole is not written as incompetent and corrupt, and
contracts it has promised do not default at random (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L150). The commerce
side (商贸派) is a state-level system of industry and commerce, not private merchants (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md`
L1454; `Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L194, R-1103-2243a).

Politics behind the scenes shows only through limited records, the bounds of authorizations, real deployments,
stagnation and recovery: there is no senate voting, no courting of factions, no compulsory court dialogue and no office
management (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L152). The player is not a saviour set from the start;
importance comes from real contribution, and the ending does not force the player to replace the Emperor or take a
saviour's title (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L152).

The three groups do not appear before the player as persons or separate entities: they are not united within, and the
colony's leaning changes, so no representative or leader of a group visits, and no group has its own grades, reputation
or screen; leaning is felt only through changes in content (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L156). For the same
reason, pre-review documents graded separately by issuing body and a rank III visit by the upper layer of the body the
colony deals with most are not adopted (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L156).

The Empire shows itself to the player through its people, events and notices, and the relation works underneath
(`docs/imperium.md` L59-64, L101, L103). Text shows the Empire through what it does rather than telling players what it
is (`docs/imperium.md` L102). No number for the colony's standing with the Empire appears on any screen
(`docs/imperium.md` L68-69, L103), and a colony in the Empire is never asked to manage or understand the Empire's inner
politics (`docs/imperium.md` L59-60, L70-71, L104).

## What the Empire has given up from Royalty

The base game's Empire does not exist in this world (`docs/setting.md` L83). Imperium has retired the Royalty title
system for the player and the Empire: title, favor, permit and heir progression of player and Empire pawns is blocked by
runtime guards, and bestowing outcomes for player and Empire pawns stop before separate psylink rewards; the player's
honor, bestowal and tribute, noble requirements and Royalty research progression are retired; the Empire's title tags,
the title requirements of pawn kinds and the chances of generating titles are removed; and title and permit gates are
removed from the Empire's trader kinds (`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/royalty-retirement-v1.md` L9-15;
`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/royalty-text-display-mapping.md` L25). The base game's Royalty promotion is retired
(`Imperium-Knowledge/synced/IMPERIUM_STATE.md` L19). Titles are cancelled and the interface shows only rank I to V
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L82; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/待用/文本盘点-2026-10-27.md` L345). With the titles
gone, the base game's requirements for title bedrooms and throne rooms do not apply (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/NPC
机械.md` L12; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L284, R-1029-0010).

The 19 Royalty research projects are hidden and cannot be used: NobleApparel, RoyalApparel, Harp, Harpsichord, Piano,
JumpPack, CataphractArmor, BrainWiring, SpecializedLimbs, CompactWeaponry, VenomSynthesis, ArtificialMetabolism,
NeuralComputation, SkinHardening, HealingFactors, FleshShaping, MolecularAnalysis, CircadianInfluence and Gunlink
(`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/royalty-retirement-v1.md` L7-9). The jump pack, crown, coronet and stellic crown have no way to
be got or made, and the throne, grand throne, harp, harpsichord and piano cannot be built or made by the player, while
NPC furniture stays; the drape, brazier and darklight brazier, the military, prestige and Gunlink gear, the eltex and
psychic gear, psylinks, ordinary psycasts, neuroformers, psytrainers, anima and natural meditation, psychic sensitivity
and neural heat remain in the game (`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/royalty-retirement-v1.md` L17-25).

The Empire faction is labelled Empire (帝国) and its leader title Exarch (总督); the base game's fixed leader kind
Empire_Royal_Stellarch is labelled Exarch, and its delegation pawn Empire_Royal_Bestower is labelled Imperial Envoy
(帝国使节) (`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/royalty-text-display-mapping.md` L7-11). The Empire outside the player keeps its NPC
throne furniture, whose room is labelled Audience Hall (`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/royalty-text-display-mapping.md` L12).

## The Empire's people

### Officials and commanders

The officials the Empire sends are told apart by their posts, each recognized by one hat or one object in hand, and
their clothes are shared as far as possible (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L157). The persons, under provisional
names (拟名), are these (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L159-167):

| Person | Where they serve | Base-game person replaced |
|---|---|---|
| clerk (帝国吏员) | commission visitors, handovers, leasing, transit passengers, witnesses and other general business | Empire_Common_Lodger |
| commerce officer (商务官) or post officer (驿传官), possibly one kind | imperial goods visits and temporary markets; delivery and return of leases, handovers at landing pads, changes of ship | Empire_Common_Trader |
| inspector (考察官) | the promotion inspections for rank II and rank III | none |
| judge (法官), also arbiter | circuit court, arbitration | none |
| drill instructor (教官) | joint drill | none |
| commander (指挥官) | military musters, garrisons, transit troops; imperial strongpoints and raids | Empire_Fighter_Trooper and the other gun-carrying persons |
| envoy (使节) | the representative at joining; reads the edict at rank IV | Empire_Royal_Bestower |

Rank V is granted by the Emperor at the triumph in the capital; no court administrator is set
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L167). In English the persons carry plain names, clerk, commerce officer, post
officer, inspector, judge, drill instructor, commander and envoy, and in Chinese the names of their functions; the Greek
original names appear only in descriptions (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L172).

The commander is himself a mechanitor and wears command gear that the player can also make; what he leads is in
[military.md](military.md) (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L169). The base game's persons are not deleted
until their replacements are made; replacing the persons, the imperial face, follows right after rank II, and art goes
in the order machines, then the persons' clothing, then building parts
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L170). Pack carrying for imperial caravans is done by caravan machines,
and where the base game does not let machines carry for caravans, shuttle visits are used instead
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L170). Capture is allowed, with no death acidifier, and the only thing
worth capturing from imperial personnel is a commander's gear (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L171).

Colonists hold no imperial office: the Empire's systems belong to the whole colony, and colonists only wear its costume
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L55).

### How the people look

Among imperial officials purple follows rank, and gold is given only to the highest places
(`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L37). The machines' white is set apart from the landmark paint
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L176) (replaces one white shared by officials, machines and landmarks,
`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L37). Clothes have a white or light-grey ground, with purple only
on edges, embroidered patches and clasps; a body all in purple is the Emperor's alone, the same rule as the machines'
purple on the edges only (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L173). Civil and military officers wear the same
white, and military officers are told apart by the standard command helmet, the lamellar cuirass (札甲胸甲) and the
gold-hilted sword (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L176). How much purple an official wears follows rank,
and posts are not told apart by colour: clerks wear pure white with no patch; commerce officers, post officers and
inspectors wear a purple patch on the chest and are told apart by its pattern, their hats and their staffs; judges wear
a white toga with a purple border; envoys, at rank IV, wear a larger patch with gold thread
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L177-182). Gold belongs to the highest places and to flag and badge, as
gilt gold #c9a43c (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L183) (replaces, in the same line, gold as only the gold
thread from rank IV up, the gold thread of the skiadion hat (斯基亚迪昂帽) and the commander's gold-hilted sword). Red is not used, since red and gold are the style of the
base game's Royalty (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L184). The toga is open to colonists as formal dress
from rank III (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L174).

> landmark paint, and which of the two the clothing takes is undecided (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md`
> L176).

### Civilians

Civilians (平民), craftsmen, residents and other non-combatants, live only in the Empire's own settlements, produced
from the settlements' resident anchors; they do not go to the player's colony, so the Empire still sends no population
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L186). The Empire's small, medium and large settlements have residents,
and its military strongpoints (camps, small forts, watchtower forts, forward positions, checkpoints and the like) have
none (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L187). In the Roman design atlas the
small and medium settlements are a small fort and a camp, both military, and Roman small and medium settlements with
residents are not added for now, so at present only the large tier, the city, has residents
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L188). Civilians wear a classical cut, long tunic, cloak and head cloth,
in warm, low-saturation colours, linen beige, wool grey-white, ochre, olive green, grey-blue and brown, the warm ground
of the city (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L189). They carry a little of the imperial colours: some
civilians' main garment is linen white, one step darker than the officials' cream so that officials stay the brightest
white; head cloths, belts and other small pieces may be a pale, low-saturation purple, never the officials' deep purple
edging and patches; and they wear no gold and no red (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L190-193). As few
new items as possible are added: civilians wear the base game's clothes and tunics without purple edging, coloured when
the person is generated (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L194).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the kinds of imperial civilian. A new civilian pawn kind is needed and its scheduling is open; until
> then the resident anchors use existing persons (`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L37;
> `Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L186).

## Rank

### What rank is

Rank is the colony's formal political identity, not a Royalty personal title, technology points or an experience bar of
contribution; real contribution, formal rank and real bargaining influence are three related but different results;
completing tasks does not automatically grant any technology, and promotion does not automatically complete research
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L8). Rank is the grade at which the Empire internally sees the settlement, the
colony's place in the imperial machine (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L38). Ranks I to V are different identities
and rights, and the imperial insignia changes its look with rank to show how important the node is in the Empire's eyes
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L56). Promotion is the colony's position in the machine changing: each rank
connects more of the machine's functions to the colony, in the order relaying and transit, then imperial mechanoids,
then core technology and influence, then campaigns, then rank V, which is designed later
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L54-55).

| Rank | Political meaning | Direction of its permissions |
|---|---|---|
| I | joined the imperial political circle | voluntary cooperation, building for local reception, single technology techprints that come by department and rank; no early making of standard machines by the colony itself |
| II | trusted | the qualification for basic imperial research and the machine industry; the basic catalogue of standard machines |
| III | formally mature | standard imperial technology and medium manufacturing; influence; the archive office can query scheduled voyages within the planning window, with no guarantee that they arrive |
| IV | shapes the pattern | advanced imperial technology and heavy manufacturing; the campaign opens, commanded by the Emperor with the colony going with the army |
| V | a regional force | ultimate imperial technology and super-heavy manufacturing; non-war power and the privilege of command |

The table above is the source's table of ranks (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L24-30). As rank rises, task
content, requests and the techprint reward pools grow together, and there is no separate list of high permissions
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L109). A higher rank may keep legitimate low-specification business and lower
techprints it has not yet obtained, and the player is never made to stop at a low rank to collect low-level content
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L34). The length of rank I is calibrated to a four-to-five-year target and is
not a forced wait, an automatic promotion day or a graduation requirement; the player need not collect every techprint
or finish a fixed tutorial task to keep growing, and administrative facilities and the machine industry are ordinary
inputs that bring different abilities one after another, not an extra penalty for newcomers
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L32).

In narrative text, not as an interface label, the colony's standing is named in place of "rank" in visit letters,
promotion letters and the descriptions of orders and claims: rank I outpost (前哨), II settlement (聚落), III town (城镇),
IV city (城市) and V regional capital (首府) (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L84-92). The scale runs from a
small post just entering the imperial system to a regional capital, and never to provinces, great regions or other
districts that govern other places (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L94). A regional capital sits deeper in the
imperial system and is not independent; duke, ducal domain and other Latin-Western feudal titles are not used, nor
Despotate (despot means tyrant), Dominion (self-governing) or Exarchate (easily read as half independent)
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L96). The interface shows only rank I to V, and the title scheme of client,
province, prefecture, theme and metropolis is cancelled as a whole, because those titles were too large for one colony
and hard to recognize; the look of the imperial insignia, changing with rank, carries the recognition of standing, and
promotion letters describe what each rank brings (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L82).

The theme of each rank shows through five channels and is not written out: the insignia's look; the Empire's daily
activity at the settlement; the basic technology unlocked; more complex and larger commissioned tasks; and the
promotion ceremony the Empire holds at the settlement, except for rank IV to V, which is held at the triumph in the
capital (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L55).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the concrete resource formula of promotion, the exact permissions of ranks IV and V and the items the
> rank table does not list (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L34).

### Who looks at the colony

The Empire is huge and a small settlement has no pull on it, so every promotion is applied for by the player, and as
rank rises the one who looks at the colony changes (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L38-45):

| Promotion | Who looks | What they look at | How it is granted |
|---|---|---|---|
| I to II | the grassroots officials of each body | whether the colony has become a node of real help to the Empire and reliable | an inspector visits |
| II to III | the upper layer of each body | whether the help now carries more weight | an inspector visits; influence opens after it |
| III to IV | the Emperor | whether the colony has grown large enough to be a rising power that balances the Empire's inner forces | an envoy visits and reads the Emperor's edict |
| IV to V | the Emperor | redesigned with rank V | granted by the Emperor at the triumph in the imperial capital |

### Contribution

At present contribution is the only channel of promotion: it is computed internally in silver value and can come from
commissions, requests, military help, excavations and other ways (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L47-48). The
player sees how the Empire judges the colony in documents, and dealings with the Empire are not only at the archive
office terminal: visits, requests, excavations and commissions all happen on the map
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L49). Promotion asks the player to build nothing, city building being the player's
own reward, with the exception of the core imperial facilities, the archive office, the imperial insignia and the
landing pads; promotion from I to II needs an insignia and landing pads that qualify
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L50; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/帝国/帝国是什么.md` L4, L57). Special promotion is
designed later (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L51).

Contribution measures the scale of help, not the number of errands: the imperial archive records what the Empire really
gets from the colony, at the base game's market value (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L69). What counts is an
order, once, at the frozen valuation of its quote, the price the Empire set for that service by the base game's quest
valuation curve; goods delivered in requests, at the base game's market value; and military help, counted when imperial
personnel are on the map and a raid is repelled, whether or not they fight, at 50% of the repelled threat points
converted by the same curve (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L70). What does not count is the number of orders
or requests completed, the registration of techprints, which the Empire gives and which are no gate of promotion, and
ordinary sales to imperial buyers, which the Empire has already paid for (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L71).
A colony on the Empire's line may also hand in the document items it finds in ruins, before or after decoding them; once
handed in they are no longer the player's, and they count as contribution at market value on the same basis as other
contribution, so keeping or handing them in is the player's trade-off (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L149).

Contribution has one ledger only, not split by body, and work that several bodies do together is recorded once,
because a ledger split by body would be a hidden faction reputation (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L72;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/RankI-设计核对-2026-10-29.md` L64-68).

### Thresholds

The charter sets the same fixed thresholds for every node, and they do not change with the colony's wealth
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L74). Promotion from I to II needs contribution of the average frozen
valuation of rank I commissions times five, written as an adjustable constant and adjusted after measurement

### Promotion

Promotion runs from qualification, through the player's application by way of the archive office (档案署) and a real
visit, to the grant; there is no examination, the visit being the ceremony of the grant, no automatic promotion, and the
player may choose not to apply (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L67). When the conditions are met the Empire
sends someone, and when they are not the Empire says one sentence (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L168,
R-1029-2133). In the
mod the application is made from the archive office terminal's menu at rank I, and the terminal checks the bad record,
the facilities and the contribution in that order (`Imperium-Knowledge/synced/IMPERIUM_STATE.md` L45).

For ranks II and III the visitor is an inspector, who lands by shuttle at a landing pad and stays about one game day;
attacked during the visit, the inspector defends himself or retreats to the ship, and an interrupted visit is
rescheduled (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L67). For rank IV the visitor is an envoy who reads the Emperor's
edict, and rank V is granted by the Emperor at the triumph in the imperial capital (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md`
L67). An inspector's death is handled by the base game's faction relations and brings no further demotion
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L67); in Imperium the Empire replaces the base game's goodwill with relations of
its own, given in [relations.md](relations.md) (`docs/glossary.md` L25). The visit's initial values, listed for later
review, are a wait of 12 to 24 hours, a stay of one day, a new visit one to two days after a raid that interrupts it,
and an escort of two scythers and two lancers (2 只螳螂、2 只枪骑兵) (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L174, R-1104-1636;
`Imperium-Knowledge/synced/IMPERIUM_STATE.md` L46).

At the grant the visitor hands over the imperial codicil (委任敕书) of that rank, which replaces the technology chip and
opens the rank's systematic imperial research, and the insignia changes to that rank's look
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L103). There is one codicil item for each rank, named in the form "commission
codicil (rank 2)", and the base game's research prerequisite accepts that rank's item directly; the integrated research
of ranks III to V each takes its own rank's codicil as prerequisite (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L170,
R-1103-1759) (replaces, in part, the single codicil told apart by rank, as the bracket there states). In the mod the
four codicils, ranks II to V, are consumed on analysis at the research bench, cannot be traded and have no market value
(`Imperium-Knowledge/synced/IMPERIUM_STATE.md` L43). From rank II, a rank II codicil that has not been analysed and that
the player no longer has can be reissued from the terminal for 100 silver, an initial value listed for later review, the
landing pad being checked before the silver (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L174;
`Imperium-Knowledge/synced/IMPERIUM_STATE.md` L47).

When granting rank II the inspector also leaves one base-game mechlink beside the rank II codicil, meaning that the
Empire appoints a mechanitor; it comes once, at the grant of rank II, not again with a reissued codicil, and it is an
item that advances no technology tree (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L172, R-1103-2303a;

## Leaning

Leaning (倾向) is not explained in the game: it is felt only through changes in content, and a player who wants to know
the mechanism looks it up (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L115). The military, commerce and civil
administration departments share one leaning, not three mutually exclusive reputations
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L117). A department's count has two sources: each techprint, on its first
formal registration, counts once for its one main tag, and accepting an order counts for the order's leading body;
repeated manufacture, repeated registration and requests add nothing, and a techprint that came from one department's
contract need not have that department's main tag (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L117). The count is kept by
number of times and apart from the ledger of promotion (`Imperium-Knowledge/裁定归档/2026-11.md` L36).

Leaning abstracts the colony's direction of development: helping a department raises its leaning, and registering a
technology means the colony will use it to make and build, and so leans that way; registering more military techprints,
for instance, makes the Empire see the place more as a military fortress, and military activity and content grow
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L119). Leaning and promotion are two ledgers: leaning decides what the Empire
puts into the colony, the scale of contribution decides whether it can rise, and the registration of technology counts
for leaning and not for contribution (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L119). When a department's leaning is
relatively high, it opens core technologies that are normally closed, two per department at each of ranks III and IV and
none at rank V, fitting that rank's position and not limited to buildings or machines
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L119) (replaces the three per department per rank of the same day, as the
passage states).

No number of leaning is shown: it appears in text and in the player's own sense of it, as more requests from that
department, more of its related orders among the quotes, and the tone and wording of documents; claims are issued only
from the ruins batch on, and the issuing department affects only their wording and the kind of ruin
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L121). The changes are made large enough to be noticed, and whether players
notice them is measured after implementation (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L121).

Each source carries a weight: accepting an order counts 1, the first registration of a regular techprint 2.5 and of a
rare techprint 5; rare techprints are the departments' specialty techprints and core technologies, which appear only at
a leaning of 45% or more, while the six of rank I and the like are regular techprints
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L129). By the current formula a source matters most at the start and less
later, a regular techprint moving leaning about 4.6 percentage points at the start and about 0.9 at sixty records, so
the colony's early choices set its direction; leaning does not fall back with time, and balance rests on the fun of the
tasks themselves rather than on pressing early leaning down (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L129). In an order
done by several bodies, all the leaning counted at acceptance goes to the leading body and none to the collaborators,
for the same reason that contribution has one ledger (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L131) (replaces the
proportional split of 0.7 and 0.3 in `Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L31).

The formula is `P(i) = 0.20 + 0.40 × (c_i + 4) / (c_total + 12)`: each department starts at one third, and with one-way
accumulation one approaches 60% and the other two 20% each; 60% is a hard cap (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md`
L133-139; `Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L31). Leaning affects later legitimate quotes and the relative
chance of the visits it applies to; it does not rewrite published contracts or scheduled voyages, and it is not farmed by
counting buildings (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L139). The formula is implemented, with the wasp pack
counting toward civil administration (`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/code-status.md` L29;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/RankI-设计核对-2026-10-29.md` L74).

A department's specialty techprints and core technologies can be won only when its effective leaning is at least 45%,
which under the formula needs about 62.5% of the records, and when the colony holds the rank they belong to; being
eligible does not guarantee a win, and an authorization already registered is not withdrawn when leaning drops
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L141). While a department's effective leaning is at least 45%, each truly
successful contract of that department that brought no core technology counts one, and after three, its next successful
contract is sure to carry a core technology of that rank the colony does not yet have; the count pauses, and is not
cleared, while leaning dips below 45% (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L143). This guarantee is never shown:
no count, no hinting document, and the reward looks and reads exactly like an ordinary contract's, so the Empire does
not seem to give alms; it only smooths luck in the background (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L143).

The commerce leaning amplifies the floor of commerce, the loop of producing, selling to the Empire and expanding
production, since the essence of commerce is speculation and risk control and the factory is the capital for its floor
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L123). Its orders grow: requests for bulk supply and contract manufacture come
more often and larger, and long-term supply contracts delivered over several days appear
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L124). Its buyers grow: the budget of imperial goods visits rises with the
commerce leaning, so they can buy a whole batch (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L125). Its labour grows: lease
visits come more often, by an amount set by measurement, and the rank III guild mech station lets leased machines take no
bandwidth (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L126). Prices stay on the base game's basis, storing silver and
ordering on demand are not revived, and stronger raids brought by wealth are the base game's normal balance, which the
commerce mechanisms do not aim to reduce (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L127).

> [!NOTE]

## Influence

From rank III, contribution turns into influence (影响力) in proportion, about one typical rank III contract's valuation
for each cell, the value set by measurement, and promotion to rank III grants two cells
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L148). Influence does not come from one channel alone: once rank III opens it, its
channels are spread, the concrete channels being decided with the rank III design (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md`
L173, R-1103-2333a; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L786). Influence and the support it buys open from rank III
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L169, R-1029-2137).

Influence comes in ten discrete cells, which can be stored up to ten and are precious and hard to get; once influence
opens, hovering over an order shows a ghost of the cells it may give (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L149).
Exchange has no cooldown and is limited only by cells: expedited service, one cell, arranges one lease or caravan visit
at once; a supply drop, two cells, has a shuttle really bring a class of goods the player picks from the imperial
storage list, at the base game's valuation; and military aid, three cells, comes by a shuttle landing at a landing pad or
by troops marching in from the map edge (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L150). Fleet fire support is also
exchanged for influence, carried out by real imperial ships, and the Empire's general permits are merged into this
exchange with no separate permit system (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L150). Military aid has one make-up
only, imperial machines led by one commander, its scale rising with rank, and influence is not used to change leaning
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L151) (replaces influence being unable to push leaning past 60%,
`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L31). Influence is
precious enough to buy a good deal, and all support comes by real people and ships and carries traces of the Empire's
inner politics, not a button that drops things as the base game's permits do (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md`
L152). Money cannot buy political authority without limit, and influence adds no interface for political management
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L154).

## The campaign and rank V

Rank IV gives the chance to launch a campaign: the player applies, and the Emperor commands the war against the
Empire's foreign enemies only, with the Empire paying the resources; Roman allies handed over the right to open war
("the same friends and the same enemies as Rome"), so it is not the colony's own war
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L58). Campaigns can be launched many times and do not end with rank V
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L58). In a campaign the colony goes with the army, since one settlement cannot
command; command is a rare privilege of rank V, open once after a cooldown and not the main body of rank V
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L59). Techprints, campaign and triumph are three things: rank IV techprints
come from campaigns; the triumph is held after a campaign won, and the Emperor grants rank V there once only, later
victories giving only techprints and spoils (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L60). The campaign is the Empire
carrying real national military force and logistics to show its scale, and the player carries out a clear and important
goal, not an enlarged ordinary excavation (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L160).

The three groups support a campaign out of their own interests, the military group for merit, the civil service to
extend its power and the commerce network for a war dividend, and how much each supports depends on how the colony's
actions benefit it (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L62). The opponent is only an outside hostile faction, and
there is no imperial civil war (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L62). The direction for rank IV is that military
roads are built for the campaign, for moving troops and supplies; the concrete play is decided with the rank IV design
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L63).

Rank V is the endgame, designed later, and the line of war closes with a triumph; its signature is non-war power, with
numbers at most at the level of the VE series of mods and abilities that are unprecedented
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L65). The Empire line's endgame is designed last
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L65; `Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L143) (the independent line that passage
sets beside it is deleted, replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L143 in part).

State military force, strategic privileges and special authorizations may carry clear political conditions; whether
independent trade, ruins and technology content have other ways in is approved item by item, and having independent
content does not mean regular permission can be bypassed (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L158).

Joining the Empire has a cost: factions hostile to the Empire grow more hostile to the colony as its rank rises, and
the presence of imperial personnel makes raids come more often (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L164). There
are no taxes and no compulsory duties, and the duty taken on at joining is, in play, only receiving imperial transit
(`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L164). The concrete changes of goodwill and frequency of raids follow the base
game's faction relations and raid mechanics at implementation, as the base game's expansions give their costs side
effects that lead to later events (`Imperium-Knowledge/03_Rank与政治权限.md` L164).
