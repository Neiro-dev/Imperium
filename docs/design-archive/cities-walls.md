# City walls and gates

This section covers the walls of Imperium's settlements: when the Roman city wall is laid and what it protects, how it
is built from defence fronts and curtain walls, how it borrows rock and water, its towers, its gates and water gates,
its section, the moat and cleared strip outside it, what walls, towers and gates are made of, the walls of the Roman
camp tiers, and the walls and boundaries of medieval, modern and tribal settlements. Earlier wall schemes that newer
settings replaced appear only as Deleted notes. It meets `cities.md` (the order in which a city is made, the period table
and the old wall line after a city expands), `cities-siting.md` (terrain and where a city stands), `cities-inside.md`
(the ring road, the palace quarter, streets and their paving), `cities-outside.md` (roads outside the gates, fields,
banks, bridges and the river), `cities-forms.md` (city files, templates and the city grammar) and `art.md` (the imperial
colours).

## When the wall is laid and what it protects

### Laid last, around the city

The city wall (城墙) is the last thing laid in a Roman city: the interior is laid first and the wall wraps the city
already laid out, with no preset shape (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L12; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L18-19;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-城建版本盘点-2026-11-10.md` L178). A wall drawn first around an empty site gives at most a tidy
fortress, so whole cities are judged and not walls alone (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-城建版本盘点-2026-11-10.md` L20;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L723).

A city that follows a template takes the wall line, gate positions and tower positions from the Anno (纪元) template and
adds only two things: where the wall head meets the river it is sealed, by deep water or by extending the wall along the
river, and every gate is passable; firing positions are left to the ICF defence-effect acceptance
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L12-13; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L939;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/进度-拷打入账二.md` L22; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·墙.md` L108).

### What is protected and what is saved

The wall's path is derived from what it protects and how it saves most: what it protects is the urban area, and the
saving lies in two places, taking the shortest line that protects the already built city without enclosing empty land,
and borrowing terrain only when counting shows that it saves wall and defenders (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L17;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L786; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L844;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-原意走样二.md` L44; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/城建错题转拦截-2026-11-05.md` L29;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·墙.md` L107) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778, where "cheap"
applied only to borrowing terrain). The city's outline grows from its core and is not reshaped to save wall; the
intended reading is that the wall's course shows which part of the city it protects and which terrain it borrows
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L17). The reference comparison gives, as its principle, the shortest wall that
encloses the core and its contents with no projecting convex corner; near-round is a result, not a template, and
departures come only from terrain or expansion along a route
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L9), and the comparison adds that the later-period
wall is tighter, its length matched to the garrison (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md`
L35).

The urban-area protection envelope fixes only the protected range, the wall's inner setback, the limit of expansion and
acceptance; the outer outline of the building groups or of the blocks is not taken as the wall
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L18; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/红线表.md` L25;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/城建错题转拦截-2026-11-05.md` L32). The wall encloses only the part that is already city and does not
circle empty land or an empty opposite bank, and a wall around empty land is not judged on its own
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L85; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/红线表.md` L26;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/城建错题转拦截-2026-11-05.md` L36).

### One wall and a moat

The wall is a single wall with a moat, with no added thickness and no added short walls; the meaning of real walls is
learned, not their form, and no real city's wall belt or outline is copied (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L23;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/城建错题转拦截-2026-11-05.md` L13; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L672). Reference images
and real cities are read for why a wall runs as it does (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md` L144). A full
multi-layer defence is left to a later capital, when the map area is larger, and the capital's Theodosian double wall is
not generated or tested before the capital is made (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L240;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L331).

### The outline and the wall

The city's outline (圈) fixes the city's extent and is its upper bound; the city, its streets, walls and towers go
outside the outline by 0 cells, and the wall follows the city and does not trace the outline
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L829; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L129). The Roman outline folds
regularly: its edges run only horizontal, vertical and at 45° in straight runs, folding where terrain and direction
change, and on flat land it does not grow round (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L129;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L818; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L461). Its sample is the right image of
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/Code-第二段轮廓-校准与核心/校准-R1-第四轮.png`, and the medieval outline is the fine outline of the left
image (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L129). The outline's area target is 1.5 times the city area as a trial
value, taking the largest multiple that fits the map without relaxing the distance from the map edge or the core's
distance from water (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L829; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L838). The
outline turns at nodes (where it crosses rivers and roads and at the ends of borrowed rock or water edges), the other
stretches are straightened, and a straight run over the limit folds only where direction changes; each fold faces a
river, road or rock, and on flat land with few nodes this method falls back to a near-octagon, which another method is
being tried to fix (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L840).

The city places no rock, pond or mound to give the wall long straight stretches and does not change the outline for them;
on a plain the wall closes around the whole city, its straight runs divided by tower spacing and gates and turning at the
outline's own turns and at features the map has (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L830-832). The order of the
generation's constraints from hard to soft is: the city does not leave the outline; the outline is not changed and no
features are made; the city fills the wall and the wall follows the city; the shape is decided by the city and every
fold has a source; Roman feeling; counting (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L833).

## Defence fronts and the curtain wall

### Defence fronts

The wall is assembled from several defence fronts (防御正面), each with its own direction and reason; a front's direction
and position are set by the overall extent of the urban area, the roads and gates leading out, the planned axes, the
reserved space and continuous large terrain such as a rock face, a river bank or a narrow pass
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L29; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L735;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·做法与顺序.md` L58). Wall planning runs "defence front, curtain wall, node"
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·墙.md` L25; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·做法与顺序.md` L128). A front decides
which sides are defended, what each faces (approach road, river, rock) and where the gates open
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L35). The wall does not use a convex hull
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L56; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/城建错题转拦截-2026-11-05.md` L66;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-城建版本盘点-2026-11-10.md` L18).

### Curtain walls and the tower chain

By the curtain-wall method (幕墙法), each front is joined from several straight curtain walls (幕墙) that turn at towers;
several staggered blocks are protected by one curtain wall of one direction, not by a projecting corner for each block
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L30; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L774). Between the curtain wall and the
blocks only the ring road (环城路) remains; extra land is filled by infill and not left as garden, and the large-piece belt
is dealt with in 罗马城-城内 five (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L30; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L802). The
intended reading is that the curtain turns on towers following terrain, a large change of direction is where two fronts
meet, and the city reads as several continuous lines of defence (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L30).

The wall is a tower chain (塔链) in the manner of Nova Roma: the city's interior keeps to the grid while the wall does not,
and the wall is not rigidly straightened; each front places towers along a guide line at the tower spacing and then joins
neighbouring towers with straight curtain walls (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L34-35;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L737; `docs/glossary.md` L212). A curtain does not bend in the middle and turns only at
towers; every tower may turn through a small or large angle following terrain and the guide line, and the tower where the
turn is large marks the meeting of two fronts and holds one big tower (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L36;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-原意走样二.md` L43) (replaces
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L774, where towers inside a front turned only by small angles). The walls on the two
sides of a tower may be offset and need not meet at one point; the tower absorbs the seam
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L47). Between two towers is one straight wall, with no cell-by-cell arc and no small bend
without a tower (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L48). The wall bends often and by small angles at its towers, with no
straight wall of a hundred cells and no large square-box corner; it stops at cliffs and coasts, where rock and cliff go on
as the screen, and inside it the city lies close against the wall with open land outside
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/城/环世界自然生长城镇-2026-11-05.md` L46-49). One city uses one way of writing, the river side and the land side
alike (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L49).

Each curtain takes the nearest of eight directions: horizontal, vertical and 45°, each in both senses; the rounding error
of laying it on cells is absorbed by the tower (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L37; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L824;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L660). On the grid the wall is then clean straight lines and neat steps, not long-and-short saw
teeth (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L824) (replaces the 16-direction set with 2:1 and 1:2 slopes,
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L735-736).

### The guide line and its bends

The guide line (引导线) follows visible things: a river bank, a rock edge, the edge of marsh or soft ground, a pond, and the
city's extent counted by district, by block and not by single building; on flat ground where only the city's extent
remains, it follows the rough course of the city's outer edge, with only the ring road against the wall; gates and
approach roads are fixed points (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L41) (replaces the strongly smoothed guide line,
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L737). Every bend faces something visible in RimWorld: a gate, the main street, a rock
edge, a water edge, a terrace, the palace or harbour quarter or another large block, and a bend facing nothing is not made
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L42; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/审查清单-图面.md` L28-32). No bend is made to add edges,
to shorten wall segments or to accommodate a single building (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L43).

Every tower where the wall turns records its reason, which is one of rock (岩), water (水), marsh (池沼), gate (门), approach
road (来路), block edge (街区边) or large piece (大件); the thing named lies within 20 cells of the tower, and a large piece is
a composite piece or any piece of 300 cells or more (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L64-68;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/kit/城建数据格式-草案.md` L132-141; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/城骨架格式-v1.md` L78). A
concave corner, turning toward the inside of the city, is explained only by rock, water, marsh or a large piece, never
by a gate, an approach road or a single block edge, and the towers on its two sides are no more than 60 cells apart
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L69). Wherever a curtain turns, by one degree or more, there is a
tower (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L63).

### Fitting terrain and the outline of the wall

Fitting terrain takes three steps: first a whole run is moved sideways, then a whole run is turned, and only last is it
split into several long curtains with corners; banks and rock are not traced cell by cell
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L53; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/check-覆盖表.md` L410-413). The shape is
not set by a composite score: construction rules and hard constraints choose among legal plans and report failure if
none is legal, and a small change inside the city does not change the whole wall drastically
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L54). The wall's outline does not take a very regular symmetric shape of the crude
community kind; derived from planning conditions it may be near-rectangular, pentagonal and the like
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L55; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L122;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L130). The outer outline shows no copied symmetry, is not assembled from
rectangles, is not a preset shape such as a square, circle or regular polygon, and every strip, bulge or annex outside
the main body has a reason (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/审查清单-图面.md` L45-47). The brief for the outline
three-versions trial (任务书-外形三版-乙) asks to keep the gate on a front with the wall in one line on either side, and not to
want a square city, four big cut corners, a turn forced in only to look irregular, or a wall that follows block edges in
and out (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/派活/任务书-外形三版-乙.md` L7). Frequent towers mean the wall runs wrongly, and the wall's
direction is not changed to seat a tower (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/check-覆盖表.md` L410-413).

### Corners

The number of corners comes only from where defence fronts meet and is not cut to make a shape, so flat ground does not
get a corner-cut octagon (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L64; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L654;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·墙.md` L99). Every land-side convex corner turns in two steps on a big tower, with an
obtuse convex angle and no right angle, so a corner is not attacked from two sides and its tower covers both flanks
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L65; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L781;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/原意走样排查-2026-11-06.md` L15; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-原意走样二.md` L20) (the cut
corners of `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L762 were replaced by R-1103-1338, and the right-angled corners without
a cause of `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L774 by R-1103-2238). A corner with one side against water or a cliff
may be a right angle, because only one side is exposed (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L66). In the check, at a
land-side convex corner an interior angle of 90° or less is an error; a corner is passed when a stretch of its sides
lies against water within the 5-cell bank band or against rock within 12 cells (a trial value), a face counting as
against water or rock when at least 50% (a trial value) of its points do, judged by distance and not by whether a gate
opens on it (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L82).

## Borrowing terrain

### Rock

Only terrain the map already has is borrowed, namely rock, soft ground and water; no lake or bank is added for the city
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L72; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L36). Small
rises do not show on the map and are not borrowed or joined to the wall line (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L802; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/罗马城·墙.md` L102). A rock body serves as wall and is neither built over nor enclosed
into the city; rock right outside the wall is taken into the wall line or held by a small fort, not left to the enemy as
cover (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L73; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙外.md` L51;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L15;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/审查-城建版本盘点-2026-11-10.md` L16). At both ends of an open stretch (开口段) where it meets
hills, the wall end is set into the rock at least 2 cells (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L74). Natural barriers and
the wall close together, and no opening is left for the attacker (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/地形与选址.md` L116;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L75).

### Wall stretch kinds

### The water side

On the water side the river reach that serves as the wall's face is shaped into several long straight banks and the wall
follows the bank, not one long straight line to the end; a bank band (岸带) is kept between wall and river, with an upper
limit, so that no wide beach is left for an enemy to land and deploy (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L84;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L39). Where the wall follows water (one bank of a
river, or the sea, or a medium or small city with no district on the far bank) the bank band is 5 cells and may be wider
at bends (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L84; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L762;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L772).

### Crossing a river

A wall that spans both banks meets the river at just two places, where it enters and leaves the city, each with one water
gate whose passage is deep water, and the wall closes at each bank with a tower

far bank (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L86). No large cut and fill is made just to keep the wall straight
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L87).

### The palace quarter and large pieces

The palace quarter (宫区) or citadel juts out only where terrain lets it be held at no extra cost, and the palace terraces
may push locally out beyond the wall (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L79). On flat ground large pieces are taken inside the
city, except the palace terraces, and the wall makes no inward notch for an empty corner
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L80). The palace quarter has no wall of its own: only the palace proper (gate tower,
colonnaded courtyard, main body) has a courtyard wall, and defence belongs to the city wall

## Towers

### Where towers stand

Each corner has one big tower, not a string of towers, and along the curtain towers are added by the danger of the face
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L93; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L772). Needing frequent towers shows that
the wall has gone wrong; the wall's course is not changed to fit a tower, and helper vertices that come from laying the
wall on cells do not become corner towers (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L104-105).

### Tower spacing

Tower spacing is measured between neighbouring nodes; corner towers and gate groups are nodes and no tower is added
beside them, and the two towers of a gate group count as one node and are not measured between, each being measured to
the next tower on its other side at the lower limit (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L97;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L778; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L59;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L62; `docs/glossary.md` L213). The lower limit is 24 cells and the
upper 60, and a straight run over 60 cells has a tower (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L98;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L51). Between the limits the spacing follows the
danger of the face: about 26 facing an approach road, about 32 on an open flat face, about 45 backed by rock and about
55 backed by water, all trial values (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L99; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L772; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L55). Towers therefore gather on the land faces where
attackers come and are sparse on the water side, so the defended direction reads at a glance
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L99). Sparse spacing on the water side presupposes a straight bank: a curved bank is
first reshaped or its band widened, and no towers are added (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L100).

> **Deleted**: the earlier tower lines, mid-wall towers of 9 and corner and gate towers of 11 cells at a spacing of 24-30

### Tower form

Towers are always octagonal: 7 cells in the middle of a run, 9 cells at corners and side gates and 11 cells at the main
gate, with the cut corner following the size, 1 cell on a 7-cell tower, 2 on a 9-cell and 3 on an 11-cell
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L109; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/draft.md` L36). In RimWorld a tower
reads as one only as a big octagonal tower, so towers are not made denser and their spacing follows the spacing rule
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/城/环世界自然生长城镇-2026-11-05.md` L46-49). A 7-cell octagon with a 2-cell cut would form a
stepped cross (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L158). A tower juts outward with its inner face flush with the
wall, and its front, cut corners and both sides have loopholes, the sides close against the wall for flanking fire
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L110). An outward corner tower has its centre on or beyond the outer wall line, at
least half its body outside the wall, and its side loopholes cover the wall foot (set by the design session, based on
R-1027-1619) (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L111). The number of loopholes is not fixed and the face toward the
city has none (the city-facing clause set by the design session, based on R-1027-1540a)
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L112; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L247). Towers and gatehouses carry no
gun platform or turret (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L110), and the ballistae in the CBF source templates are
removed (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L248).

A tower is a room with a door to the shooting gallery or into the city; it holds no furniture, only one floor lamp,
leaving room for the defenders (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L113). When the walls on its two sides are offset,
the part of a tower reaching into the city is passed on its inner side by the ring road and is not cut
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L114). Gate passage width and the side lengths of gatehouses and towers are odd cell
counts (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L155).

The 11-cell tower's centre is no longer reserved for a heavy ballista platform; the platform, a curved-fire weapon like
a mortar, is a decided design direction whose implementation time is not set, to be added as a template once designed
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L249; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L331;
`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L41).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the heavy ballista platform for the great Roman towers is to be designed and its time is unset
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L41).

## Gates

### Gates follow roads

Gates follow roads: one gate per approach road (来路), and roads to fields and estates may add a wicket gate (便门), with no
pasture road gate; a gate in a planned defence front is fixed together with the roads leading out
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L120; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/城建错题转拦截-2026-11-05.md` L43). The comparison
with classical city planning lists as an open item (待定 D) that the number of gates follows wall length and approach
roads, and a large city may exceed four and may add wicket gates
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L42;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L51). Gates and towers are not pinned to hand-laid
coordinates: the skeleton records relations, not coordinates (R-1030-1406②), and a gate is set by its approach road
(R-1102-2310); the error-log entry that records this lesson is only proposed to be kept (建议留、记下)
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/城建错题转拦截-2026-11-05.md` L67). Where the map has no world road, the approach is the edge
on which the main gate chosen in stage ② lies (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L656).

In a type A city with two land gates, the gate on the approach road of higher world-road grade is the main gate, and at
equal grade the one farther from the palace quarter (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L121;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L764; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L80). A comparison with
classical sources supports (支持，补) a main gate facing the approach road with the gate segment perpendicular to the
street, and adds that a gate is not put in a low place prone to flooding
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L41). The last stretch of road before a gate runs
horizontal or vertical, squarely facing the gate passage (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L128) (the same comparison
row adds, as a supplement, that the last stretch of road outside the gate runs close along the wall with the wall on the
visitor's right, `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/城市建筑学对照-罗马城理念-2026-10-30.md` L41).

The wall's shape is derived from its gates and roads; Roman flavour comes from gates and streets, not from adjusting the
wall further (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L122). A gate sits in the middle of a wall segment and is not shifted
for artistic expression (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L123). If a gate is placed wrongly the gate moves, and the
wall is not pulled out into a spike to reach it (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L124;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/整理/城建错题转拦截-2026-11-05.md` L31).

### Gatehouse and gate group

A land gate is a gate group (门组): a group of buildings with twin towers, a square gatehouse and paving outside the gate,
and the approach road widens before it (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L60; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L755; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L59). The gatehouse (门楼) is square and sits astride the wall,
and gatehouse and passage run horizontal or vertical; the gate meets its road by the passage and the stretch before it,
not by the wall segment's direction, also on a 45° segment (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L128;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L718; `docs/glossary.md` L183).

The main gate is just twin towers flanking the gate, with no barbican (瓮城) and no gate court
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L129; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L638). Main gate, side gates and the
water-outlet gate are all twin towers flanking the gate: the two gate towers are of one size, symmetric about the passage,
with their outer edges on one line parallel to the wall (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L130).

The gatehouse is modelled on CBF's `Gatehouse_A`, with a door at each end of the passage; it has no guard room or other
lived-in room, its passage joins the street directly, and the defenders live in barracks
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L131; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L419). The main city gate is a
3-cell-wide `RB_LargeOrnateDoor` (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L132).

### River gates and the ground before a gate

At a river gate (河边的门), a bridge gate or the palace quarter's side gate, going out leads onto the quay bank band and not
into water, and the bridge faces the bridge gate's passage (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L136;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L718; `docs/glossary.md` L197). Outside a city gate a widened open space is left,
not against water or rock, its depth set with the gate group (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L137).

### Water gates

Water gates (水门) let the river into and out of the city: where the wall spans the river it meets it at the entry and the
exit, each with one water gate (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L85;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/city/说明/check.md` L79; `docs/glossary.md` L215), and a city on sea or lake adds a
harbour water gate (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L147). Water gates sit only on straight runs with a gate-side
tower on each side, and a water gate is no reason for the wall to bend (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L147;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/审查清单-图面.md` L30). The outlet water gate is downstream, in the middle of its stretch of
wall, away from the bridge gate and corner towers (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L148). A water gate's passage is
deep water that people cannot cross, so it lets no one walk into the city (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L85;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L720; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L1425). A harbour water gate opens once the
sea or lake wall totals at least 30 cells, with a mouth 7 wide (6-9) between twin towers
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L149).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: vanilla deep water cannot be crossed, which the water gate's closure rests on, to be checked against
> `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L720).

> [!NOTE]

## Section and construction

### The three-cell section

The wall section is 3 cells on one level: an outer firing wall, the shooting gallery (射击廊) and an inner wall
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L156; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L802). The outer wall alternates merlon
and loophole at 2:1, the loophole being an impassable wall segment that can be fired across, and every Roman grade of

## What walls, towers and gates are made of

### Stone

The city wall, towers and gatehouse are of one stone, single-colour limestone (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-墙.md` L173;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L575; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/roman-cards/罗马规格卡总表.md` L94;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成第2轮重做-第一段批准与第二段补充.md` L325). Light stone rests on the Theodosian, Aurelian,
Nicaea and Thessalonica walls being light stone or brick, while a dark grey stone wall is the look of northern Europe
and medieval castles (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L569; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/roman-cards/罗马规格卡总表.md`
L80). The Burgus tower main house A22 is wholly limestone (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/roman-cards/罗马规格卡总表.md` L80;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/roman-cards/罗马规格卡总表.md` L94).

> [!NOTE]

## The Roman camp tiers

The camp tiers, Castellum, Fortlet and Burgus, follow the City's logic at smaller size and with fewer layers: one main
wall with a moat and cleared strip, and no gate court or upper city (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L573;

A camp wall changes direction only at a street end (a gate) and at corners, and is otherwise straight; a corner is a
short 45° piece between two open straights with the corner tower at its middle and a leg a quarter of the shorter
adjacent straight (±4 cells), and the wall heads join mountain or water directly
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L188-189; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成具象规格-现行版.md`

Camps have no ring road and their buildings may touch the wall; the moat is 3 wide and the cleared strip at least 5

## Medieval walls

Wall logic is Imperium's own for both Roman and medieval walls, and the components were made as hand-made templates for
each (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L187-202). CBF's smoothing into long straight runs and its tower count by
perimeter are replaced by the defence-front ruling (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L187). The medieval wall grows: it is
wrapped last around the grown settlement and is rounded overall, whereas the Roman wall stops where it meets terrain
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/instructions/生成第2轮重做-第一段批准与第二段补充.md` L374; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L188-189).
It follows coast, riverbank and rock edge, walking along edges, may cross a river with bridges, lets the built area hug the
water, and uses a mountain as wall (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L425). Only the Large tier has two circuits, and its
outer circuit is a whole closed one (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L198; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L331).

## Boundaries of other settlements

Each open segment of a settlement boundary takes one form: a solid wall, a closing stockade across the opening only,
fire coverage from control points, or no outer circuit with the boundary on the yards
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L124-145). By default Roman and medieval settlements have a solid wall;
modern ones fire coverage; a tribal ring village or longhouse camp a solid wooden palisade with bastions at intervals; a
cliff village or riverside long-row camp a closing stockade; and a family-courtyard village no outer circuit
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L124-145). A tribal ring village's palisade is a circle or ellipse of
logs, not smoothed into straights (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L188-189). Tribal bastions stand at
intervals, and modern control points go first at checkpoints, then at the outer corners of the fortified core, then at
outer corners up to a cap, the checkpoint barricades set in an S-bend with sandbag gun positions
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L149-154). A tribal ring village has 1-2 staggered entrances and, where
it lies by water, another small gate, and a modern settlement one checkpoint per road into it
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L215-217). Nested inner boundaries (内圈) also include the fortified-core
yard of a modern settlement, with sandbags or barricades and gun positions at four corners, and the chief's yard or the
family yards of a tribal one (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L221-226).
