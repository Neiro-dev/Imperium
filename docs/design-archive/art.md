# Appearance and art

This section covers how Imperium looks and how its art is made: the art direction, the four imperial colours and what
each stands for, the emblem and the flags, the look of the imperial mechanoids and of the old empire's guardians, the
placeholder clothing, the mod's own art, borrowed pieces and components, who makes the art and in what order, and how
art is checked. It meets `mechanoids.md` (what each mechanoid is and does), `empire.md` (how the Empire's people dress
and how the imperial insignia changes with rank), `cities.md` (the references a city learns from and its eras),
`cities-inside.md` and `cities-walls.md` (the city's colours in use and the materials of streets, buildings, walls and
gates), `cities-outside.md` (graves outside the gate), `ruins-and-technology.md` (the ruins the guardians keep) and
`text.md` (in-game wording).

## Art direction

### Classical form, modern material

Costume and objects keep their classical forms and in the interstellar age only change their material to modern
materials; the visual reference is the "classical outline plus high-tech material" of the Argol (阿戈尔) faction of
Arknights (《明日方舟》), taken as a direction for art only, with none of its designs, signs, names or assets copied
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L113; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/流程/借用与第三方.md` L108). The weapons
follow the same principle, a classical shape with industrial power (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L113).
From the Argol faction the principles are borrowed, not specific designs: a noble, restrained and distant temperament,
set apart from the showy noble style of the base game's Royalty; the white-purple identity kept, with a smooth,
liquid-like treatment of material, white marble with purple and liquid lustre; and no copying of its specific shapes,
signs and names and none of its biohorror (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L115, L117, L119-120).

Imperial buildings, such as the archive office (档案署), the imperial insignia (帝国徽记) and the landing pads, carry
water as their visual signature, in troughs, water ripples and fluid pipe forms; machines do not
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L116; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L28).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the cited lines give imperial buildings their water signature and give water in the look the meaning
> of the sign of a working-fluid civilisation, and do not say whether the water signature of the buildings fell with the
> hydraulics (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L116, L177).

### Choices taken from the Romans

For a visual choice, such as an emblem, a flag, a colour or a component, how the Romans themselves did it is looked at
first and the reasonable practice taken, and only then is something invented
(`Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L166). The community already reads white-grey walls, mosaic floors and
columns as Roman through VFE Classical, and Imperium borrows these learned signs; what sets it apart is a true Roman
heritage running through the whole line: its high technology is Rome at its peak, what an unbroken Roman civilisation
would have become, not a science-fiction skin pasted onto Rome, and the heritage runs from ancient Rome to medieval Rome,
to 1453 (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L571). VFE Classical is ancient Rome's clothing, while Imperium is
heir to the whole line (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L571).

### Ancient Rome and New Rome

Ancient Rome (古罗马) and New Rome (新罗马) divide only the skin and the contents of cities and objects: ancient Rome is
the old empire and the ruins, with temples and wayside tombs in the ancient Roman style; New Rome is the present Empire
and the living cities, with churches and cemeteries inside the city (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md`
L13). A living city also has a cemetery outside its gate, and the base game's graves and sarcophagi take New Roman
textures (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-做法与顺序.md` L13). Imperial machines take the New Roman form as well, as
the section on them below says (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L27).

### Crafted like a DLC

The main audience of a Roman city is its attackers and the commission sites, since the base game's friendly
settlements cannot be entered, and players may seldom see these designs; they are still crafted with the design
philosophy of a DLC (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L372).

## The imperial colours

### Four values

The Empire has four colours (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L177). White, purple and aqua-cyan take the base game's
painting colours from Core's ColorDefs: white is Structure_Cream (195,192,176), purple Structure_Purple (99,50,119) and
aqua-cyan Structure_MossBright (78,130,131) (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L178). Gold has a single value,
gilt (鎏金) #c9a43c, which replaced Structure_Mustard (163,131,49)
(`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L179; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L603) (replaces
`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L178, in its gold).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: a palette taken from a picture of an Eastern Roman emperor's purple robe with gold, ivory white
> (200,186,170), imperial purple (96,50,104), old gold (172,130,78) and aqua-cyan (82,150,158)
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L176-177); the base game's named painting colours took its place
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L178).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: Structure_Cream (195,192,176), Structure_Purple (99,50,119), Structure_Mustard (163,131,49) and
> Structure_MossBright (78,130,131) are painting colours in Core's ColorDefs, to be checked against `Data/`
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L178).

### What each colour stands for

Each colour has one job (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L177). White is what the Empire makes: the walls of
landmarks, the machine shell and the bodies of officials' garments (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L177). Purple
is power (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L177); since purple dye is no longer scarce in this Rome, it also marks
identity on a small scale and is not only the sign of the supreme (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L215). Gold is the
sacred and the highest, the Empire's mark (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L177; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
L223). Warm colours belong to the common people, whose natural sandstone houses make warm blocks in which the white
public buildings float (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L575; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L214). The old
empire's guardians in the ruins are the base game's old olive with a pale red light (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
L214). Accents use only purple and gold, never red, since red and gold is the base game Royalty's style
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L365; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L215). How the colours fall on a city's
streets, buildings, walls and gates, and the materials that carry them, are in `cities-inside.md` and `cities-walls.md`.

### Gold

Gold is used on the highest places, the landmark facades, the city gates, rank IV and above and the Emperor, and on flags
and emblems, all with the one value (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L179;
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L603) (replaces gold for the highest places only,
`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L176). Machine shells carry no gold
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L223; `Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L179).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: ornament such as ornate walls first uses ReBuild's own textures and later the mod's own art; a golden
> Vitruvian wave pattern was raised as a candidate, to be decided when the art is made
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L177).

### Purple

Purple stays on bodies, the clavus of machines and people, and in the city on the porphyry-type purple stone of
landmarks; it is not put on doors, dwellings or flags, and no base-game carpet dyed purple is used
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L223; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L232; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md`
L659) (replaces purple on ornate walls, flags and carpets, and the purple door, in `Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md`
L178; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L214; `Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L363, L569, L575). The purple carpet
down the axis of the governor's ceremonial hall is to be an own texture, as `cities-inside.md` describes; until that
texture exists the generator places no purple and the hall takes limestone brick painted ivory white
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L659). The Rank I design check lists the landing pad's white-and-purple platform
image beside the 15:14 note that purple stays only on bodies, with porphyry-type purple stone for landmarks, and beside
the Art Language's line 63, the same (the user's ruling 13) (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/RankI-设计核对-2026-10-29.md` L215,
L222); the line it cites is `Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L47.

### Aqua-cyan

Aqua-cyan belongs to the water-feature parts of the city, any glow being only one lighter step at very low brightness
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L233). Its use on imperial aqueduct parts and on machines belonged to the
hydraulics, as the Deleted note under Art direction says (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/城/罗马城-城内.md` L233;
`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L177).

### Machines in a warm city

White machines with their purple walking in the warm city stand out for order, and the city's landmark white and gold
receive them, so that they read as coming out of the imperial core, not as an occupying army
(`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L379-380). The machine white and the landmark paint are different values
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L221; `Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L36) (replaces
`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L381; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L43).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: once the machine white and the landmark paint were split, which value the ground of imperial clothing
> takes was left undecided (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L176).

## Emblem and flags

The imperial emblem is a gilded disc after the phalera: in pixels a gold circle with its centre left empty; no eagle and
no cross flag are used, and a version with a cross is kept only as a candidate (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
L223; `Imperium-Knowledge/裁定归档/2026-11.md` L24; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/RankI-设计核对-2026-10-29.md` L211)
(replaces the gold cross banner and the eagle badge of `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L221). It stands on pole
tops, on the imperial insignia building and on the ornate walls of landmarks (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
L223).

Flags differ by type: rank II has the vexillum, a square cloth hanging with gold tassels; rank III and up have the
bandon, a square flag with two or three streamers whose tips are gilded and whose number is graded; every pole is
topped by the disc; the cloth is the team colour, ivory by default, and can change from team to team
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L223). The flag is one of the things
that tell rank II machines from rank III (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L283).

## Imperial mechanoids

### Who shapes the look

The look of imperial machines and their equipment is set by the maintainer and the painter; the design session does
only content, role, play, numbers, names, manufacture and rebuild, and adds no Roman flavour of its own
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L10; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L419-420). The art limits
already set, among them the shape reference, no legs or limbs and the colours, stand and are handed to the maintainer
and the painter (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L420).

### Theme and family

The insect is the base of every imperial machine, as it is the base game's theme, and the Empire shows another side of
it: the base has the insect's segments and its stoop, the insect's own posture, and an insect face on which eyes and
mouthparts can be drawn (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L16-17; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L431). The family
feeling comes from a shared language of form: the same shell, the same way the black base shows through, the same
outline and shading (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L18). A creature allusion (neta) takes only outline or
function and adds no creature parts such as horns, antennae or tail stings; a form taken from a name stays, as the
ballista takes the scorpion as an inspiration (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L19;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L286, L406). The work mechanoids take no particular creature: they use Roman
architecture and machinery, and are deliberately set apart from the base game, with a heavier feel in construction
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L20; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L285).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: a chain of creature themes for the imperial machines: Rome's own animals of the New Roman period, then
> sea and river creatures, then soldier ants in Roman armour for the mass-produced troops
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/裁定归档/2026-11.md` L6; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L284-287;
> `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/戍卫.md` L11). The ants were withdrawn and the insect base took the place of the
> chain (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L406, L431; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/通则-美术.md` L8-15).

### Roman by function

A machine is Roman in its function, its name and its place in the line, and its form need not look Roman; each machine
answers three questions: its ancestor, its place in the line, and the principle pushed to its limit
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L24; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L216). The original principle
of classical weapons and engines is taken to its limit, with no parts added on top
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L37); the shield becomes a deployable wall and the bow stays as it is
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L216). Rank shows through complexity: forms take the language of the peak, basic
machines are plain and higher machines elaborate, and no gear of a declining or resource-poor period stands for a lower
rank (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L25; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L234).

The look is Roman armour and a few geometric blocks set on the insect base; the plates follow the reference image
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/美术/参照/胸甲-1.jpg`, and `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/美术/参照/胸甲-2.jpg` serves only as
a reference, without its gold, red and cloak (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L26;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L431). All imperial machines take the New Roman form, and only forms that can
stand for the ancient Roman period, such as the legion's Gallic helmet, are left to the ruins; this is read by effect,
not by strict dating (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L27; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L283,
L366). A machine's shape is divided neither by rank nor by peak; rank II and III differ in the amount of armour, their
volume and their flag (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/设计会话工作规矩.md` L182;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L283).

### Drawing and references

The textures of Biotech Mechanoid Retexture stand for the base game, and an imperial machine is an extension of the base
game's forms with a changed function; it is the main body of the drawing, but only as a reference for shape: the shell,
the black base showing through, the outline, shading and volume, while the design thinking follows the base game
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L32; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L417, L432). The looks of all
rank II machines are redone (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L33). The work mechanoids and rank III take
the base game's design thinking, with the pack's hauler, builder, agricultural and cleaning machines and its medium
standard type as shape references (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L417).

Humanoid machines, the stratior (列兵) included, share one drawing method: between More Mechs and the Retexture's
Militor, a little more abstract than More Mechs, the body inside as abstract as in the Retexture and the outer armour
as much as in More Mechs; More Mechs' faces, gendered figures and exaggerated outlines are not taken
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L34; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L432). Soldiers are drawn, not
characters: no gendered figure, the arm of service recognised by its equipment, the outline kept within the base game's
mechanoid scale, and from More Mechs the contrast of black and white, the dark body and the white armour each in blocks,
so that the outline holds when small (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L35;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L256). Plates may stay abstract and an imperial machine need not be round; More
Mechs and Alpha Mechs are references only, Alpha Mechs showing only that the idea can be made; a large volume does not
mean more black (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L36-38). No legs or limbs appear in the look, for
non-humanoid machines too; a humanoid has a big head and a short body whose lower half ends in a round base
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L39; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L418;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L105).

The south-facing image is the one used for icons and portraits, so each machine's signature part shows from the front,
and a new machine is checked from all three directions (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L41). Forms taken from
clothing become smooth hard shells, and armour is drawn as large smooth shapes, since a RimWorld sprite cannot hold the
fine texture of mail or scales (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L39). A half-shoulder cloak, buckled on the right
shoulder and thrown over the left, becomes on a machine a hard shell guarding the left side and leaving the right hand
free (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L236). It follows the place in the line: front-line ordinary soldiers and
shield-bearers do not wear it, ranged and light troops without a large shield wear it as left-side armour, and elite
and command types wear it as a mark of rank, possibly longer (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L237) (replaces the
cloak on every humanoid from rank II of `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L236). Its buckle is a small square in shell
colour, neither gold nor round, its west-facing texture is drawn on its own because the cloak is asymmetric, and a
non-humanoid carries a shell of the same form on one side of its back (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L236).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the rules of the original principle taken to its limit (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L37), of the
> south-facing image and three directions (L41), of hard shells instead of soft materials (L39) and of the half-shoulder
> cloak (L236-237) are recorded in 08, the cloak rules dated 30 October and L39 carrying a 29 October amendment about
> crest fins; the current machine art card (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L24-39) restates the principle pushed
> to its limit only among its three questions and does not restate the south-facing image, hard shells or the cloak;
> whether they still hold under the rulings of 6 November is not said. The scutator's card still applies the cloak rule
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/戍卫.md` L132).

### Colours on the machines

The shell takes the white of Biotech Mechanoid Retexture, and the base game's brightness cap of about 200 no longer
applies to machines (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L235; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/戍卫.md` L135). Machines carry a
purple identity band after the Roman clavus, narrow for equites and wide for senators: wide on command types and narrow
on ordinary ones (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L214). Where it is added, it is a narrow purple edge along the edge
of the shoulder plate, small in area, a purple line on the white from afar; the scutator's first version has none
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L229; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/戍卫.md` L136). Shells carry no gold
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L223), and the look carries no water element (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md`
L28). The present Empire's machines have the white shell, and the old empire's guardians in the ruins the old olive with
a pale red light (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L214).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: the base game tells two generations of machines apart by two colour sets, white for the new and an old
> olive for the ancient, which Imperium follows, to be checked against `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
> L214).

### Weapons

A non-humanoid machine carries no classical weapon: its weapon grows on a specialised body, as the base game's blade,
claw and spike do (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L43; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L407). A
humanoid may use spear, shield, bow and other classical melee weapons, drawn less literally
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L44). Humanoid weapons have one drawing method: made in one piece like the
base game's mechanoid weapons, without a grip, in the same blocks as the body, the shape following the role, a gun for
the stratior, spear and shield for the scutator (戍卫), a bow for the bow guard (弦卫), basic soldiers and other

## Making the art

Art is outsourced, as far as possible to one painter for the long term, paid at a price that reaches the expected
quality without needless bargaining, with steady monthly input and output, possibly less in a month and in batches;
formal work starts around rank IV at the earliest, and commissions are discussed as non-commercial
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L11; `Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L191). Once the art begins,
about four to six machines are made a month and the machines take about half a year
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-美术.md` L12; `Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L192). Machines and
buildings come first and clothing is scheduled on its own; the alpha opens once the machine and building art is done,
with base-game or borrowed community art standing in before it, and the beta opens on the Workshop about three months
later (`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L66).

## Checks

A texture checker is planned, to be made before the painter's line art comes back, to check the size multiple, the
