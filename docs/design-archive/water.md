# Hydraulics

This section records that the hydraulics axis, waterworks (水利), renamed the Imperial Aqueducts (帝国水道), is no longer
part of the design: the maintainer deleted it by the direction of 2026-11-24, and Imperium-old's root glossary
cross-checks this, having dropped its fluid-powered equipment on 2026-11-21 (Imperium-old PR #269, commit `e05b4ffc`;
the commit history only cross-checks). Nothing here is design; the note below names what the axis covered, so that its
passages are known as deleted. Rivers, banks, bridges, water gates, harbours and the water features of a city, as parts
of its terrain and layout, are not hydraulics and are in `cities-outside.md` and `cities-walls.md`; where those files
touch water as a city's power grid, they mark it as resting on these deleted hydraulics. `industry.md` marks the
water-powered processing that the cancelled standard workshop handed to the axis.
