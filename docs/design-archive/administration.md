# Administration and regions

This section covers the Empire's presence on a colony's own map: the local facilities a colony builds for it (the
imperial insignia, the imperial area and the landing pads), the kinds of imperial activity that come there, the regular
visits and how often they come, the transits, the requests and how they are paid, the letters on contribution at rank I,
and the trade channels that remain. It meets `relations.md` on joining the Empire and on hostility, `orders.md` on
orders and claims, `empire.md` on rank, leaning, contribution and the Empire's people, `core.md` on the core the insignia
holds, `mechanoids.md` on the mechanoids that are leased, `ruins-and-technology.md` on techprints and core technology,
`industry.md` on the mechanoids' manufacture and charging, and `cities-inside.md` on the imperial buildings of the
Empire's own cities.

## Kinds of imperial activity

Three kinds of activity are told apart: the regular visits, in which the Empire patrols, garrisons, trades and leases at
the colony; the transits (过境), in which the Empire assembles, changes cargo and changes ships as part of its own
running, which the player need not answer; and the requests (请求), imperial business done on the colony's own map,
started by visiting imperial staff and joined by the player of their own will (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md`
L6-12; `docs/glossary.md` L69). The three are not merged into one scheduling pool (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md`
L4). The transits are a kind of visit, so the player sees four classes of imperial interaction: visits, requests, orders
(委托) and claims (发掘) (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L82).

After the colony joins the Empire at rank I, only orders and the regular visits open; requests open once the imperial
insignia and a landing pad are built, so that the player learns the Empire layer by layer
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L14-15). What an imperial visit still lacks is seen on the imperial
insignia (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L16). Visits that run purely in the background, such as patrols
and transits, send no letter; only those that need the player's answer do
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L17).

Normal docking of shuttles depends on legal facilities, while military business on foot keeps using the map edge and is
not moved onto shuttles; Contact, as political recognition, is an exception, see 03
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L141).

### Letters on contribution

At rank I the colony's contribution is told in tiers: nothing yet to report, meagre (below 30%), considerable (below
65%), notable (below 100%) and worthy of promotion; the Empire sends one letter for each tier, only the highest when a
tier is skipped, and the sentence confirming the tier goes in the letter that ends an order's administrative handling
or, where there is none, in the letter announcing the reward's arrival (`Imperium-Knowledge/synced/IMPERIUM_STATE.md`
L42).

## Local facilities

The imperial insignia (帝国徽记), the imperial area (帝国行政区) and the landing pads (帝国停泊点) can be set up only in
settlements on the ground, not on a gravship; the Empire deals only with settlements on the ground
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L23).

### The imperial insignia

The imperial insignia stands at most once on each of the player's settlement maps; it is 3×3 and costs 80 steel, 2
components and 1600 work, and its plan, its frame and the finished building all count for that limit
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L27; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/RankI-设计核对-2026-10-29.md` L205-212). It marks the colony as one that works with the Empire and is the
symbol of its rank, showing how much the settlement matters in the Empire's eyes
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L31; `docs/glossary.md` L57). It holds the core recovered from the imperial
ruins, and once built it brings up the button at the bottom of the screen (`docs/glossary.md` L57).

The insignia is the local administrative core: the imperial area, visits and the landing of shuttles all need a built
insignia, and it is one of the conditions for rising from rank I to rank II
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L29; `Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L31). It is the centre
of the group of local facilities, but it does not decide political recognition, has no work slot of its own and makes no
activity circle of fixed radius (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L33). Its look changes with rank, one look
for each of the five ranks, changed to the new rank's look on promotion, while its function stays the same
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L29).

A buildable imperial flag also exists, unlocked by rank, with a pattern that changes with rank; it gives only the base
game's beauty, and the player decides whether to use it (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L29).

### The imperial area

The player decides the extent of the imperial area, and imperial staff are active only inside it: the base game's
caravans that wander at will are a common complaint, and the Empire keeps more to order
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L37). Imperial staff do business only in a settlement with an imperial
insignia and only inside the imperial area the player marks out
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/待用/文本重写指令-随RankII批次.md` L78).

The imperial area works like the base game's home area: the area around each built imperial insignia and landing pad
joins it automatically, in the way the base game's home area grows with buildings and with a scope calibrated by
measurement, and the player draws, extends and erases it freely, with irregular borders allowed
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L39; `docs/glossary.md` L55). Before Contact the drawing tool is hidden entirely,
and after rank I it is shown (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L39).

The area governs only the daily stay, waiting and business of legitimate imperial visitors; it does not restrict the
colonists, and it is not a storage or scoring zone (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L41). It need not touch
the insignia, has no fixed size and need not contain all the insignia's cells; entering, fighting, leaving and emergency
withdrawal follow real pathing and safety (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L41).

### Landing pads

Each landing pad is 5×5, a colony can build several, and each holds or reserves at most one real shuttle at a time
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L45; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/RankI-设计核对-2026-10-29.md` L213). A pad costs 240 steel, 5 components and 1200 work, about 1.4 times
the value of the base game's four landing beacons, to keep the material feel of a whole hard 5×5 area
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L45). The standard hull of 5×3 sits in the middle, with a platform row
above and below and three rows of hull between them; rotation follows the real occupied cells, and the pad uses the
white-and-purple platform picture the user chose (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L47).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: the base game's four landing beacons cost 160 steel and 4 components together, the price the pad's
> price was compared with, to be checked against `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L45).

A new landing needs the whole 5×5 inside the imperial area, a built insignia, a built pad, legal terrain, no blockage
and no other occupation or reservation; once the area is erased no new illegal landing starts, but a handover already
on site finishes and leaves normally (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L49). More pads add only the capacity
for physical concurrency, not orders, activities or techprint chances; queues, recall by due time, loading and saving
follow real occupation and reservation, and a pad that is briefly lacking cannot erase a promised reward
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L51).

## Regular visits

The Empire makes four kinds of regular visit (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L53-60):

- **Patrol (巡逻)**: mechanoids walk in from the map edge, leave through legitimate activity positions, fight in
  self-defence by real hostility and take no berth (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L57).
- **Garrison (驻防)**: mechanoids walk in from the edge, stand guard and patrol for one to two game days and leave on
  foot; they are not a free garrison the player commands (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L58). The garrison's
  mechanoids may use the colony's charging, including a base-game recharger, and are not repaired there; its few
  commanders need beds and meals; it defends together with the colony if raided during the stay; and the Empire pays
  nothing for it, which is inferred (推) (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L58).
- **Imperial goods visit (帝国商货到访)**: a real trader with real stock and budget, not a techprint shop; buying is
  voluntary, and goods not bought leave with the staff; the permission for restricted goods such as shield cores follows
  04; an arrival with interaction gives a short note on what it is (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L59). It is the
  one trading visit, for selling, for buying and for the trade in public property (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md`
  L151; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/待用/文本盘点-2026-10-27.md` L650).
- **Engineering lease (工程租赁)**: from rank II, imperial mechanoids only, for a rent of 25% of market value and an
  absolute term of 180000 ticks, three game days; the mechanoids do real work, their authority ends at the term and the
  originals are taken back, with no double charge and no gift of the mechanoid (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md`
  L60).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: that the garrison uses the colony's charging, beds and meals only when the colony has them to spare,
> and that providing none has no effect, is marked in the source as inferred (推)
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L58).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: separate visits for selling, for buying and for trade in public property, a separate request for a
> garrison's rest, and a lease of the base game's constructoids and lifters
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L58-59; `Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L62;
> `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/待用/文本盘点-2026-10-27.md` L649). The trading visits were merged into the imperial goods

### The lease

The lease offers imperial mechanoids, among them the builder (施工机) and the loader (装载机), and every rank II imperial
mechanoid can be leased; there is no lease at rank I (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L62;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/待用/文本盘点-2026-10-27.md` L649). The rent is 25% of market value for each mechanoid for
each three days; imperial staff bring combat and work mechanoids alike, deliver them and take them back at the end; they
come in groups of three of one type, and at a lease visit the player chooses one to three groups
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L62). Leased mechanoids take the bandwidth of the colony's mechanitor and
the player commands them, so with too little bandwidth only the groups that fit can be leased, and a colony without a
mechanitor gets no lease visit (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L62). Leased mechanoids that are destroyed
are not paid for, since the rent includes the risk (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L62). The colony's
leaning toward the merchant department amplifies the lease, a direction that is decided while the time of its
implementation is not (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L62; 
`Imperium-Knowledge/11_开发进度与未决事项.md` L41).

A permanent lease house at rank I is not approved (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L64). Losses of friendly patrols
in normal fighting bring the player no penalty, and a deliberate attack on them follows the base game's relations
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L64).

### How often visits come

Taken together, the three departments make one regular visit every five game days on long-term average: about three in
a quadrum of fifteen days, six in half a year of thirty days and twelve in a year of sixty days, as statistical
expectations that do not guarantee an arrival (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L68; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/RankI-设计核对-2026-10-29.md` L213-219). Missing facilities,
occupation or an illegal environment can make fewer visits succeed (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L68).

Each department plans in its own window of twelve days, staggered against the others, with a weight `w_i` taken from 03,
the weights summing to one: `r_i = w_i / 5`, `μ_i = 12 · w_i / 5`, and the number of visits in the window is `N_i =
floor(μ_i) + Bernoulli(μ_i − floor(μ_i))` (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L70-76). A neutral colony gets 0.8
visits from each department in twelve days, 2.4 in all, and at the cap of 60%, 20% and 20% the three get 1.44, 0.48 and
0.48 (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L78). Idle gaps, lawful concurrency and cancellation by circumstance are
allowed, a fixed batch every five days does not replace the random schedule, and the number of berths does not change
the total rate (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L78).

## Transits

The transits are the military assembly (军事集结), the cargo exchange between two ships (双船换货) and the change of ships
by an official (官员换乘); they are the transit kind of visit, and their cooldown and queue do not change the five-day
schedule of the regular visits (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L82). A transit is the Empire's own running
and also the obligation the colony takes on in joining, with no tax; it may bring the colony nothing at all and the
player need not answer it, and its role is to show that the Empire does not exist only to give the colony tasks
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L84). It is to be widened and deepened later
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L84).

Each transit needs real entities to carry out its actions; the cargo exchange and the change of ships use enough legal
berths or an ordered queue by real simultaneous occupation, and ships do not overlap
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L86). With one landing pad, a transit that needs several shuttles waits in
order, the second shuttle landing on the same pad after the first, empty, has left, while the first shuttle's people stay
and wait for it; with two or more pads they run at once, and the player is not pushed to build more
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L88; `Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L197;
`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/code-status.md` L43). A military assembly ends after a timeout of 12 hours and lasts at most 1.5
days, as initial values to be calibrated by measurement (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L197;
`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/code-status.md` L42).

The cooldown of transits is 18, 16, 14, 11 and 8 game days at ranks I to V, counted from a transit's real end; items
that could not start follow the existing rules for lawful cancelling and rescheduling, and transits are not mixed with
the regular pool (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L90). The friendly contracts and the colony's own imperial
mechanoid system are judged enough, so no random imperial reinforcement is needed any more
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L92).

## Requests

Requests are separated from the Orders that never require an expedition: visiting imperial staff start them on site at
the colony, the player may choose to take part, and no compulsory long-term management system is added
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L96). An item stays in the catalogue because it is fun, not to fill a fixed number
per department and rank (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L96). The orders are all expeditions and the content done
at home is all requests: I2 and I3 are moved into bulk supply, while the B3 local rebuilding stays an expedition order;
moving the old orders across needs no compatibility with old saves, though the migration of old task code, rewards and
evidence still needs its own acceptance (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L137).

### When requests come

Requests are not part of the transits, are independent of the regular visits and do not inherit their five-day
frequency (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L98). They use the same formula with staggered windows, only with
an average interval `D` that depends on rank: `μ_i = 12 · w_i / D`, with `D` of 8 days at rank I, 7 days at rank II and 6
days at rank III (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L98-105). Requests keep a more leisurely pace, and the
Empire's coming and going grows denser as the colony's standing rises; the high frequency of orders serves the rotation
of their candidates, and requests do not copy it (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L105).

### The catalogue

This is the one list of requests (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L107-113):

| Department | Rank I | Rank II | Rank III |
|---|---|---|---|
| Merchant | bulk supply (大宗供货), prospect drilling (勘矿试钻) | imperial temporary market (帝国临时集市) | auction of retired goods (退役物资拍卖会) |
| Military | weapon trial (军械试用) | none | joint drill (联合操练) |
| Civil administration | official-case custody (公务匣代管) | circuit court (巡回法庭) | arbitration (仲裁斡旋) |

A rise in rank only widens the pool of requests and removes no lower-rank item, and later content is preferably given as
stronger tiers and variants (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L107).

The Empire's forces are mostly mechanoids, with humans mostly the command layer, and the requests are made to fit
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L117):

- **Bulk supply**: it absorbs I2 and the old bulk procurement. Its contract-manufacturing variant, from rank II, has the
  Empire supply the materials and the player only the labour. Its military-supply variant, open at rank I, delivers
  supplies to troops in transit, mostly mechanoid repair materials (steel and components) with a little food and
  medicine for the commanders (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L118).
- **Prospect drilling**: the Empire wants to know what lies under newly registered land; a prospector brings a vague map
  of possible ore belts, the player picks a few spots to drill, and each is revealed in turn as an empty well, a small
  deposit or a large one, or now and then an insect nest or a collapse; ore found stays on the map and is shared with
  the Empire by agreement (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L119).
- **Imperial temporary market**: an imperial caravan sets up a market in the imperial area for about two days with two
  or three kinds of base-game trader; the player buys and sells freely, and the colony takes a small stall fee for each
  trader (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L120).
- **Auction of retired goods**: the Empire auctions retired and surplus goods in the colony's town, and traders of
  another faction come to bid against the colony; techprints and political authority are not auctioned
  (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L121).
- **Weapon trial**: the Empire lends a trial piece of equipment, colonists carry it through a few fights, then return it
  and report, and the player gets to use new equipment (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L122).
- **Joint drill**: imperial instructors stay several days and colonists who join the drills gain shooting or melee
  experience; the Empire sends no people, but it makes the colony's people stronger
  (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L123).
- **Official-case custody**: a local office entrusts the colony with a sealed official case for several days, colonists
  carry out an investigation for the office at the research bench, competing for time with normal research, and at the
  term the case and the result go back together (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L124). Each time one or two
  modules are attached, and higher ranks bring bigger cases and more modules: covetous people come to rob the case, by
  stealth or by storm, and where it is kept and how it is guarded is the player's choice; the seal may be broken early,
  which makes the investigation much faster and shows the contents but lowers the reward and the Empire's goodwill; and
  the investigation may turn up a lead to an old cache on the map or a small site on the world map, which the colony can
  take at the cost of the Empire's goodwill (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L124-128). In the witness variant the
  colony shelters an important witness for several days, and assassins may slip in (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md`
  L129).
- **Circuit court**: an imperial judge holds court in the imperial area for several days; the player may hand over
  prisoners for trial and is paid by their value (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L130).
- **Arbitration**: an imperial arbiter presides over the colony's negotiation with a neighbouring faction, the colony
  hosts both sides, and success improves relations between them, while the talks may break into a fight
  (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L131).

### Payment and joining

Requests pay at the base game's valuation, on the same basis as orders, and count toward contribution and leaning
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L135). Delivery requests settle at the items' base-game value; the
contract-manufacturing variant pays the labour's added value, the product's value less the materials' value, times 1.5,
with a floor of 250 silver; and requests that deliver nothing, such as receiving guests or custody, follow the base
game's reward formulae for hosting quests by headcount and days (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L135).

When the visitors arrive, the player sends a colonist to talk with them and accept, as with base-game traders
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L135). Refusing, abandoning or failing a request brings no penalty at all,
touches neither contribution, leaning nor relations, and makes no administrative lock: requests are voluntary chances,
and penalties belong only to orders (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L135). Trade-offs the player chooses
inside a request are another matter: breaking a case's seal early and taking a lead privately lower the Empire's goodwill
(`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L135).

## Trade channels

The Empire trades with the colony through the imperial goods visit, with a real trader, real stock and budget and the
permission for restricted goods following 04, and through the temporary market of the requests, which is kept as a
one-off activity of its own and not merged (`Imperium-Knowledge/06_行政设施与地区活动.md` L151).
