# Imperial mechanoids

This section gives the design of the Empire's own mechanoids: what they are and how they stand beside the base game's
mechanoids, the principles they are designed by, the ranks and the roster, how they are made, rebuilt, charged and
commanded, the standards for their bodies, numbers and weapons, the combat and work mechanoids of rank II, the
older lines that newer settings deleted, the directions for rank III and above, the mechanoids the player does not get, and how they are judged.
It meets `military.md` for the forces and weapons of people, `art.md` for the looks of the mechanoids, `terms.md` for
their names and the rules for naming them, `text.md` for their in-game descriptions, `empire.md` and
`administration.md` for ranks, research and the Empire's support, and `ruins-and-technology.md` for the old empire's
guards.

## What the imperial mechanoids are

The Empire's fighting strength rests on machines: the Empire has long been short of soldiers, so mechanoids take the
soldiers' place and inherit the Roman army's strengths in discipline, formation and logistics, each weak alone and very
strong in combination (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L105; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L289,
R-1103-0159; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L10). Where the base game's mechanoids are a swarm joined by a
hive network, the Empire's are a mechanoid swarm divided by an organisation chart: both keep the nature of a swarm, the
Empire's is not a human army in a mechanoid skin, and its organisation shows in production, clear roles, deployment and
working together (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L434, R-1109-2331;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L11; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md`
L24, L62-64). The mother machine (母机) and its child machines are one high-rank example of that organisation, not the
organisation of the whole faction (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L66).

The Empire's technology takes as its main axis a tradition of making machines, tentatively called inside the design the
automata tradition (自动机传统) and not shown to players under that name (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L190,
R-1103-1133). Imperial mechanoids are man-made, run on man-made cores and have a lineage from Ctesibius through Hero and
Eastern Rome to the present Empire, where the base game's mechanoids are an alien swarm of unknown origin on subcores,
each strong alone (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L40, L76-78). The base game leaves the
origin of its mechanoids open; the Empire's tradition stands beside it and does not explain where the mechanoid swarm
came from, so the design never says that mechanoids descend from Roman automata (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md`
L190; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L70, L84).

What the base game fixes, such as psychic abilities, cores that carry a psychic source and the mechanitor framework, is
the truth of this world, which the Empire reached by its own development: the same principle by a different path, so the
mechanitor, bandwidth, the core's psychic source and psychic ability need no explanation of their own, and the
differences lie in the Roman form, the engineering choices and the grouped tactics (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md`
L190; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L96-97). This holds only for what the base game fixes,
not for all technology (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L96). An imperial core carries a
psychic source, without which a mechanitor could not link to it, so the imperial mechanoids are not called pure
machines; the psychic source is an unseen base layer handled as the base game handles subcores, and players get no
psychic skills from it (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L83-84, L99-100;
`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L190). The base game names huge infrastructure as the other road to a core; the
Empire's military, political and industrial system is that infrastructure, and the Empire builds its cores through it at
the cost of poor efficiency, which is the user's 11-03 note on the pending list (待定清单)
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L66, L71; `Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L190).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: The base game's texts call the mechanoids killing machines of unknown origin, say they are not mere
> robots but carry a faint psychic source, and that a mechanitor controls them through both a psychic and an
> electromagnetic link, and say that an advanced subcore comes only from a full brain-pattern transfer, which without
> huge infrastructure means destructive scanning of a living person; to be checked against `Data/`
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L63-66).

The present Empire's mechanoids all take the form of New Rome (新罗马), whatever their rank and period, and the form of
Old Rome is kept for the ruins (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L140, R-1105-1908;
`Imperium-Knowledge/14_来源同步与权威.md` L218; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L96, R-1103-0051;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/散兵.md` L68). Roman and East Roman military images are borrowed for themes and roles without fixing a
period or an exact army, and not every mechanoid is humanoid or carries ancient arms
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L397-409). The design of a
mechanoid sets its role, play style, numbers, names, manufacture and rebuild; the shape and look of mechanoids and of
their gear are left to the maintainer and the artist, and art constraints already fixed are recorded, not carried
further (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L24; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L420;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/tools/红线表.md` L18). Roman flavour is not added on the designers' own account: Roman
tactics, formations or objects are not used as reasons for a shape or a function
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L23; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L78, R-1106-0825a).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: An older setting made rank II and rank III two eras, the old peak and the new peak, with simple and
> complex forms told apart only within one era (`Imperium-Knowledge/裁定归档/2026-10.md` L6). It was replaced by the form of
> New Rome for every rank (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L140; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L96).

## Principles of the design

Imperial mechanoids follow the base game's design thinking without merely imitating it: a design differs from the base
game by specialising in one thing while giving up another, or by going the opposite way
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L10; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L289, R-1103-0159). Traditional
RTS unit design may lend traits, fitted into RimWorld's frame, with the base game's thinking ranking highest
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L10; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L385, R-1104-2339a).

Each imperial mechanoid takes the slot of a base-game mechanoid with a different tendency (不同倾向) instead of being an
all-round upgrade of it: the Empire does not use an advantage without a weakness, numbers that crush the base game or a
forced pairing to push players into the full imperial set (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L12;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L434; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L22,
L68-76). Every mechanoid has a clear use and can be used alone; a purely imperial force, a force of one kind and a mix
with colonists or base-game mechanoids are all allowed, the player covering weaknesses with other forces, so that an
all-ballista force stands but with a clear weakness (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L13;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L28, L70-82). A basic unit gives ordinary combat,
while a specialist is deeper and stronger at its own work and may be weaker in moving, deploying or withdrawing
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L70-82). Imperial things are not promised to be
stronger: some only make the imperial system convenient and may alone be worse than the base game's, others are
especially strong in combination, and the choice is the player's (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L20). The setting
describes the Empire's mechanoids as machines with generally plain strengths and weaknesses (`docs/setting.md`
L102-103). The imperial mechanoids can run as a complete set by themselves and mixed with the base game's, and research,
production, charging, repair and rebuild, range and cost each exist and work around the Empire's own system
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L18-19).

The Empire as a whole leans to deployment, positions and frontal battle, yet keeps advancing, assault and output units
that move with the force; deployment is not a threshold for each mechanoid, and a mechanoid in a base-game output slot
is not turned into position support for the sake of the faction's theme (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L14;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L435, R-1109-2331a;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L26, L88). Mechanics come in step by step: low
units are easy to understand and higher units carry more decisions, not every mechanoid needs an exclusive skill or
deployment, basic mechanoids need no complex mechanics to show the theme, and two modes on a mid- or late-game unit do
not count as too complex (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L15; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L435;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L52, L86-90). No new low-level displacement such as
knockback is added for shooting effects, and existing rules are combined first (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md`
L16; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L435); this preference does not forbid every new implementation in the project
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L56). The discussion record adds no formation
bonus, morale, forced chain of command, ownership of the whole army by a mother machine or other general RTS mechanic
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L52, L610-616).

Skills are added sparingly: imperial mechanoids do not go far beyond the base game's unless a flavour cannot show
otherwise, role and distinction are expressed first through numbers and existing mechanics, and a new skill is put
forward only with the reason it cannot be left out (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L25;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L32, L403, R-1106-0512). Low-grade soldiers are kept as simple as the base game keeps
them, without even an automatic-fire switch; this is an aim and not a fixed rule, and anything to be added is first
checked for a simpler form (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L27; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L416,
R-1106-0808) (replaces in part `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L291). As a measure of complexity and not a ban, a
low-grade soldier's one automatically triggered ability is its identity and does not count as extra design, while
effects, buttons, switches, switchable modes or charging stacked on it do (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md`
L28; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L415). The base layer is kept simple where it can be, and complexity underneath
with a simple surface is used only where the base cannot be simpler (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L29;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L300).

Specialised automation has bounds: choosing a work spot, lawful gathering of goods and calibration on arrival may exist,
but they give no city building, research decisions or political management; a clear weakness of a single unit is
allowed, and nothing wins through a hidden aura or a formation of the whole Empire
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L34; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L25). Mechanoids have no hard
cap: they fill roles that human pawns would otherwise hold, and each needs an appeal of its own
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L35; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L192). Core mechanoids are rare,
through leaning, chance and a guarantee, and may be clearly stronger than basic mechanoids, as they are not held to the
basic mechanoids' cautious standard (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L192).

Imperial items correspond to the base game's by function, not one to one (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md`
L36; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L117, R-1103-2313). Mechanoids are made cheap by mass production,
which means a core prefabricated in the manufacturing bay and standardised assembly, but an imperial item with the same
function as a base-game one is not made as a discount version (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L37-38;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L117-118). Mass production applies to mechanoids only: imperial gear for
people takes the elite route, made fine and not cheapened, and the mechanitor is the Empire's small commander and wears
officer's gear (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L38; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L118,
R-1103-2316; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L31) (replaces in part `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L117,
where lowering prices by mass production was not limited to mechanoids).

## Beside the base game's mechanoids

The imperial line and the base game's mechanoid line meet at the base game's mechanitor: one mechanitor, whose
bandwidth, charging, repair, control groups, work modes and mechanoid page are all the base game's, commands both kinds
mixed in one force, and what one side builds the other can use (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L42;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L116, R-1103-2303). Control, waste, charging, work, combat, pathing and saving
run as in Biotech without copying the mechanitor system, the imperial mechanoids are mechanoids in the base game's sense,
free mixing with the base game's mechanoids is supported, and no single best formation is set
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L43-44; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L19;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L69;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-模组工程.md` L149).

Making is split: imperial mechanoids are assembled and rebuilt only in the Empire's manufacturing bay (制造仓) and the
base game's only in the mech gestator, and neither building holds the other's recipes
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L45-46; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L18, R-1103-1422;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L116; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/Code-RankII-勤务机一轮.md` L25;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/Code-RankII-重建统一.md` L7). Keys are split: the base game opens its technology with
boss chips and the Empire with the imperial codicil (委任敕书), and neither stands in for the other
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L47; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L116). Research
progress does not cross in either direction: the manufacturing bay, the berths, the command helmet and the imperial
mechanoids require only each rank's integrated imperial research and no base-game mechanoid research, and imperial
research does not complete any base-game research (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L48, L61;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L116). Crossing goes only through items: at rank II the Empire gives one
mechlink, and boss chips handed to the Empire count toward contribution only by their value as items and open no
imperial technology (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L49; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md`
L116).

A larger overhaul of the base game's mechanoids is a separate mod, made after Imperium and able to run alone: the two
work alone or together, Imperium does not depend on it, and the imperial mechanoids are always balanced against the pure
base game (`Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L164, R-1103-1402;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/原版数据/Biotech上线版机械数值调研-2026-11-10.md` L9; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/调研-流程-游戏设计与制作.md`
L60). Within Imperium the base game's mechanoids keep their values and behaviour, and only interfaces the base game
restricted are opened, such as cleaning mechanoids clearing pollution and hauling mechanoids carrying downed mechanoids
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L50). A rollback of the base game's own nerfs counts as a rollback and not as a
change to the base game (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L51; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L428, L443;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/入账-机械1110批.md` L23) (replaces in part `Imperium-Knowledge/01_项目宪章与规范索引.md` L164, where
Imperium changed no number of a base-game mechanoid).

Only nerfs are rolled back: the Militor's mini-shotgun goes back to 12 damage a shot and a 1.5-second cooldown, the
Legionary goes back to its launch weapon, the needle gun with range 44.9, and the Lancer keeps its present bandwidth of
3 (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L51-55; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L443, L447;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/入账-机械1110批.md` L19, L29) (replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L428, where all
base-game mechanoid values went back to the launch version). A rolled-back mechanoid takes the lowest combat points
among its versions, which leaves the Militor at 45 and the Legionary as it is now (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md`
L56; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L447). The one-bandwidth baseline is the Militor as Biotech launched it, and the
rank II imperial mechanoids take their numbers from it (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L57;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L428, L443).

## Ranks and the roster

The basic imperial mechanoids follow the base game's mechanoid research tiers one to one, with the same number in each
tier: the basic tier answers to rank II, the standard tier to rank III, the advanced tier to rank IV and the ultimate
tier to rank V, and every other kind is unlocked by blueprint (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L62, L405).
Rank III is the regular army in the field and rank IV keeps elite directions such as the cataphract
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L10, L439; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/戍卫.md` L10). A single rank II combat mechanoid is not the main force, but in numbers it
can be, as the Militor became the main force by being cheap and mass-produced
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L17; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L394).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: The buildable mechanoids by research tier are, basic: Militor, Lifter, Constructoid, Agrihand,
> Cleansweeper; standard: Scyther, Pikeman, Scorcher, Tunneler; advanced: Lancer, Fabricor, Paramedic, Centipede gunner
> and burner, Diabolus; ultimate: Legionary, Tesseron, Centipede blaster, Centurion, War queen; Odyssey adds the Cyclops;
> to be checked against `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L62, L393).

Rank II has five imperial mechanoids, each taking one bandwidth, which its integrated research opens together with their
facilities: one combat mechanoid, the stratior (机械列兵), set against the Militor, and four work mechanoids (勤务机)
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L17, L62, L64; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L405, R-1106-0647;
`Imperium-Knowledge/synced/IMPERIUM_STATE.md` L54). More rank II combat mechanoids may be added when there is an idea
for them (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L17). The garrison soldier (戍兵) of the older roster rose to rank III as
the scutator (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L17; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L445).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: The older rank II roster had seven mechanoids, three of them combat mechanoids set together against the
> Militor: first Limitanei, Psiloi and Alarius as auxiliaries and local garrison that were not the main force, then the

## Making imperial mechanoids

Mechanoids come in four sizes, light, medium, heavy and super-heavy, and manufacturing bays and empty cores (空白核心)
come in only two kinds, one for light and medium and one for heavy and super-heavy
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L10; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L78-79). The light-medium
manufacturing bay opens at rank II, takes 3x2 cells, costs 300 steel and 6 components, twice the mech gestator, and draws
50 W idle and 350 W working; the heavy manufacturing bay takes 4x3 cells, costs 600 steel and 12 components, twice the
large gestator, and draws 50 W and 500 W (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L71-74). A bay costs at most
twice the base game's, its power use follows the base game, and before any heavy imperial mechanoid exists the heavy bay
does not appear in the build menu (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L80-81; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md`
L23, L39).

An empty bay with power and material prefabricates one empty core and keeps it inside, at most one to a bay and not an
item that can be taken out, and pausing or losing power stops the spending (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md`
L85). Prefabrication chooses no mechanoid in advance: the choice among unlocked mechanoids is made when an order is taken
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L86; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md`
L30, L315-321). A light-medium empty core takes one game day (60000 ticks) and costs 50 steel and 2 components, like the
base game's basic subcore, and a bay holding a finished core shows it as the mech gestator shows a forming mechanoid in
its window, with progress and spending on the inspect panel (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L87-88, L105;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L39).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: Whether a fielded mechanoid can be turned back into an empty core, have its shell changed in the
> field, be refitted across sizes or be converted freely is not decided
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/ChatGPT讨论记录-帝国机械体设定-2026-11-09.md` L315-321, L373-391).

An order spends only the chosen mechanoid's body material and goes into assembly and calibration on site
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L92). The cost in body material and assembly time follows the base-game
mechanoid of the same combat points, not the body size: 45 points and below pay the light price of the Militor's tier,
75 and above the medium price of the Scorcher's and the Scyther's tier; assembly takes one game day at the light tier and
two at the medium, with one and two calibrations inside that time (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L93-94;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L354, R-1104-1426). Shortage, power loss and waiting for a calibrator only lengthen
the calendar time and never take away finished progress, every step has a real state, and a bay at full load still counts
whole cycles, prefabrication while idle only shortening the wait after an order
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L95-96).

Core refining follows the rank that unlocks a mechanoid, not its body size: rank II mechanoids use the prefabricated
empty core without refining, and from rank III an order has one more step of core refining, with more material and time,
in the place of the base game's standard subcore, without any separate refining building

`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L122). Rank III's medium regular mechanoids run on a standard batch line without

## Cores of higher grade

Players can make cores of higher grade themselves in the imperial manufacturing bay, which is the stable channel; there
may be several channels, but at least one is stable (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L112;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L33, R-1103-2320; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L120). Using people is an extra
channel: scanning colonists or captives, criminals left by the travelling court (巡回法庭) and prisoners under escort; the
specific method is not decided (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L113; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L119).
Heavy cores are made in the heavy bay at a higher cost, their refining needs a scarce material such as advanced
components so that the way is not too easy, scanning a criminal or captive can stand in for that material, and quest
rewards can speed it up or replace it (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L114-115;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L124).

Super-heavy cores come from the Empire by a fairly stable way that is not rationed by time, since the player pays no
tax and the Empire sends no tribute: their stable channel is a fixed core requisition in the support list of the archive
office (档案署), which appears only at rank V, is not in the random storage list, costs a large block of influence, is delivered
by shuttle and allows one core in transit at a time, and their extra channel is the reward of high-grade quests
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L116-118; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L36, R-1103-2329;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L123-124). Random support never delivers cores, and no such option appears

`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L125). The cost of a core is not laid on morality: cores that the Empire

## Berths, charging, repair and rebuild

The light-medium berth (泊位) takes 1x1 cells, draws 75 W idle and 200 W charging and costs 175 steel and 1 component; the
heavy berth takes 3x3 cells, draws 150 W and 400 W and costs 300 steel and 2 components, and the two are split between
medium and heavy as the bays are (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L128-131). A berth only charges and
stores no steel, as the base game's mech recharger does, and it gives a mechanoid a fixed charging port, smaller than the
base game's and perhaps no better in cost, with lawful assignment, automatic docking when unbound, the base game's

## Bodies and numbers

Every mechanoid takes one cell (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L142; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L296).
Combat mechanoids have body size 0.7, as the base game's light mechanoids do, and work mechanoids are all light with
body size 0.7 (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L143-144; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L301). A
deploying work mechanoid keeps body size 0.7; when deployment completes it is drawn 1/0.7 larger, about 1.43 times, so
that it looks like 1.0, and it returns to its size when packing begins, always on one cell, because body size governs
only how easily it is hit and what it carries, while the sense of scale comes from the deployed picture
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L145-146; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L324).

Work mechanoids have the armour and health of the base game's light mechanoids, 20% sharp, 10% blunt and a health
multiplier of 1.0, and move at about 70% of their base-game counterparts' speed
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L147-148).
Combat points follow the tier of the base-game mechanoids of the same kind (10, 45, 75, 110, 150 and the like) and do not
change once set, balance being done with other numbers; work mechanoids take 10, as the base game's work mechanoids do
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L150; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L326, L351, R-1104-1353)
(replaces `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/哨骑.md` L15, L33, where tests against base-game mechanoids fixed combat
points). Market value follows the base game's ratio of mechanoid price to material value, about 2.71, with the
prefabricated core valued at a basic subcore's 200 (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L151). For comparisons
the design uses two approximations, hit chance as weapon accuracy times shooter accuracy to the power of distance, and
the armour factor as 1 − 0.75 × (armour − penetration), taken as zero where armour is below penetration, with
measurement deciding (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L152;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/机械/RTS远程兵种武器类别调研-2026-11-05.md` L381). Base-game data are taken from the
Defs, even where the wiki disagrees (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L153).

## Weapons

Imperial mechanoid weapons are made in one piece with the body, as the base game's mechanoid weapons are: dropped means
destroyed, and colonists cannot take them (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L33;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L424, R-1106-2019). Their form may be a gun, with a barrel and no grip, trigger or
stock, like the base game's mini-shotgun; what is not made is gunpowder, and a gun-shaped weapon may have a name and
description of its own, as the base game's charge lance does (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L33;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L424). This governs only the mechanoids' built-in weapons; that
imperial people use no firearms is unchanged (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L33). For the Empire's
weapons in general, artillery included, the later setting rules out both firing by gunpowder and driving a projectile
electromagnetically, counts a bolt thrower as artillery, and leaves the mechanoids' built-in weapons to their own
section (`docs/setting.md` L67-70).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: Older weapon settings let no imperial mechanoid weapon take the form of a gun, and then put every weapon
> into the three effect families, the stratior's into plasma (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/做法史/设计会话工作规矩.md`
> L38-39, L169-170; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L429). They were replaced by built-in weapons that may take a
> gun's form without gunpowder and by the families' use at ranks IV and V only (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md`
> L424, L442).

Weapon effects come in three families after the three ultratech weapons of the base game's Empire: mono (单分子), after
the monosword, with high armour penetration; plasma (等离子), after the plasmasword, which adds burning; and EMP (电磁脉冲),

## The work mechanoids

The four rank II work mechanoids are light, keep body size 0.7, move at about 70% of their base-game counterparts'
speed, take 10 combat points and can be used whatever the colony's leaning among the three departments, having nothing
to do with leaning (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L144, L147-150, L158; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L301,

missing, and each also does its base-game counterpart's side jobs at very low efficiency through the base game's work
types and speed stats, with no system of its own (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L18, L167;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L302, R-1103-1355; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L17). Their design is recorded as
settled with two shared features: they can load resources, or collect and transport them, and after deploying they work

chosen for a Roman feel (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L191; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L340, L357).

### The builder

The builder (施工机, formerly 营造机) is modelled on the base game's Constructoid and does continuous works from a fixed work
spot: a large site is to be finished no slower than by one Constructoid at the same bandwidth, a few walls to go slower
and a whole big site clearly faster; by a back-calculation after the 600-tick deploy and pack time, a few walls come to
about 67%, about 15 walls to equal the Constructoid and 60 walls to be about 40% faster, with the three tiers to be
retested (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L8-12; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L349, R-1104-1331). It does the
Constructoid's repair and roofing at very low efficiency, building first (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L14). It
moves at 2.4 against the Constructoid's 3.4, keeps body size 0.7, and when deployed only its picture grows, its inside
empty so that other units walk past, on one cell (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L20-26;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L300).

It deploys and packs in 600 ticks each, which by the design session's judgement puts small jobs at five to seven and a
half tenths of the Constructoid per bandwidth; the maintainer has not commented on it, it can be vetoed, and Code is to
retest the three tiers (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L32; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L349). It covers the
cells with x²+z²≤26, an 11x11 area of 89 cells whose centre alone is idle, leaving 88 workable, and several builders
keep apart by a separate 7x7 core zone (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L33-35). Its automatic choice first filters
lawful spots and then prefers the one covering more lawful unfinished targets; several builders overlap cores only when
no other lawful place exists and never contest a reservation; the deployment centre may not stand on an unfinished
blueprint or frame; and it never picks a spot that finishing the work would wall it into
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L36-42; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L316). It works the whole spot from far to
near without packing between jobs, packs only when the whole spot has nothing to do, and at a fixed spot ignores
straight-line sight blocking, finished walls included, without relaxing target legality, delivery, reservation or real
reachability (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L46-48).

Its construction efficiency is 35%, for lawful building, deconstruction and, where eligibility allows, uninstalling, with
real work time and conserved returns (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L52-55). Repair and roofing run at
construction speed times 0.3, about four tenths of the Constructoid, taken as just these two base-game jobs and done
packed, walking up to the target, only when no lawful workable spot exists in its allowed area
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L56-58; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L302, L307).

Its three construction slots each hold a stack up to the real lawful limit, of the same or different kinds; the base
game's way of supplying was tried and ran over twice as slow as the base-game machine on large works, so the slots stay
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L63-64). It fetches material for the frames it has claimed, filling all three
slots in one trip and going again when they run out, and does no hauling work; while it is deployed, colonists and
loaders may bring what its claimed frames still lack to its slots by the base game's haul, so that with someone refilling
it need not leave (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L65-66; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L312-313;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L307; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/Code-RankII-勤务机一轮.md` L30-32). The slots
serve only normal supply of blueprints and frames, and unused material is kept through packing, relocation, interruption
and saving (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/施工机.md` L67, L69).

### The harvester

The harvester (收割机, formerly 屯田机) is a walking intensive-farming mechanoid: it does not deploy but works while walking,

It does the base game's planting, sowing and harvesting, and cutting work types by walking up to the plant, does no

### The pollution sweeper

The pollution sweeper (清污机, formerly 复垦机) works after Biotech's pollution pump, the base game's cleaning mechanoid
being only its reference for size, movement and charging, and it also does the base game's cleaning at very low
efficiency (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/清污机.md` L8-11). It only produces and takes no input: it has no slot, and its
waste packs drop to the ground for the base game's logistics (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/清污机.md` L12). It moves at
2.4 against the cleaning mechanoid's 3.4, keeps body size 0.7, and when it roots, its suction pipe goes into the ground
without enlarging its one-cell footprint; it charges as a normal mechanoid with no grid connection, the pump's 200 W being
only a work reference, and comes with the rank II grant without pollution-pump or power research
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/清污机.md` L18-26).

Clearing pollution is its own work and sweeping the cleaning mechanoid's, each doing about half as well at the other's
work (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/清污机.md` L32; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L348, R-1104-1321a;
`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L379). It clears a polluted cell in about 930 ticks, twice as fast as the cleaning
mechanoid clears by hand, about 64 cells a day without movement, deployment or charging
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/清污机.md` L33). Its range is the base game's radius of 10.9 cells, it deploys and packs
in 360 ticks each, and it clears only when fully deployed, stopping when packing begins
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/清污机.md` L34-35). For every 6 cells of real pollution removed it makes one real toxic
waste pack beside its platform, which dissolves and pollutes as any base-game pack does, with no special case; it gets no
credit for unpolluted cells, and clearing progress and pack count are kept through interruption and saving
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/清污机.md` L39-41).

It finds real pollution by itself, picks a truly reachable spot within its allowed area and controls that covers more
pollution, moves, deploys, and packs and moves on when the site is cleared, never deploying at a distance when the site
is unreachable and never opening and closing for nothing; its clearing work type is hidden on the work page
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/清污机.md` L46-49; `Imperium-Knowledge/designs/回报/Code-RankII-勤务机一轮.md` L28). Its
cleaning runs at 0.25, half of the cleaning mechanoid's 0.5, and clearing pollution comes first: it folds to sweep only
when no reachable pollution lies in its allowed area (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/清污机.md` L53-55;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L348).

## How the mechanoids are judged

Imperial mechanoids are not designed to fight the base game's mechanoids and can be used alongside them, the Empire not
being a hostile faction by default; they are judged by specialisation, counters and whether three mechanoids form a
ring, the base game serving only as an upper reference with no one-to-one duels, and functions come before balanced
numbers (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L172-174; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L297, R-1103-1213e).
Balance testing is sealed until the content is done, its methods kept for when it is unsealed, while each delivery is
still checked to install, run and take effect (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L171;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L392, R-1105-1113).

Strength toward the player is judged by bandwidth, against base-game mechanoids of the same bandwidth and function; combat
points only price raids and set the cost tier, and equal points are only a diagnostic
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L176; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L62, R-1104-1840;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L362, L365). Most fights in RimWorld are few against many, which is a design idea
for the player: rank II mechanoids are thought out as the outnumbered player uses them, not judged by duels at equal
points, while scale tests stay as before (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L177;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L388, L390; `Imperium-Knowledge/现行/索引.md` L65) (replaces in part
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L388, where tests were to be set up as few against many). The Empire goes by sheer
numbers: battles run at normal raid scale, about 1000 to 1500 points in mid rank II, and small fights only show whether a
function works (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L178).

Command and mechanoid are judged apart: being clearly stronger than same-bandwidth base-game mechanoids under fine control
is acceptable, but winning heavily over them without control or under the base game's AI means the mechanoid is too
strong and is changed (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L179). Results are read by match type, counter,
neutral or countered, since equal points are not equal strength, from the player's side, where bandwidth, cost,
resurrection and time are all costs, and with the enemy's mechanics in mind: a counter-match is won with some loss, a
neutral match only needs no crushing against the same-function base-game mechanoid, and the countered side may lose
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L180-181). Per bandwidth, a single mechanoid's toughness and output are
compared item by item with the same-function base-game mechanoid, the specialty up to two tenths above, its weakness up to
two tenths below and the rest within two tenths, only to find the skewed item
(`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L182). New numbers are measured in fixed scenes, a deviation of more than 20%
from calculation is reported with a proposed adjustment for the maintainer to confirm rather than changed automatically,
and design goals rank above particular numbers (`Imperium-Knowledge/现行/机械体/通则-基线.md` L183) (replaces
`Imperium-Knowledge/裁定归档/2026-10.md` L179).
