# Mechanized industry

This section covers how a colony makes and keeps the Empire's mechanoids: the two lines of production beside the base
game's, the two kinds of manufacturing bay and the empty cores they prefabricate, how a mechanoid is ordered, assembled
and calibrated, where higher cores come from, the charging berths, rebuilding a fallen mechanoid, where bandwidth comes
from, and the civil industrial buildings. It meets `mechanoids.md` on the mechanoids themselves and the cost of each,
`ruins-and-technology.md` on the techprints, research and core technology that open these buildings,
`administration.md` on leased mechanoids, `orders.md` and `empire.md` on order rewards and the support the colony asks
of the Empire, and `water.md` on the deleted hydraulics.

## Two lines of production

The Empire's own manufacturing bays stand beside the base game's mech gestator, and the mechanitor, bandwidth, waste,
charging, pathing and saving keep using Biotech's own mechanisms (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L8). Size
sets the basic production specification and political rank sets the legal right to manufacture; a small mechanoid
unlocked later needs no heavy line, and a small core does not become a heavy core without cost
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L8). Mechanoids come in four sizes, light, medium, heavy and ultraheavy,
while manufacturing bays and empty cores come in two kinds: light and medium share one core, which the light-medium bay
prefabricates, and heavy and ultraheavy share another core and bay (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L10).

Imperial mechanoids are made only in the imperial manufacturing bay, and base-game mechanoids only in the base game's
mech gestator (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L48; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L109). The empty core
prefabricated in the bay is the Empire's mark, so an order needs only the second half of the work, assembly, and costs
only that mechanoid's assembly materials, with no subcore (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L48;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L109). The body follows the base-game mechanoid of the same combat-point tier, not
its size: for a light one, the core (50 steel, 2 components) and 50 steel of body come to the same total as the base
game's light mechanoid (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L42; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L354). The cost of
each mechanoid is in `mechanoids.md`.

Production facility, core prefabrication and the authority to make a body are separate layers, and the colony's real
politics, research, facility, materials and calibration all have to be met at once; the imperial bays, the berths and
the imperial mechanoids recognize only each rank's imperial integrated research (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md`
L103). The medium field mechanoids of rank III are meant for standard batch lines and are not taken to consume a
repeated scan of a person for each mechanoid (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L44). A few basic heavy models can
come by regular research, while the other special heavy models and all ultraheavy models come by their own techprints,
and the test-use Scyther, Tunneler and Diabolus give no normal right to manufacture at a low rank
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L44).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the catalogue of which heavy and ultraheavy models come by research and which by techprints is not
> decided (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L44). Final costs, the recipes of mechanoids not yet listed and
> the missing parameters of new industrial buildings are kept open and are not to be filled from historic temporary
> mappings (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L103). A precision core item and its recipe exist in the mod with
> no design in the documents and were under review (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/待用/文本盘点-2026-10-27.md` L654).

## Manufacturing bays

There are two manufacturing bays (制造仓), the light-medium bay and the heavy bay, split like the two charging berths
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L107; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L113). The split falls
between medium and heavy, not between light and medium as in the base game, since ranks II and III use only the
light-medium bay and a base-game split would open the large bay at rank III
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L112).

The light-medium bay is 3×2, draws 50 W on standby and 350 W running, costs 300 steel and 6 components, and opens at
rank II; light and medium imperial mechanoids are both made in it (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L40;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L113; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L126). The heavy bay is
4×3, draws 50 W on standby and 500 W running, and costs 600 steel and 12 components
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L40; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L126). Until there are
heavy imperial mechanoids the heavy bay is not in the build menu, since it would have nothing to make and would still
prefabricate cores and spend materials (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L111;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L113). A bay costs at most twice the base game's comparable facility: the
two bays cost twice the base game's small and large gestators, and their power is the base game's
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L31; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L126).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: the base game's small mech gestator makes only light mechanoids and its large one medium and above, and
> its small recharger serves only light mechanoids and its large one medium and above; the small gestator costs 150 steel
> and 3 components and the large one 300 steel and 6 components, to be checked against `Data/`
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L112; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L31).

## Empty cores and the making of a mechanoid

An idle bay with normal power and enough resources spends real material to prefabricate one empty core (空白核心), which
stays inside the bay, one at most to a bay, and is not an item that can be taken out; the bay's pause or a power cut
stops the spending (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L14; `docs/glossary.md` L130). The empty core is kept as
the mark of imperial manufacture (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L27). Prefabrication does not choose the
final mechanoid: the light-medium bay's core serves both light and medium, and which unlocked light or medium mechanoid
is made is decided at the order (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L16;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L107). The light-medium core costs 50 steel and 2 components, like the base
game's basic subcore, and takes one game day (60000 ticks) to prefabricate
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L40; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L110). While a
prefabricated core is in the bay it shows on the building's texture, with a placeholder first, and the inspect panel
shows the progress and the consumption of prefabrication (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L126).

The player orders a mechanoid through a normal manufacturing bill, choosing an unlocked type; the bill spends that
type's added materials, and the mechanoid then goes through assembly and calibration on site
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L16; `docs/glossary.md` L105). Idle prefabrication shortens the wait after
an order, but a bay working at full load still turns out mechanoids at the pace of the full cycle
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L16). Assembly takes one game day with one calibration at the light tier
and two game days with two calibrations at the medium tier, and which tier a mechanoid takes follows the base-game
mechanoid of the same combat points, not its size (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L18-21;
`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L354; `Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L355). Calibration is inside the
assembly's time and adds no cycle; missing materials, a power cut or waiting for a qualified calibrator lengthen the
calendar time but take away no finished progress and cancel no calibration, and every step, spending, interruption,
cancellation and damage has a real state (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L25).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the prefabrication times of the heavy and ultraheavy rows are left to ranks IV and V
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L27).

### Refined and higher cores

Core refining follows the rank at which a mechanoid is unlocked, not its size: mechanoids of rank II use only the empty
core from the bay, and from rank III an ordered mechanoid's bill adds a step that refines the empty core with more
material and time, in the place of the base game's standard subcore (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L354;
`docs/glossary.md` L108; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L121). The refining stays in the bill, with no
separate refining facility (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L122).

The player can make higher cores in the imperial bay, and that is the stable channel; scanning colonists or prisoners,
criminals left by the circuit court and convoyed prisoners are extra channels, since the Empire has facilities for mass
production and does not depend on using people (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L120). There are several
channels, at least one of them stable, and medium mechanoids reach mass production at rank III
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L119-120). Heavy cores are made by the player in the heavy bay at a higher
cost, their refining needing a scarce material such as advanced components; scanning criminals or prisoners can stand in
for that material, and order rewards are a further channel (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L124-125).

Ultraheavy cores are not handed out by time: they come through the support the colony asks of the Empire with influence,
delivered by a real shuttle, as a fixed core requisition (核心请领) that appears only at rank V, stands outside the random
list, costs a large part of the colony's influence and can have only one core in transit at a time; rewards of high
orders are an extra channel (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L123-125). Random support never delivers cores,
and none of these options appears before the colony reaches the matching rank
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L124-125).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the exact materials and influence cells of the heavy and ultraheavy core requisitions are left to
> ranks IV and V, and the requisition's tiers and cells are not decided; four or five of the ten influence cells is the
> figure given while the cells remain open; how much refining each rank takes is referred to item 59 of file 12
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L354; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L123-124).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: core refining as an extra step of the medium mechanoids' bills, and cores delivered by random support for
> heavy and ultraheavy mechanoids (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L121;
> `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L124). Refining by rank replaced the first, and the second was deleted

## Charging berths

There are two charging berths (充能泊位): the light-medium berth for light and medium mechanoids, 1×1, drawing 75 W on
standby and 200 W charging, costing 175 steel and 1 component; and the heavy-ultraheavy berth, 3×3, drawing 150 W and
400 W, costing 300 steel and 2 components (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L54-57). They take legitimate
designation of mechanoids, choose a dock automatically for an unbound mechanoid, fall back to base-game charging, and
charge and make waste for real; a mechanoid that can be recovered is moved in by real work, and power, refunds on
cancelling or destruction, pausing and loading keep their balance (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L63). A
berth only charges, as the base game's do, and stores no steel; repair and the rebuilding of wrecks are done in the
automatic rebuilding bay (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L61; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L110;
`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L112).

## Rebuilding a fallen mechanoid

A fallen imperial mechanoid is rebuilt in the imperial manufacturing bay from its corpse and one empty core prefabricated
in the bay, with no steel, in half the assembly time of its type and by the same process as assembly; the light-medium
bay uses its own core and the heavy bay the heavy core, and without a prefabricated core the rebuilding waits for one
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L413). The core costs more than the base game's revival, so the gain is time, and
since a bay prefabricates one core at most, how fast several fallen mechanoids come back depends on the number of bays
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L413). Base-game mechanoids are revived as before in the base game's gestator
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L115).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: reviving imperial mechanoids with the base game's resurrection serum, and then with a revival bill of
> corpse and steel in the imperial bay (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L61; `Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md`
> L115). Rebuilding from a prefabricated core replaced the revival bill (`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L413).

## Bandwidth

Bandwidth comes from the base game's band node, and the Empire adds no bandwidth building of its own
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L114). The imperial command helmet can be made from rank II with no signal
chip and no advanced components, from 110 plasteel, 14 components and 15750 work
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L114). An imperial item matches a base-game item by function, not one to
one, and an imperial item with the same function as a base-game one is not made as a cut-down version
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L117).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: the base game's band node can be built with the earliest mech research for 200 steel and 4 components,
> adds one bandwidth to the one mechanitor it is tuned to and can be built without limit, and neither it nor the
> airline headgear needs a boss chip, to be checked against `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L114).

> [!NOTE]

## Civil industrial buildings

The civil industrial buildings have a decided direction and belong to the civil administration department: the automatic
rebuilding bay is a core technology building of rank III, the pollution-to-fuel converter a regular techprint, and the
fixed operating range facility a core technology of rank III (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L77-79;
`Imperium-Knowledge/目录/主文档-01至08.md` L88-91).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: their exact recipes are not complete (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L77;
> `Imperium-Knowledge/目录/主文档-01至08.md` L88). Core technology is two items per department and rank, so one of the
> civil rank III candidates, the rebuilding bay, the fixed range facility and the lictor, moves into the rank III regular
> techprints, and which one is not decided (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L79).

### The automatic rebuilding bay

The automatic rebuilding bay (自动重建仓) is 4×3 and draws 50 W on standby and 1000 W running; it keeps both repair and
the full rebuilding of wrecks, and the Empire's own repair rules are written only for it
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L83-85). Damaged mechanoids or wrecks that qualify are carried in by normal
hauling, and with real steel and components supplied the bay repairs or rebuilds them on its own, for imperial and
base-game mechanoids alike; its gain is automation at half the cost, to support attrition tactics with many light
mechanoids (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L85).

Repair spends steel at 25% of the speed of the base game's mechanitor repair, and the steel to fill all health is about
25% of the materials of a new mechanoid of that type, deducted by the share of damage, clearly cheaper than a full
rebuild (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L86). A rebuild costs 50% of the material value and the work time of a new
mechanoid of the same type, and the rebuilt mechanoid keeps its name and its mechanitor
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L87). Only the colony's own wrecks qualify, so enemy or ownerless wrecks cannot be
repaired or rebuilt to get round the base game's mech research and the Empire's right to manufacture
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L89). The bay costs 900 steel, 18 components and 100 plasteel, set on 2026-10-25
as the medium bay's recipe of the time plus 100 plasteel, the medium bay having since been merged into the light-medium
bay; it is not a free instant revival and not the paused project Salvus (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L91).

### The pollution-to-fuel converter

The pollution-to-fuel converter (污染物转燃料转化器) turns real toxic wastepacks into chemfuel by manufacturing work, with
more value going in than coming out, since its worth lies in handling pollution, not in profit from nothing; the output
is about 70% of the input's value (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L95). One toxic wastepack and 20 steel
give 12 chemfuel, an input value of 38 and an output of 27.6, about 73%; its power follows the base game's biofuel
refinery, and each batch takes about 1500 ticks of work, the base values to be checked at implementation
(`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L95).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: the base game's toxic wastepack has a market value of 0, so the converter's input value counts the
> added materials and the work, to be checked against `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L95).

### The fixed operating range facility

The fixed operating range facility (固定操作范围设施) gives 1.5 times the base game's mechanitor operating range, on the
same basis, and changes only the range, adding no bandwidth (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L99). It is 2×2,
costs 300 steel and 6 components and draws 400 W; it needs power, is centred on itself with a radius of 1.5 times the
base mechanitor's range, works for all the colony's mechanitors and stops without power; any number can be built, and
their coverage does not stack (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L99). The signifer, the mechanoid that
carries the standard and gives a moving control range, is separate, and the two are not both widened into a buff for the
whole team (`Imperium-Knowledge/07_机械工业与制造设施.md` L99; `docs/glossary.md` L135).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: how the facility behaves on losing connection and on saving is to be measured
