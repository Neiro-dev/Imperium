# Ruins and technology

This section covers the ruins of the old empire and what they hold, the excavations and claims that reach them, and
the ways technology comes to a colony: techprints (蓝图) and their registration, the imperial research opened by the
imperial codicil, the techprint catalogue with its rewards and its rank I equipment, the core technologies of the three
departments, and the base game's research beside them. The core and the starting ruins it comes from, with the
analysis device and the imperial insignia that use it, are in [core.md](core.md); handing finds over to the Empire and
the ill will of excavating alone are in [relations.md](relations.md); claims as a class of the Empire's business are in
[orders.md](orders.md); ranks, leaning and the grant of the codicil are in [empire.md](empire.md); the imperial
mechanoids and their research are in [mechanoids.md](mechanoids.md); the guardians of the ruins and the Empire's
weapons are in [military.md](military.md); the generation of ruin maps is in [mantle.md](mantle.md); Old Rome and the
silence are in [world.md](world.md); colours are in [art.md](art.md).

## The ruins of the old empire

The ruins of the old empire take the base game's forms for places on the world map: ruins that quests lead to and,
where Odyssey is active, ruins that stay where they are; without Odyssey only the ruins that quests lead to appear
(`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L41-45, L52-56, L60-61). The Empire yields techprints, and so do the ruins, and both
forms of ruins can yield them (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L63-64). Ruins that stay give the colony something the
ruins of quests cannot, suited to what players take up Odyssey for: techprints that come only with Odyssey
(`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L46-47, L65-66; `docs/glossary.md` L28).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: the base game offers places on the world map that quests lead to, and Odyssey adds ruins that stay
> where they are; the two forms of the ruins rest on this, to be checked against `Data/`
> (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L60-61).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: what else ruins that stay give is open, tracked as issue #208 (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L67).

The starting ruins are a decayed town near the colony that a quest leads it to early in the game and that holds the core
(`docs/core.md` L10-11, L59-63); they are described in [core.md](core.md).

A ruin is generated from a settlement plan with a decay mask laid over it, mostly the Roman plan of the old peak
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/ICF/聚落平面语法-v4.md` L406). The look of mainland Italy, of Ostia, Pompeii and the city of
ancient Rome, goes to the ruins, while the Empire's living cities take mainly that of New Rome
(`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/city-plans/罗马大城住宅类型-查证-2026-11-03.md` L10). The roads of an old Roman ruin are
broken old stone paving with grass and missing blocks (`Imperium-Knowledge/09_地图生成与CBF技术.md` L731, R-1030-2214).
The old empire's machines found in the ruins are its craft as it was in the old empire, and the present Empire's
mechanoids are the same craft in its present form (`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md`
L92). The ruins keep the old empire's guardian mechanoids (`docs/glossary.md` L29, L151, L155), which
[military.md](military.md) describes.

## Excavations and claims

An excavation takes the colony to a site of the old empire, and a claim (Claim) is an excavation that the Empire
sends the colony on, not the base game's Claim command (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L10, L71;
`docs/glossary.md` L46). Excavations, claims among them, follow the base game's quest form
(`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L71). A colony in the Empire reaches the old empire's sites through claims and a colony
outside it by its own excavations, and both reach the same sites (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L72-74); a colony in
the Empire cannot excavate on its own and hands the old empire's technology it finds over to the Empire, as
[relations.md](relations.md) describes (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L80-84). When claims come in a colony's rise
through the ranks is in [orders.md](orders.md).

A colony outside the Empire takes on excavations of the old empire's ruins, finds techprints that carry the old empire's
technology and analyzes that technology with the analysis device, a way to technology that does not pass through the
Empire (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L10-13, L88-89). The first excavation the colony takes on, not counting the
starting ruins, unlocks making the analysis device, and a colony that has the device can analyze with it the old
empire's technology it has excavated (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L20-23, L75-76). The analysis device, which a
colony makes outside the Empire, uses the core and analyzes the old empire's technology more slowly than the imperial
insignia does (`docs/core.md` L38-39, L69-70).

## What the ruins hold

The ruins keep away from what the base game already does: Anomaly's study and containment of entities, Ideology's
ancient complex and the base game's ancient dangerous rooms (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L10). How Rome came
to the Rimworld, the point of inheritance (传承接点), belongs to the truth of the ruins and is deliberately left
unexplained (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L12). The truth gives a partial answer: it reveals the direct cause
of the lost contact but leaves open whether Sophia still exists (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L44).

On a site the colony clears collapses, digging through rubble as in mining to reach the parts that are buried, and opens
sealed zones by repairing the site's old generating equipment and connecting its lines, a small spatial puzzle
(`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L66-68). The threats of the ruins are both on the site: the guardians that the
player's own actions wake, which are security machines of the old empire's own make, unlike the base game's mechanoids,
are only enemies and cannot be obtained, and no new faction comes with them, and the holding of a node while it starts,
when its signal draws hostile forces to contest it; digging and deeper discoveries add no other threat
(`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L34, L88, L90) (replaces the obtaining of guardians through claims,
`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L90).

Relics (遗物) and lost technology (失传技术) are among the directions that belong to the ruins axis
(`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L9, L102). Lost technology is one class, joined with the technology dropped for
efficiency: practical facilities and equipment that the old empire had and lost through war or other causes, close in
weight to the Empire's mechanoids and of higher practical use (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L104). It holds no
mechanoids, since a player who plays only the ruins may be unable to make and control them, and since the weapons and
armour of imperial people are mostly ceremonial, of the base game's strength but not common, relics and lost technology
hold no personal armour (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L104). There is no quota of relics or lost technologies, and
empty places need not be filled (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L106, L124).

Single relics are taken only from sites and cannot be made (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L108):

- the mask of forgetting (遗忘面具), an awakening (启灵) relic that, worn for a time, removes one traumatic memory and
  some skill experience with it (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L109);
- the omen compass (预兆罗盘), an awakening relic whose wearer sometimes has warning hours before a raid but suffers
  occasional nightmares and lower mood (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L110);
- the resonance pillar (共感石柱), an awakening relic and a building, around which the moods of colonists converge, good
  and bad alike (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L111);
- the star-track calculator (星轨测算仪), an object of the old empire, a geared device on the model of the Antikythera
  mechanism that forecasts eclipses, cold snaps, heat waves and similar events (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md`
  L112; `docs/glossary.md` L150);
- the eternal lamp (永燃灯), an object of the old empire that gives light and warmth for ever without fuel or power
  (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L113);
- the last communication record (末次通讯记录), the last message before contact was lost. When it is taken an imperial
  official comes to demand it, and the player chooses on the spot, before it is decoded and with nothing to preview:
  kept, the colony decodes it alone over a long time and has the full original, the last piece of the truth; handed
  over, the colony hears only the Empire's retelling, which may be edited, in exchange for an earlier push of the
  Empire's endgame or weight in promotion. The two cannot both be had, both outcomes are fixed whatever is reloaded, and
  a colony outside the Empire has no one to demand it and keeps it (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L114-118).

Of lost technology, the colony builds what it gains: the field repair swarm (野战维修蜂群) automatically repairs the
buildings and machines in its range and is a fixed building that needs no mechanitor
(`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L120-121). The river dam, the culvert seepage irrigation, the city shield tower,
the thermal dome tower and the mechanoid pack camel were removed from lost technology, and the heavy siege machine and
the ancient legion soldier with them as machines (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L120).

> [!NOTE]
> **Deleted**: the defensive flood gate (防御泄洪闸), which stored water and, opened, washed a preset band to knock
> down, stun and strike enemies, muddy the ground and put out fire, refilling by the ways of the waterworks, and the

the ruins, each of which gives at least one psylink neuroformer (灵能链接神经塑形器) (`Imperium-Knowledge/16_旧日遗迹主轴.md` L76;
`Imperium-Knowledge/designs/研究/主轴对比-工质与自动机传统-2026-11-03.md` L98; `docs/glossary.md` L152-153).

## Techprints

Techprints are a layer of technology across the past and the present, and those of the ruins carry the old empire's
technology (`docs/ruins-and-technology.md` L94-95). Legitimately obtaining a real techprint and registering it
authorizes its technology at once, with no further round of ordinary research, no days of waiting for restoration and
no separate rank I research stage (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L21; `docs/glossary.md` L92). What has not
been registered stays hidden in the imperial research display; authorization does not replace the materials,
facilities and real work that making the thing needs, and the Empire's restoration of ancient data does not become a
lock the player waits on (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L21). In the Empire the imperial insignia reads
techprints (`docs/core.md` L36-37, L67-68).

Each techprint is still one physical item, labelled "techprint: X", in Chinese "科技图纸：X", with X the name of what it
authorizes, so that techprints line up and can be found in item lists (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L23;
`docs/glossary.md` L72; `.github/deprecated-terms.json` L468-477, L1080-1092). The texts of research and techprints say
what they really open and keep authorization, research and making apart, without suggesting that an authorized thing is
already built, a hidden research wait, or that what is ancient is necessarily more advanced
(`Imperium-Knowledge/10_文本命名与界面规范.md` L24).

The three departments are identified as MilitaryGroup, CivilAdministration and MerchantGuild only for the Empire's
internal business and for developers; the player's interface shows none of their names, tags, weights, contribution
values or progress and has no tab for them, while a techprint shows the device's real effects and the conditions of
its authorization and making (`Imperium-Knowledge/synced/IMPERIUM_STATE.md` L69-70; `docs/glossary.md` L45, L62-63).

> **Deleted**: the techprint line of the code-era specification. One physical techprint, for the wasp drone trap

> the nine techprints of the catalogue below (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L41).

## Imperial research

No imperial content hangs on the base game's mechanoid research: the Empire's buildings, equipment, ways of production
and services do not need it, and the imperial mechanoids need only each rank's imperial integrated research
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L8, L27, R-1103-2303) (replaces the base-game mechtech prerequisite of the
imperial mechanoids, `Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L8, L27). The imperial mechanoids run through a system of
their own, from research and making to charging and supply, repair and rebuilding, range and cost; what is imperial is
not guaranteed stronger, some of it existing only to make the imperial system convenient and some being especially
strong in combination, with the choice left to the player; and imperial content covers every category, not only
mechanoids (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L9-11). Each rank brings at least one new mechanism and not only
higher numbers; new mechanisms grow only from Imperium's axes and the techprint layer, and no rank opens a system of
its own; at rank II the imperial mechanoid system, requests and leasing are that mechanism
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L12). The Empire's advance differs from the base game's, in which a boss is
defeated for a chip: the colony advances by helping the Empire, being promoted and receiving the imperial codicil
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L12).

> [!NOTE]
> **Unverified**: the new mechanisms of ranks III to V are still to be inventoried
> (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L12).

A colony has five political ranks, I to V, and there are four imperial research projects, for ranks II to V; rank I has
no imperial research, no hidden prerequisite and no research completed of itself, and contact gives political rank
only (`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/dromos-integration-v1.md` L9; `RimWorld-Imperium/docs/imperial-contact-v0.md` L15;
`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L21). Basic, standard, advanced and ultra correspond to ranks II to V
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L25):

The four costs and X positions are those of Biotech's four mechtech projects, no mechtech prerequisite is added across,
and the separate stage once called Core is gone (`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/dromos-integration-v1.md` L18;
`RimWorld-Imperium/docs/vanilla-empire-reference-index.md` L114).

Imperial technology is not invented by the colony; its colonists come to understand how the imperial system works, so
each rank has one integrated research (集成研究), opened once that rank's codicil is analysed, which opens that rank's
base content directly; specialties and special projects are unlocked by techprints, core content does not depend on
luck, the colonists still have to study, and no research is split into fragments for the sake of numbers
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L27). For rank II the base content is the imperial mechanoids with the
facilities that go with them (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L27), as [mechanoids.md](mechanoids.md) gives
them; the command node and the berths' steel store named there are removed (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L27,
R-1103-2247, R-1103-2255).

Political standing opens normal research, and each rank's research needs the colony to analyse the imperial codicil
(委任敕书) that the visitor delivers at that promotion (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L25; `docs/glossary.md`
L56). A codicil is consumed when analysed; one destroyed or lost before analysis can be issued again and is brought by
shuttle, for a fee of 20% of the market price of the base-game chip of that rank's level: 100 silver at rank II, whose
level has no base-game chip and is priced as the signal chip, 100 at rank III, 200 at rank IV and 300 at rank V; the
reissue and its fee stay, as what makes the expansion look rigorous (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L25). The
archive office (档案署) at which that passage places the reissue is removed, its functions now in the interface opened by
the button at the bottom of the screen (`.github/deprecated-terms.json` L976-1001). The grant of the codicil is in
[empire.md](empire.md).

## The techprint catalogue

The techprint catalogue stands side by side, with no chain of collecting, no gate of promotion and no sale or barter of
techprints, and after a promotion a lower-rank techprint not yet obtained can still be obtained legitimately; requests
and claims can also bring techprints as rewards (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L29). The pool of
techprints is divided only by department and rank: any successful order (委托) of a department can carry one techprint
of that department, at or below the order's rank, that the colony has not yet obtained
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L70) (replaces the allotting of techprints by the business
variant of orders, `Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L70). An order carries one with a chance of 1/3 while the
department still has techprints to give at or below its rank, the exact chance set by measurement; a techprint
obtained but not yet registered is not drawn again as a new kind; the reward is fixed before the quote is published
and is a public reward; and after three real successful contracts of a department without a new techprint, that
department's next contract surely carries a regular techprint not yet obtained, counted apart from the guarantee of
core technology and not shown to the player (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L72-77). Eligibility for
specialty techprints follows the leaning, with no second threshold, and eligibility does not guarantee a drop
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L79); how registering techprints moves the colony's leaning is in
[empire.md](empire.md).

The counts are set to the rhythm of the base game's expansions and are provisional
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L43). Each department has three regular techprints at rank I, two at rank II,
three at ranks III and IV and two at rank V; core technologies are two per department at ranks III and IV and none at
rank V, where there are other rare ways, and the departments' specialty techprints are counted apart
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L41, L44) (replaces two at rank I, `Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md`
L44). At every rank regular techprints outnumber core technologies, and each department has at least two at each rank,
since with one the order's reward would leave nothing to choose and lose the cost of choosing; the ways to techprints
grow with rank, from order rewards to request rewards and excavations, without growing complicated, so that the player
only feels that more can be done; rank IV's techprints come through campaigns, a campaign being something rank IV lets
the colony start and that can be started many times (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L45). Promoted to the
next rank, a colony has about half of the rank's techprints, and drop rates and guarantee counts are worked back from
the real number of orders at each rank and calibrated by measurement (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L52).

> [!NOTE]
> **Base game**: each tier of the base game's expansions unlocks four to six things (Biotech's mechanoids five, four,
> six and five), and Royalty has only 13 research projects that need techprints, which can be bought; the counts rest on
> this rhythm, to be checked against `Data/` (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L43).

A techprint is chosen by whether players would really pick it from a row of techprints and use it often once picked;
development cost is no objection in itself, but what needs heavy development to break the base game's limits and is
then little used is not made (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L56). A techprint of little appeal but good use
may exist, and it is judged only by whether it fits its department's design language, not by orders
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L57-58). A techprint is equipment, designed as technology the Empire uses,
not as an event trigger; the merchant's speculation is carried by orders and requests, and merchant techprints are what
makes the speculation affordable, through risk control and mass production (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md`
L59). Techprints give no production lines, least of all early, and rank I gives no heavy fixed firepower such as a
repeating ballista (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L60). Players of city building are served by the imperial
area's planning and by requests, and players of storytelling by the Empire itself, not by techprint rewards
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L61, L129). The form of techprints changes with rank: rank I is the stage of
groping, at which the Empire grants only the tactical items its own troops use; from rank II the Empire's base content
is open and authorization has weight, so regular techprints of ranks II and III are facilities and works, fixed
buildings connected to power or to the mechanoid system, and neither mechanoid variants nor shells are taken as forms
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L62).

### The three departments

The essence of the three departments is the ground of all department content: each answers "what do you win with" and
values something different (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L120). The civil administration is efficient use:
it values each thing and gets more out of the same thing, by reuse, conversion, amplification and borrowing the
terrain, and choosing to sedate or knock down rather than kill is efficient use too, turning enemies into prisoners and
new people (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L121). The merchant department is speculation and risk control:
the merchant takes chances others dare not touch and bears risk for a higher return while pressing the risk down so
that he can afford the bet, since speculation alone loses everything and risk control alone returns nothing; the
factory is the capital of its floor, mass production and lower losses being the base, speculation the chance and risk
control what makes the bet affordable, and a lopsided technology is itself a bet
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L122). Speculation lands on the map with a visible process and consequence and
is no number game of stocks or exchange rates, the factory half stands on its own, and the two halves join, speculation
betting on what the colony produces and its return feeding the production line (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md`
L122). The military department is initiative and mobility: it values time and position, striking a decisive blow at a
time and place of its choosing and making the enemy follow (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L123).

These three are the technology thinking of the whole camp, used to judge the ownership and design of techprints, core
technologies and mechanoids, while what the departments do (orders, requests, visits, people) is written from their
real institutional functions (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L124). Getting more value out of the same thing,
or only avoiding or reducing loss, belongs to the civil administration; staking a little for a large return, or
pressing down, passing on or spreading risk to afford the bet, belongs to the merchants; making a moment whose time and
place the colony decides and that opens the situation belongs to the military; and the test looks at the means, not the
result (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L125). Equipment for avoiding or reducing loss, such as saving the
wounded, repairing in battle or revival as insurance, therefore belongs to the civil administration, and merchant
techprints are single-use but efficient, or fun and handy, equipment (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L126).
The departments serve no class of player in particular: all players may use any department's content, and players who
prefer a kind pick its department first; since a techprint is not guaranteed and its value includes its share of the
prize pool, a little-used techprint dilutes the pool (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L128).

## Core technology

A core technology (核心科技) is the core content of a department, concentrating its character, strong and flavourful
for the player, able to change play greatly or to mend a problem the community has long complained of; what falls
short is not a core technology, and the automatic rebuilding pod and the guild mech station are its examples
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L135). Ranks III and IV have two core technologies per department and rank V
none; each needs only to fit its rank's place and need not be a building or a mechanoid; it enters the techprint pool
only at a leaning of 45% or more, is still drawn at random and has a guarantee, so it can clearly exceed base content
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L133; `docs/glossary.md` L81, L83).

The core technologies of rank III are, for the military, the steam mortar and the mechanical signifer; for the civil
administration, two of the automatic rebuilding pod (自动重建仓), the fixed-operating-range facility (固定操作范围设施) and the
lictor retinue (Lictor 机械扈从), the third moving into rank III's regular techprints; and for the merchants the guild mech
station (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L138-142; `docs/glossary.md` L115, L135). Each department's signature is its
essence (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L144). The merchants' second place, first the mechanical scorpio, is left
empty for now, to be set when rank III is reopened, since the scorpio moved to the mechanoids
(`Imperium-Knowledge/08_机械体设计.md` L393, L441) (replaces the scorpio as merchant core technology,
`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L142). The commercial port Emporion is deleted, rejected core candidates are not
raised again, and ceremonial weapons and armour became base content of ranks III and IV
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L146).

The steam mortar (蒸汽炮) is the military department's rank III core building
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L148; `docs/glossary.md` L91). Vegetius records a Roman legion with about 55
ballistae for direct fire and 10 onagers for indirect fire, so the ballista lineage belongs to the merchants and
indirect-fire artillery to the military; the mortar answers rank III's regular legion in field battle and the military
order of fortress assault, and the community's complaint that the base game's mortar is too inaccurate
(`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L150). Its prototype is the steam cannon (Architronito) that da Vinci recorded
and attributed to Archimedes, and its future form a steam mass projector pressurized by a multi-stage boiler, firing
in a high arc without gunpowder, in keeping with an Empire that uses no guns (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md`
L151). It follows the base game's mortar as far as it can, manned, firing indirectly and able to be taken down and
carried on expeditions, with its own fire control and a scatter clearly smaller than the base game's mortar; its
pressure rises with time, so that at half pressure it can fire at once with less range and power and at full pressure
it reaches farthest, each shot asking whether to fire now or wait (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L152). It
fires all the base game's mortar shells, the colony's own explosive warheads among them, and has no shells of its own,
"no gunpowder" meaning only its propulsion (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L153, L157). Its one new item is
the steam accumulator (蓄压罐), a pre-pressurized steam cylinder made at a workbench from steel and chemfuel, loaded as
the base game's mortar loads its reinforced barrel and replaced after a number of shots, carrying both fuel and wear,

The guild mech station (行会机械站) is the merchants' rank III core technology: leased guild mechanoids park in it and
take none of the colony's bandwidth, though the mechanitor's control range still limits them; its places are limited,
rent and term follow the lease, and destroyed mechanoids are not paid for (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md`
L160-162). Its play is when to pay for what, renting harvesters before the harvest and combat mechanoids before a large
raid; the merchants' dimension for rank II combat mechanoids is acquisition, and the station pushes it to the largest
limit, bandwidth (`Imperium-Knowledge/04_蓝图与科研授权.md` L163).
